Bottom line

  1. No standalone Chinese conversion tax on converting CNY or wiring own principal was found in official sources. Bank document requests are compliance, not tax. The actual quoted considerable tax remains unknown - get it itemised in writing (Q03). T04 T17
  2. Least-penalty route = lawful route matching the money's documented origin. For legitimate Chinese current-account income: source docs + tax evidence -> buy USD -> one own-name wire (M01). If already FX: wire only (M03). T04 T08
  3. US passport alone is not the $50,000 convenience amount. Only Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders get it for foreign individuals (T05). Do not split through others (T10). T05 T06 T10
  4. Big exposures are separate: unpaid Chinese tax; US currency gain on appreciated CNY; FBAR/Form 8938 reporting (willful FBAR max = greater of $165,353 or 50% of balance); prohibited-channel fines up to 100%. T25 T31 T32 T17
