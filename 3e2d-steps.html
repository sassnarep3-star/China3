Step-by-step playbooks

Exact sequence per route. Do not skip document steps; do not invent a purpose.

M01 - Documented current-account income - buy USD + one wire

  1. Bring passport (valid ID) to the bank (needs: Passport) T04 T01 T02
  2. Present source-of-funds materials for the income (contract, salary slips) (needs: Source materials) T04 T01 T02
  3. Present tax vouchers (payment/exemption/filing) (needs: Tax vouchers) T04 T01 T02
  4. Buy USD at the bank's execution rate; confirm amount (needs: -) T04 T01 T02
  5. Instruct one own-name international wire; pay wire fee (needs: Wire instruction) T04 T01 T02
  6. Keep exchange slip, wire confirmation, tax vouchers for US reporting (needs: All documents) T04 T01 T02

M02 - Reconvert unused CNY from own FX exchange + wire

  1. Bring passport + original exchange slip (within 24 months) (needs: Passport + slip) T09 T01 T02
  2. Reconvert unused CNY to FX at bank (needs: Original slip) T09 T01 T02
  3. Wire FX to own overseas account (needs: Wire instruction) T09 T01 T02

M03 - Already foreign currency - wire only

  1. Bring passport to bank (needs: Passport) T08 T02
  2. Instruct own-name outward remittance of FX balance (needs: Wire instruction) T08 T02
  3. Pay wire fee; keep confirmation (needs: Confirmation) T08 T02

M04 - Chinese home-sale proceeds

  1. Bring passport (needs: Passport) T15 T01 T02
  2. Present registered sale contract + title/registration docs (needs: Sale/title docs) T15 T01 T02
  3. Present tax voucher from tax office (needs: Tax voucher) T15 T01 T02
  4. Bank converts/remits up to price less taxes (needs: -) T15 T01 T02

M05 - Inheritance transfer

  1. Prepare inheritance/property/tax documents (needs: Inheritance docs) T12 T13 T01 T02
  2. Apply to decedent's last-hukou SAFE office for approval (needs: Approval) T12 T13 T01 T02
  3. Bank buys FX and remits to own account abroad (no cash) (needs: Approval + wire) T12 T13 T01 T02

M06 - Emigrant property transfer

  1. Prepare hukou cancellation/settlement/property/tax documents (needs: Hukou/property docs) T12 T13 T01 T02
  2. Apply to former-hukou SAFE office for approval (needs: Approval) T12 T13 T01 T02
  3. Bank remits once or in instalments to own account abroad (needs: Approval + wire) T12 T13 T01 T02

M07 - Annual convenience quota - $50k/yr ID-only

  1. Bring ID (green card / domestic ID as applicable) (needs: ID) T05 T06 T07
  2. Buy/remit up to USD 50,000 in the calendar year (needs: -) T05 T06 T07
  3. Above USD 50,000: add transaction materials (do not split via others) (needs: Materials) T05 T06 T07

M08 - Cross-border RMB offshore, convert there

  1. Ask bank for written cross-border RMB offer (needs: Bank offer) T24
  2. Send CNY to own offshore account (needs: -) T24
  3. Convert offshore; obtain offshore quote (needs: Quote) T24

M09 - Dividend - 20% statutory scenario

  1. Obtain profit-distribution resolution (needs: Resolution) T25 T13
  2. Complete tax filing; pay 20% (statutory scenario) (needs: Tax filing) T25 T13
  3. Remit net proceeds (needs: Wire) T25 T13

M09T - Dividend - 10% treaty-cap scenario

  1. As M09, plus treaty eligibility evidence (residence/beneficial ownership) (needs: Eligibility evidence) T27 T25
  2. Apply 10% treaty cap; pay 10% (needs: Tax filing) T27 T25
  3. Remit net proceeds (needs: Wire) T27 T25

M10 - Overseas ATM withdrawals

  1. Use mainland UnionPay card abroad (needs: Card) T19 T03
  2. Withdraw within CNY 10,000/day and CNY 100,000/year (needs: -) T19 T03
  3. Pay per-withdrawal fee (CNY 12 + 1%, max CNY 112) (needs: Fee) T19 T03

M11 - Carry lawfully purchased FX banknotes

  1. Buy FX banknotes lawfully at bank (needs: -) T20 T35
  2. Carry <=USD 5,000/trip (<=USD 10,000 with bank permit) (needs: Permit if needed) T20 T35
  3. Declare >USD 10,000 entering US (FinCEN Form 105) (needs: Declaration) T20 T35

M12 - Wise from-CNY (own account only)

  1. Verify identity + tax records in Wise (tax paid within last 3 years) (needs: Tax records) T28
  2. Fund from China bank app (max CNY 300,000/transfer) (needs: Bank app) T28
  3. Receive in own account; check in-app fee/FX quote (needs: Quote) T28

M13 - Western Union China (cash pickup)

  1. Go to WU partner bank with government ID (needs: ID) T29
  2. Fill transfer details; pay quoted fee (needs: Fee) T29
  3. Receiver picks up cash with MTCN (needs: MTCN) T29
