United States - currency gain on the conversion itself

  1. Only the gain is taxed: USD received minus USD basis of RMB sold. Wiring USD you already hold recognises no gain. T26
  2. Personal money: gain <=$200 ignored; gain >$200 reported as capital gain (Pub. 525). Personal loss generally not deductible (26 U.S.C. 165(c) - see Flagged F05 for character of savings balances). T31
  3. Investment/business money: s.988 gain/loss is ordinary (26 U.S.C. 988(a)(1)(A)); s.988(e)(1) excludes personal transactions from s.988. The two statements cover different cases - do not apply ordinary to personal money. T31
  4. Illustrative gains at IRS yearly averages are on the Costs page. Tax = gain x your marginal rate (capital vs ordinary) - rate blank until advised (Q07). T26 T31
