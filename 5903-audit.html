The short version

  • The bank cost is right. All 30 published checkpoints across six routes recompute to the cent from the board and the bank's tariff, with no shared code.
  • Two rows on the Costs page contradict themselves. They print the 11 November rate beside a per-100 figure from the 10 November board (A05, A06).
  • One tax row is wrong in a way that understates a saving. A personal currency gain over $200 is a capital gain, not ordinary income (A24, A25).
  • One sentence is too absolute. A U.S. citizen resident in China for a continuous year is a 境内个人 for FX purposes, so the blanket statement that a passport holder never gets the USD 50,000 convenience amount is not supported (A08, A35).
  • One number was missing. The 2025 IRS average is 7.129 and the Federal Reserve's is 7.1875; the dashboard showed only the first (A28).
  • Four of the five requests have since been applied by the session that owns those files: the two self-contradicting rate rows, the personal-transaction capital-gain rule, and the Federal Reserve average. One remains open — the absolute sentence about the USD 50,000 amount — and is restated below with its sources.

What this audit does not establish

  • No legal or tax conclusion for your case. The rows restate what an official page says; whether a particular balance is personal or investment money, and where you are resident, decide which rows apply.
  • No live quote. The bank's published board is a reference rate. What you are actually charged appears at the counter or in the app, and the tariff allows agreements, promotions and local schedules to differ.
  • No intermediary or receiving-bank charges. Those are not published anywhere official and stay blank on the dashboard; this audit did not fill them in.
  • No test of the dashboard's other pages. Property-transfer and inheritance documents were not re-read here (A36), and are neither confirmed nor contradicted.
  • No browser rendering test. Links and anchors were resolved by script and every external link was opened; layout was not visually inspected in this pass.
