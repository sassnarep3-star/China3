Start here — getting money out of China

Short answer. Converting RMB to USD and wiring it to your own U.S. account is not itself a taxable event in the sources read here. What costs money is (1) the bank's exchange spread and wire fee, and (2) any Chinese tax that was never paid on the income that created the balance. On the U.S. side, selling appreciated RMB can recognize a currency gain under 26 U.S.C. §988, and missed reporting (FBAR, Form 8938) carries its own penalties. Read the executive summary.
