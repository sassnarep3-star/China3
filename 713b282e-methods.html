Conversion-penalty anatomy

The "conversion penalty" is not one charge. It is four separate layers, and only the first two are bank charges:

LayerWhat it isSize at $100,000Source
1. Exchange spread The difference between the rate the bank sells USD at and the fair midpoint, paid once on conversion. $209.42 (0.2094%) S01
2. Wire + cable fee Bank of China personal cross-border wire: 0.1% of the RMB amount, min RMB 50, max RMB 260, plus RMB 150 cable. $61.33 S02
3. Intermediary / receiving-bank fees Deducted by other banks in the chain. Not published anywhere official; left blank, never assumed zero. not verified U04 S02
4. Chinese income tax on the underlying money 3–45% on wages, 20% flat on dividends, rent and property gains; bank interest is exempt. Applies to the income, not the conversion. Case-dependent; blank until the source of the money is known S25 U01
