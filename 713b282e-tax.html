Bottom line. The cheapest lawful bank cost is $53.38 on $10,000 and $899.01 on $400,000 for a documented-income conversion wired once. The largest single monetary risk is a willful FBAR penalty (up to 50% of the balance) or a Chinese 30–100% penalty on an unlawful route — both dwarf the bank cost.
