Convention, stated so it can be checked: spread = amount × (ask − (bid + ask) / 2) / ask, where bid and ask are the board's Buying and Selling rates. Wire fee = min(260, max(50, amount × ask × 0.1%)) + 150 CNY, converted at the same ask. Amounts are USD; the CNY debited is amount × ask plus the wire fee. Not included: the sending bank's cable/agent charges beyond the 150 CNY above, any intermediary or receiving-bank fee, and any U.S. bank fee. Those are not published by any official page and stay blank (dashboard flags U04). One rounding detail, so the columns can be added up: the total is rounded once from the unrounded spread and fee, so in 18 of the 40 rows the two rounded columns differ from the printed total by one cent.

What this page does not do

  • It does not give legal, tax or investment advice, and it does not decide which route applies to a particular balance. That turns on where the money came from, which the brief does not state (dashboard flag U01).
  • It does not re-price any route other than L01 and L03. The other nine routes on the cost page are priced from the same tariff and the same board, so they move with it, but they were not recomputed here.
  • It does not treat the absence of a conversion tax as a finding about any individual's tax position. No official page read in this repository taxes the conversion itself; the tax that bites is the tax on the income that created the money.
  • It does not claim that any bank will accept a particular document set. The 2020 Guidelines Art. 68 lets the bank refuse a transaction it cannot satisfy itself about.
