### Why it flattens
The wire fee caps at CNY 260 + CNY 150 = CNY 410 once the amount exceeds CNY 260,000
(≈ USD 38,850). Above that, the marginal cost of every extra dollar is exactly 0.4004 % —
about **$4.00 per $1,000**. There is no volume penalty and no volume discount anywhere in the range.

## The same money, six routes

A dash is a figure no official page publishes. It is not zero.

| USD | M1 convenience | M2 current-acct | M3 emigrant | M4 gift | M5 QDII | M6 cash |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10,000 | $72.46 | $72.46 | — | $72.46 | — | $40.04 |
| 50,000 | $261.47 | $261.47 | — | $261.47 | — | prohibited |
| 100,000 | n/a over $50k | $461.67 | — | $522.94 | — | prohibited |
| 200,000 | n/a | $862.07 | — | $1,045.87 | — | prohibited |
| 400,000 | n/a | $1,662.87 | — | $2,091.74 | — | prohibited |

- **M1** stops at USD 50,000 per person per calendar year on ID alone ([SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html)).
- **M2** is the same arithmetic without the cap, available only on genuine current-account evidence.
- **M3** needs emigrant status; SAFE approval and notarisation costs are unpublished ([PBC [2004] No. 16](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2004/1116/5461.html)).
- **M4** multiplies the wire fee by the number of senders needed to stay inside each one's convenience amount — which is exactly the split-purchase pattern that is prohibited; it is priced here to show why it is both lawful-only-as-a-genuine-gift and expensive.
- **M5** is an investment channel, not a remittance.
- **M6** is capped at USD 10,000 per person per trip ([Order 532 Art. 42](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm)).

## What is *not* in these totals

1. **PRC income tax on what created the money.** 20 % flat on property gains, interest,
   dividends, rent and incidental income; 3 %–45 % progressive on salary, mostly withheld at
   source. It is due whether or not you convert. On a USD 400,000 property gain the tax is
   roughly USD 80,000 — 48× the entire conversion cost.
2. **U.S. income tax.** Moving your own after-tax money is not an income event.
3. **U.S. information-reporting penalties.** This is the only line that can exceed the balance:
   FBAR non-willful up to USD 16,536 per report, willful the greater of USD 165,353 or
   **50 % of the account balance**; Form 8938 up to USD 60,000; Form 3520 5 %/month capped at 25 %.
4. **PRC enforcement fines.** Evasion and illegal arbitrage up to 30 % of the amount
   (30 %–100 % if serious); private FX dealing up to 30 %; illegal cash carriage up to 20 %.

## Reproduce it

```bash
python3 scripts/build_cost.py     # regenerate all seven pages from data/fx_anchor.json
python3 scripts/verify_cost.py    # re-derive all 40 × 6 cells and fail on any mismatch
```

To re-anchor on a new day: replace the six BOC numbers in `data/fx_anchor.json`, re-run both
commands, commit. No HTML is edited by hand.
