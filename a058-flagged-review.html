Flagged for review: independent audit

Reviewed 10 November 2026. These are substantive cautions about existing pages, not a personalized recommendation. The complete scoped audit is here.

1. $50,000 is not a universal ID-only conversion entitlement

The S775 dashboard and playbook say a personal wire needs only identification up to $50,000 per day. SAFE’s cited daily remittance guidance addresses domestic individuals remitting foreign currency from an account for current-account expenses; it is not a universal CNY-to-USD purchase rule. For overseas individuals purchasing foreign exchange with lawful current-account RMB income, SAFE discusses identification, source evidence including tax documentation and an allowance exception for holders of a foreign permanent-residence ID. Your classification and documents are unknown.

Official: SAFE: overseas individuals’ purchase · SAFE: domestic individuals’ remittance · SAFE: annual allowance exception.

2. “Net” is not a verified recipient amount

Some existing tables subtract a modelled midpoint spread and fees from a USD amount that was already calculated using the bank’s ask price. The spread is a comparison with a benchmark, not necessarily another deduction from dollars received. The fee debit method, intermediary and receiving charges are unknown. Actual recipient proceeds and all-in cost are unverified; ask the banks for an executable quote and fee breakdown.

Official reference: Bank of China exchange-rate board · BOC historic personal-service notice. The board is not a personalized executable quote.

3. Chinese tax outcome is unknown

Do not interpret the statement “no PRC tax on conversion” as a guarantee of no Chinese tax. The reason for any bank request has not been provided; source income, prior unpaid tax, and documentation may matter. A tax receipt requirement does not itself establish a new conversion tax.

Official: SAFE documentation guidance.

4. Existing S775 display discrepancies

At the audited main commit, the $10,000 dashboard card and its table disagree and the cost page contains invalid nested table cells. Pending PR #34 proposes a correction; it does not establish actual recipient proceeds or the user’s eligibility. The historical rate assumptions are not current quotes.

Official reference: BOC rate board.

5. U.S. reporting and tax require individual review

FBAR reporting may be required when aggregate foreign-account values exceed $10,000 at any time in a calendar year. Foreign-currency gain treatment depends on facts including the transaction and basis; a foreign tax credit is subject to limits. Unknown individual taxes and penalties must not be shown as zero.

Official: IRS FBAR guidance · 26 U.S.C. §988 · IRS foreign tax credit guidance.
