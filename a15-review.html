Flagged for review: before using the numbers

Independent spot checks performed 10 November 2026. These findings supplement, not replace, the existing review registers. They do not constitute an audit of every historical row.

Bottom line: A documented bank route is the first option to investigate, not a proven cheapest route for your situation. The bank has to accept the funds' actual origin and confirm the quote and applicable procedure. No official source establishes your account's tax due or a complete, current cost for every method.

Annual $50,000 purchase convenience amountNot automatically available to every U.S. citizen or every foreign-passport holder. SAFE Tianjin says it applies to domestic individuals and foreign individuals holding a Chinese foreign permanent resident ID. For a foreign individual's qualifying domestic current-account RMB income, it describes identity, amount-bearing proof of funds and tax documentation. Classification and route still require bank confirmation.SAFE Tianjin 2024; SAFE Tianjin 2025
Tax certificate ≠ a new transfer taxSAFE Shanghai describes tax documents among the evidence for certain foreign-individual remittances. That does not establish either a tax owed or zero tax on this specific account. Salary, sale proceeds and other origins may have different treatment; consult a qualified China tax adviser.SAFE Shanghai
U.S. foreign-exchange gainDo not assume that converting CNY is never taxable. If CNY is nonfunctional currency, §988 generally treats disposition as a transaction and foreign-currency gain or loss separately; the personal-transaction rules and exceptions are fact-specific. The account's acquisition dates and U.S.-dollar tax basis are unknown, so the actual tax is unverified.26 U.S.C. §988 (2023 edition)
Historical BOC cost figures and OUR optionBOC's personal e-banking tariff lists 0.1% with ¥50 minimum/¥260 maximum plus ¥150 telecom fee for non-Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan transfers, subject to local or agreement differences. The separate BOC trade-finance notice includes a $15 or $25 full-amount charge for specified ordinary-wire circumstances. It does not establish that this charge and the personal tariff can be combined for this user. Figures on the cost dashboards that add a $25 OUR fee to a personal tariff need a bank quote before use; neither proves the full cost or cheapest option.BOC personal tariff; BOC trade-finance notice
FBAR trigger is strictly greater than $10,000FinCEN says a U.S. person must file when the aggregate foreign financial accounts exceed $10,000 at any time in the calendar year, subject to applicable rules. A $10,000 transfer is not itself the test. Check Form 8938 separately.FinCEN FBAR; IRS comparison
2025 vs 2026 foreign earned income exclusionThe prior-year $130,000 maximum is explicitly for 2025; the 2026 maximum is $132,900 for qualifying individuals, potentially reduced for a partial year. It applies to eligible earned income, not the whole account balance. A foreign tax credit cannot also be taken for taxes on excluded income.IRS exclusion guidance; IRS foreign tax credit
Cash is not a substitute for legal eligibilityCBP says physical currency or monetary instruments over $10,000 transported into or out of the U.S. must be reported, with family/group totals aggregated. China exit rules and bank cash withdrawal rules are separate. A U.S. declaration does not itself authorize the China-side transaction.U.S. CBP; SAFE Xiamen guide
Quota splitting / informal conversionUsing others' quotas to evade controls is not a legitimate tax strategy. SAFE's guide prohibits borrowing quotas to split purchases. Authorized agency for another person is a different question and does not permit quota evasion.SAFE Xiamen guide

Practical next steps

  1. Separate the account balance by original source and ownership; obtain original records, income and tax documents, and identity/residency records. Confirm the actual legal transaction category with the holding bank.
  2. For qualifying current-account income, request the bank's current written list of evidence for converting and remitting to your own U.S. account. Do not assume a salary checklist applies to property, inherited assets, corporate funds, or mixed balances.
  3. Get written quotes covering the exact customer rate and time, RMB debited, USD sent, bank and correspondent fees, receiving fees, and expected net USD. Compare eligible providers on the same date and terms.
  4. Have a U.S.–China cross-border tax professional check the original income, any eligible foreign tax credit or exclusion, currency-gain basis, FBAR and Form 8938. If filings are late, get advice before selecting a corrective procedure.
  5. Keep bank confirmations and tax filings. Avoid using another person's allowance, false purpose statements or unlicensed channels.
