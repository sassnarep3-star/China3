Practical next steps

  1. Identify the owner and legal source of the funds, and obtain records showing the amount and applicable tax treatment.
  2. Ask the Chinese bank which licensed conversion and outward-remittance route applies, what evidence it requires, and for a written live quote including all known fees. Do not use third-party quotas or split transactions to evade controls.
  3. Have a US cross-border tax professional review currency basis, FBAR, Form 8938 and any prior-year issues before converting.
