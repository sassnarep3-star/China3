Exactly how each lawful route runs, from the branch counter to your U.S. return.

Each step names the document that decides it. Where a step depends on a fact nobody has published — a branch's cash rate, an intermediary's deduction, SAFE's processing time — the step says so instead of inventing it.

M01 · One documented bank wire — the default recommendation

  1. Decide what the money is. Salary, pension, sale of property, an inheritance, or savings. The category, not the amount, decides which documents the bank will demand. V04
  2. Collect the paper. Passport; the mainland account; for current-account income the source documents (contract, payroll record, tax payment certificate); for property the title and transfer documents. V04
  3. Ask for the rate in writing. The board prints 6.6851 as the selling rate at 2026-11-10 17:39:58, but the tariff itself says the branch's execution rate governs. V01 V02 Compare that quote with the mid of 6.6711 — the gap is your real spread.
  4. Convert at the counter or in e-banking. You need CNY 66,851.00 for USD 10,000 and CNY 2,674,040.00 for USD 400,000 at today's ask. V01
  5. Accept the fee. 1‰ of the CNY amount, floor CNY 50, cap CNY 260, plus the CNY 150 cable charge: USD 32.44 at USD 10,000 and USD 61.33 at USD 400,000. V02
  6. Send one transfer if you can. Above USD 50,000 equivalent a genuine current-account payment is allowed on documents; the USD 50,000 figure is the simplified route, not a ceiling. V04
  7. Keep the receipt and the rate. It fixes your USD proceeds and, with the original acquisition record, your § 988 basis. V15 V16
  8. File the U.S. side. FBAR if the aggregate of your foreign accounts exceeded $10,000.00 at any time V12; Form 8938 if the threshold applies V12; the § 988 gain on the return V15.

M02 · Full-amount-received (OUR)

  1. Ask the branch whether it offers 全额到账 on a personal outward payment. The tariff is published for personal business; whether your branch applies it is a branch fact. V03
  2. Confirm the payment actually needs an intermediary. The published service applies only where the remitting and receiving banks have no direct account relationship. V03
  3. Price it: USD 15 if the receiving bank holds an account at BOC New York, otherwise USD 25, on top of the wire fee and cable. V03
  4. Total at USD 400,000: USD 924.01. V01 V02 V03

M03 · One transfer per USD 50,000 convenience amount

  1. Confirm you qualify for the simplified procedure — the bank classifies you first, and a foreign passport holder is not automatically a 境内个人. V04
  2. Each calendar year, convert and remit up to USD 50,000 equivalent per person. V04
  3. USD 400,000 therefore takes 8 years and 8 wire fees: USD 1,328.33 of cost in total, against USD 899.01 for one transfer. V01 V02
  4. Do not split one payment across relatives or invent purposes to stay under the figure: that is expressly penalised. V05 V06

M04 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)

  1. Establish that you owned the property before you emigrated. V07
  2. Cancel your PRC household registration and obtain the certificate; complete the PRC tax clearance before the cancellation. V11 V07
  3. Apply to the SAFE branch covering your original registration location — the approval moved there. V07
  4. No RMB 500,000 filing step is required; that threshold was cancelled. V07
  5. Once approved, remit once or in instalments inside the approved amount. V07
  6. Pay the same bank spread and wire fee as M01. SAFE's own approval fee is not published — blank, flagged F09.

M06 · Cash — why it does not scale

  1. Up to USD 5,000 equivalent, customs releases the currency. V09
  2. USD 5,000–10,000 needs a bank-issued 携带外汇出境许可证. V09
  3. Above USD 10,000 needs a SAFE permit and is in principle not allowed. V09
  4. On the U.S. side, more than USD 10,000 at one time must be reported on FinCEN Form 105, and the threshold applies to a family travelling together collectively. V14
  5. USD 400,000 of cash would require dozens of trips and would breach at least one of the two regimes. Not a route.

What happens after the money lands

  1. FBAR: aggregate foreign accounts above $10,000.00 at any time in the year, filed with FinCEN, due 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October. V12
  2. Form 8938: thresholds depend on filing status and residence; the comparison page states them per category. V12
  3. § 988: report the currency gain as ordinary income, unless it is a personal transaction with gain of USD 200 or less. V15 V16
  4. Keep the acquisition records. Without the rate at which you acquired the CNY, nobody — including you — can compute the § 988 gain, which is why this page prices it as a range rather than a number.
