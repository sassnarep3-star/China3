Every way to turn CNY into USD

Seventeen routes: thirteen lawful (one of them "do nothing"), four prohibited. Each carries the rule that governs it, the documents it needs, its ceiling, its pros and cons, and a source link. Nothing here is a recommendation for your facts; four of them depend on eligibility you may not have.

Read this before the table. The USD 50,000 annual convenience amount is not a cap on how much may leave China. SAFE states that for genuine current-account and opened capital-account business there is no amount limit, and that above USD 50,000 the transaction simply cannot be done on an ID document alone C05. It is also not generally available to a foreign national: it belongs to domestic individuals and to holders of the PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID C04.

Why four routes are marked prohibited

  • Splitting across other people's quotas — evasion of FX control: up to 30% of the amount, 30–100% in serious cases C06.
  • Private exchange, money changers, underground banks — warning, confiscation of gains, up to 30%, plus criminal liability C06.
  • Cryptocurrency or stablecoin — the PRC designates it an illegal financial activity and strictly prohibits it; the 2026 notice replaced the 2021 one and kept the prohibition C10.
  • False invoices or purpose — the conversion is reversed and a fine of up to 30% applies C06.
