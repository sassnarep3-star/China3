The tax on the conversion itself

Two questions decide this: is there a tax on converting at all (answer: none found), and is there a U.S. tax on the change in the exchange rate between when you got the RMB and when you sell it (answer: yes, and it is computed below for every amount and every acquisition year).

No PRC tax on the conversion

No official source read in this pass imposes a tax or a fee on converting RMB to USD or on remitting it. The State Council regulation says current international payments and transfers are not restricted C06. What the bank collects is a price — spread and tariff — not a tax. The tax that exists is on the income behind the money, and on the U.S. side on the currency gain.

The one genuinely open question: capital or ordinary

26 U.S.C. 988(a)(1)(A) makes a foreign-currency gain ordinary income. But 988(e)(1) says the section does not apply to an individual's personal transaction, and IRS Publication 525 then says a personal gain above USD 200 is reported as a capital gain C11 C12. The two point to different rates. On USD 400,000 acquired in 2024 the difference is $4,188.90 at 15% against $10,332.62 at 37% — about USD 6,144. This edition prices the capital reading because that is what the IRS publication says, and states the ordinary figure beside it rather than choosing silently. F07

The gain by the year the RMB was acquired

Gain = USD received − USD basis, where the basis is set by the exchange rate on the day the RMB was acquired C11, using the IRS yearly average for that year C15. Today's selling rate is 6.6871 CNY per USD C01. A negative number is a loss.

Which basis method may be used

The regulation permits any reasonable method applied consistently from year to year to all of a taxpayer's foreign-currency accounts — first in first out, last in first out, or pro rata — but a method that consistently pulls out the highest-basis units first is expressly not reasonable C11. Moving RMB between your own RMB deposits is not itself a taxable event. Choosing LIFO or FIFO can change the gain materially when the rate has moved a lot between deposits, so the method is a decision, not an afterthought. It is recorded as Q05.
