The three that change the answer most

  1. F01 — your facts. No balance, residency status, cost basis or filing history was supplied. Every numeric answer here is therefore conditional on assumptions stated on the page where it appears.
  2. F08 — two definitions of 境内个人. Order 532 art. 52(2) includes a foreigner continuously resident for one year. The current-account guidance in S04 treats foreign individuals separately and reserves the convenience amount for PR-ID holders. Which one your branch applies changes everything about the documents you need.
  3. F03 — cost basis. The U.S. tax line flips between a 7% gain and a 3.6% loss depending on when the CNY was acquired. This is the largest single number in the whole analysis and it is the one thing only you can supply.
