1. What changed for the reader, in one paragraph

The single most important correction is about documents, not money. The site previously said a personal wire out of China needs no approval "up to US$50,000/day on ID documents". Re-read at source, that threshold is 汇发〔2020〕14号 art. 64 and it applies to 境内个人 (domestic individuals) remitting FX from an FX account — and only US$10,000/day when remitting foreign-currency cash. A U.S. citizen is a 境外个人 (foreign individual), and for them art. 59 governs: to buy FX out of lawful current-account CNY income earned in China the bank must see a valid ID plus source-of-funds documents showing the transaction amount, including tax certificates (含税务凭证). The annual purchase convenience quota is available only to foreign individuals who hold a 外国人永久居留身份证 (Foreign Permanent Resident ID card). Practically: the cost model was already right, but the paperwork bar for this user is higher than the site implied, and that is now stated everywhere the threshold appears. SAFE art. 64 SAFE art. 59
