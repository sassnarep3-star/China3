Formulas, identical to the canonical ladder: CNY needed = amount × ask · spread = amount × (ask − mid)/ask · wire fee = (clamp(1‰ × CNY needed, ¥50, ¥260) + ¥150) ÷ ask · OUR = US$25 per wire where an intermediary bank is required. Sources: BOC FX board BOC personal wire tariff BOC personal OUR notice

Finding. The estimate is stable across the day's boards: the total moves by -0.04 at $10,000 (-0.07% of the cost) and -2.29 at $400,000 (-0.25% of the cost). Planning accuracy is therefore dominated by the wire-fee cap and by the unverified intermediary and U.S. receiving-bank fees — not by intraday rate movement.
