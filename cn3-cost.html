How each column is computed

  • CNY at ask = amount × 6.6903 (the bank sells USD to you at 现汇卖出价, not the mid).
  • FX spread = amount × 0.210447%, where 0.210447% = (6.6903 − 6.67625) ÷ 6.67625.
  • Wire fee = 0.1% of the CNY amount, minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260 per transfer, converted at 6.6903.
  • Cable = CNY 150 per transfer to a non-HK/Macau/Taiwan destination.
  • Known cost = spread + wire/ATM fee + cable. It excludes every blank input.
  • U.S. tax* = 24% × amount × (1 − 6.6903 ÷ 7.189). The 7.189 is the IRS 2024 yearly average; the 24% is an assumption.
  • All-in = known cost + U.S. tax*. It does not include your receiving bank's fee, which is blank.
