What would close the high-severity items

  • A bank statement showing the account type and the balance history.
  • The acquisition dates and rates for the CNY (or the records that establish them).
  • A written classification from the branch: 境内个人 or 境外个人, and the document list it will accept.
  • Your U.S. bank's incoming-wire fee schedule.
  • Confirmation of whether any FBAR or Form 8938 was filed for prior years.

With those five, every high-severity flag closes and the cost estimates collapse to a single number per route. Without them, the ranges on this site are the honest answer.
