Exactly what to do, in order

Each playbook is the shortest lawful sequence for that route. The order matters: confirm classification and documents before converting, because a conversion is hard to undo.

Convert documented current-account income and wire USD to your U.S. account

Lawful when the funds and documents qualify · The default recommendation if you can document where the money came from.

#ActionWhat to doKeep this documentSource
1Confirm how the bank classifies youAsk the branch, in writing, whether your account is 境内个人 or 境外个人. The FX Regulations define 境内个人 as a Chinese citizen or a foreigner who has resided in China continuously for one year.Passport + visa/residence permitFX Regulations (State Council 532)
2Ask for the branch's document checklistDo not convert first and argue later. Request the written list of documents the branch will accept as proof of the source of funds (salary slips, tax records, sale contract, pension statements).Written checklistSAFE salary-conversion Q&A
3Confirm no tax-recordal form is missingFor outward payments of USD 50,000 equivalent or more in a single payment, a PRC tax-recordal form may be required before the bank remits. Ask whether it applies to your item.税务备案表 (if required)Tax recordal for outward payments
4Get the all-in quote in writingAsk for the customer rate, the timestamp, the wire fee, the cable fee, and the statement that overseas bank charges are deducted at cost. Compare with the board rate on the same day.Written quoteBOC FX board
5Convert and remit in one instructionThe bank sells you USD at 现汇卖出价 and wires it. Expect the CNY wire fee (0.1%, min 50, max 260) plus the cable fee of CNY 150 to a non-HK destination.Remittance receiptBOC personal transfer tariff
6Record the rate, because the IRS will need itKeep the bank's conversion advice. The USD basis of the CNY you disposed of, and today's proceeds, determine the IRC §988 gain on your U.S. return.Conversion adviceIRC §988
7File the U.S. side on timeIf the aggregate of your foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any point in the year, file FinCEN Form 114. Check Form 8938 thresholds separately. Neither is a tax; both carry penalties.FinCEN Form 114, Form 8938IRS 8938 vs FBAR

Use the USD 50,000 annual convenience amount, year after year

Lawful for 境内个人; not a way to move USD 400,000 quickly · Fallback only: lawful, but slow and not cheaper than W1.

#ActionWhat to doKeep this documentSource
1Count the years firstUSD 400,000 at USD 50,000 a year is eight calendar years. Confirm you accept that timeline before starting.—SAFE convenience amount Q&A
2Convert one slice per calendar yearUse the convenience amount with your ID. Each slice is a separate wire with its own CNY 260 wire fee and CNY 150 cable fee once the amount exceeds about USD 38,864 in CNY terms.ID, annual conversion recordSAFE convenience amount Q&A
3Do not borrow anyone else's quotaUsing a relative's convenience amount is the exact conduct that lands you on the watch list for the year plus two more, and can attract a 逃汇 fine.—SAFE watch-list Q&A

Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) after SAFE approval

Lawful when approved and documented · Best route if — and only if — you have emigrated and cancelled your hukou.

#ActionWhat to doKeep this documentSource
1Confirm you have actually emigratedThis route needs a cancelled mainland hukou (户籍注销证明) and a certificate of settlement abroad issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.户籍注销证明, 定居证明PBC [2004] No.16
2Settle PRC tax before cancelling the hukouThe IIT Law requires a tax clearance before a hukou cancellation for emigration. Get the 税收证明 or 完税凭证 — the bank will ask for it.税收证明/完税凭证PRC Individual Income Tax Law
3Apply at the FX bureau of your former hukouHuifa [2014] No. 2 moved approval to the local branch and removed the head-office filing above RMB 500,000.Application + property documentsHuifa [2014] No.2 (capital account)
4Remit once or in several partsThe requirement to split the payout over two years was repealed in 2014. The bank may pay the approved amount in one or several remittances.Approval certificateHuifa [2014] No.2 (capital account)
5Expect the money to go only to your own account abroadThe 2004 rules require the bank to convert and wire directly to your own account in your country of residence; cash payout inside China is not allowed.Remittance receiptPBC [2004] No.16

Withdraw cash abroad on a Chinese UnionPay debit card

Lawful but capped far below USD 400,000 · Expensive and slow; not a route for a large balance.

#ActionWhat to doKeep this documentSource
1Check the annual ceilingAll your Chinese cards together are capped at RMB 100,000 equivalent of overseas cash per calendar year, and RMB 10,000 per card per day.—SAFE ATM cash notice
2Withdraw in full-size slicesThe BOC fee is CNY 12 + 1% capped at CNY 112 per withdrawal. A RMB 10,000 withdrawal already hits the cap, so smaller withdrawals cost proportionally more.ATM receiptsBOC ATM tariff (2026-11-11)
3Expect a second, unpublished costThe card network's conversion rate is not published by the bank. Treat the published 1.12% as a floor, not the whole cost.—BOC ATM tariff (2026-11-11)

Buy USD cash and carry it out by hand

Lawful only within the cash-control rules · Cheap per dollar, impractical per year, and the most heavily policed route.

#ActionWhat to doKeep this documentSource
1Buy USD cash at the bankThe board shows the same 现钞卖出价 as the 现汇卖出价 today, so the spread is the same 0.21%.Bank receiptBOC FX board
2Get the 携带证 above USD 5,000USD 5,000 or less needs no permit; USD 5,000–10,000 needs a 携带证 from a designated bank; above USD 10,000 is not permitted in principle.携带外汇出境许可证Cash-carry rule
3Report on the U.S. sideMore than USD 10,000 in cash must be reported to CBP on FinCEN Form 105. Failure can mean seizure.FinCEN Form 105CBP currency reporting

The order never changes

  1. Classify the money and yourself at the bank, in writing.
  2. Get the document list and the tax-recordal answer.
  3. Get the all-in quote with a timestamp.
  4. Convert and remit in one instruction.
  5. Keep the conversion advice — it is your §988 evidence.
  6. File the U.S. information returns on time.
