Category 02 · The routes

Every method, with its real cost and its real ceiling

Fifteen routes. Nine are lawful, six are prohibited — the prohibited ones are listed because a serious answer has to say why each is worse than it looks, not to recommend them. The two columns that matter are ceiling and cost at USD 400,000.

M01
lawful		 Bank purchase + outward wire within the convenience amount
Up to USD 50,000-equivalent per person per calendar year on ID plus a signed purchase declaration.

lawful		 Bank purchase above the convenience amount on genuine current-account evidence
No amount limit; the bank reviews the transaction documents. Above USD 50,000 per payment a tax filing form is also required.
 境内个人 with a real current-account purpose (study, medical, travel, family support, services)no ceiling No ceiling; the whole balance can move in one instruction if the purpose is real.“Accumulated savings” is not a current-account purpose. The evidence must exist before the money moves, and it must match the remittance. 0.4004% spread + CNY 410 (capped) V03 V08 V24
M03
lawful · approval		 Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)
Apply once to the local SAFE branch with ID, household-registration cancellation, settlement proof, source-of-funds and property-rights evidence and a tax certificate. At most RMB 500,000 is decided locally, above that by SAFE headquarters.
 Someone who has already emigrated and holds foreign citizenship or permanent residence; or an inheritanceno ceiling The only general capital-account exit for a whole personal balance. PBC [2004] No. 16 sets the schedule, not a bank's discretion.Only for emigrants and heirs. Phased: half first, half the remainder after one year, the rest after two. Proceeds must go to your own overseas account, never as cash. 0.4004% spread + CNY 410 per remittance tranche; approval fee and notarisation not published V05 V01
M04
lawful		 Current-account income of a foreign national (境外个人)
Art. 13 of PBC Order [2006] No. 3 and Art. 59 of 汇发〔2020〕14号: buy FX and remit against ID plus source-of-funds evidence including tax vouchers.
 A non-Chinese passport holder with lawful current-account RMB income earned in China— No annual convenience amount applies (that applies to holders of a foreigner permanent-residence ID), and the review is of where the money came from, not what you will spend it on.Only covers current-account income. It is not a route for capital or for inherited property. 0.4004% spread + CNY 410 per remittance; above USD 50,000 per payment a tax filing form applies V24 V23 V08
M05
lawful		 Return of unused inward remittance, or unused RMB from a prior exchange
Art. 14 unused inward FX may be returned by the original route on ID. Art. 15 unused RMB from a prior exchange may be converted back against the original exchange memo, small amounts on ID alone.
 境外个人 only— No tax certificate, no quota.Only reverses money that already came in. It cannot convert savings that were earned in China. 0.4004% spread; wire fee as M01 V24
M06
lawful		 Sale proceeds of a China property owned by a foreign national
Art. 22 of PBC Order [2006] No. 3: sale proceeds in RMB may be purchased and remitted with SAFE approval. Art. 26: other lawful property of a 境外个人 follows the property-transfer rules.
 境外个人 who bought a China home for own use and sold it— A defined capital-account exit with a named legal basis.Narrow: own-use residential property only, SAFE approval, title and tax evidence, and the 20% property-transfer tax is settled on the gain first. 0.4004% spread; 20% IIT on the gain; approval fee not published V24 V09
M07
lawful		 Foreign-currency account balance, remitted as FX
Art. 64 of 汇发〔2020〕14号: up to USD 50,000-equivalent per day on ID alone; above that, ID plus transaction evidence. Cash held: USD 10,000-equivalent per day on ID alone.
 Anyone already holding USD/EUR in a China FX account— Skips the conversion entirely — no 0.4004%, because the FX is already there.Only works for money that is already foreign currency. Requires that the original purchase was lawful and documented. No spread. Wire fee only: CNY 410 for the largest amounts. V04 V01
M08
lawful		 Company dividend / profit remittance (if the money is a company's, not yours)
Current-account item; the bank processes genuine, compliant profit distributions against board resolution, audited accounts and a tax filing form above USD 50,000.
 A foreign-invested enterprise or domestic institutionno ceiling No amount limit.Personal savings cannot be relabelled as corporate profit. Doing so is a documentation offence. 0.4004% spread + CNY 410; 10% withholding on dividends where the treaty does not reduce it (not verified this session) V01 V08
M09
lawful		 Cross-border RMB settlement, converted offshore
RMB crosses the border as RMB; the conversion happens in the offshore CNH market.
 Holders of lawful current-account RMB income— Avoids the onshore 现汇卖出价.Still needs a lawful cross-border RMB channel and an offshore account. The CNH–CNY gap is a market price, not a discount, and it moves both ways. CNH spread, not measured in this session V01
M10
lawful		 Gift from a relative who is in China
The relative uses their own quota or their own documentary route; you receive a foreign gift.
 You, receiving a gift from a PRC-resident relative— A gift is outside U.S. gross income, and above USD 100,000 it is only an information return, not a tax.Must be a real gift. Paying someone to use their quota is borrowing a quota — a 关注名单 offence in China and, at scale, a structuring problem in the U.S. Same bank arithmetic as M01 on the sending side V16 V17 V25
M11
prohibited		 Splitting the transfer across several people, or borrowing quotas
Prohibited. Lending a quota draws a written warning, then the 关注名单; borrowing one puts you on it directly.
 —— None.The 关注名单 lasts the listing year plus two more, and during it every single transaction needs full documentary review. Sixteen relatives for USD 400,000 is exactly the pattern being watched for. Up to 30% of the amount under Art. 40, plus loss of the convenience amount for three years V25 V01 V26
M12
prohibited		 Underground banks, FX brokers, “friends of friends”, social-media exchangers
Prohibited. Only licensed banks and authorised exchange institutions may deal in personal FX.
 —— None.No legal recourse if it fails, and the account that receives the money becomes part of an anti-money-laundering file. Warning, confiscation of gains, up to 30% of the amount; criminal liability for illegal business operation V01 V02
M13
prohibited		 Crypto, USDT and stablecoin conversion
Prohibited. Converting legal tender to virtual currency is illegal financial activity in China.
 —— None.Loss falls on the participant, and the on/off-ramp is exactly what the monitoring is built to catch. Criminal exposure; civil transactions may be void V01
M14
prohibited		 False invoices, false purpose declarations, inflated or deflated trade
Prohibited. The transaction basis must be real, and the declared purchase purpose must match the remittance.
 —— None.Banks cross-check tax filings, customs data and the purchase declaration. Art. 40 illegal arbitrage: up to 30%; Art. 44 change of use: up to 30% V01 V26
M15
prohibited		 Carrying more than USD 10,000 in cash out in person
Above USD 10,000-equivalent per person per trip is prohibited in principle; USD 5,000–10,000 needs a carrying certificate.
 —— None at the amounts in this project.Physical loss and theft risk, plus customs detection. USD 400,000 would need 40+ trips. Art. 42: warning plus up to 20% of the amount V01 V24
The honest ranking.
1. If you have emigrated or inherited: M03, because it is the only route designed for a whole balance.
2. If you are a foreign national with China-earned income: M04, because there is no ceiling and the review is of source, not purpose.
3. If the money is a genuine current-account payment: M02, which is M01's arithmetic without M01's cap.
4. If you are a Chinese resident moving your own savings with no qualifying transaction: there is no clean over-the-counter route, and that gap — not a tax — is the real problem. It is answered by M03 or not at all.
