What the columns mean. M1 is the ID-only route and stops at USD 50,000 per person per calendar year (V03). M2 is the same bank purchase above that amount, which is lawful only with genuine transaction evidence — it is not a quota upgrade (V03, V24). M3 is the emigrant/inheritance capital-account exit and needs emigrant status (V05); its approval and notarisation costs are not published, so no total is shown. M4 is a relative in China gifting you the money; the gift is outside U.S. gross income (V16) and only an information return above USD 100,000 (V17). M5 is an investment channel, not a remittance; no official cost per transaction exists. M6 is legally capped at USD 10,000 per person per trip (V01 Art. 42).
