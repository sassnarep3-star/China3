Category 05 · What is not proven

Flagged for review

Ten items this research could not verify, one item already fixed during this session, and three defects found in the pages that were here before. Nothing on this page blocks the site: each item is either blank in the tables or explicitly labelled. This is the list to work through with a bank, a notary and a practitioner.

Rule applied throughout. If a value could not be read off an official page during this session, the cell is blank. It is never filled with a plausible number, an average, or a figure from a secondary source.

A · Verification gaps in this layer

A1High Two in-force instruments define 境内个人 differently, and the difference decides your route国务院令第532号 Art. 52(2) defines 境内个人 as a Chinese citizen or a foreign national who has continuously resided in China for one full year. PBC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 37(2) defines 境外个人 as a passport-holding foreign national. A U.S. citizen living in China therefore falls inside both definitions on the face of the two texts.If 532 §52(2) governs, a U.S. citizen who lives in China is a 境内个人 and the convenance-amount / capital-account machinery applies. If Order 3 §37(2) governs, Art. 13 / Art. 59 current-account conversion applies with no ceiling. The answer changes which of M02, M04 or M03 you use.Ask the bank to state, in writing, which 主体类别 they will record for the account and which article they rely on. This is the single highest-value question in the project. V01 V24 V23
A2High Correspondent, intermediary and U.S. receiving-bank fees: no official figure anywhereThe BOC tariff page publishes no correspondent fee, no FCC/全额到账 charge and no receiving-bank charge. No U.S. bank publishes a single generic incoming-wire price in a form that could be cited as an official rate card for this purpose.Every total on this site is a floor. The missing component is typically a flat charge, so its effect shrinks as a percentage as the amount grows — but the absolute gap is unknown.Ask the sending branch for the correspondent route and its charge, and the receiving bank for its incoming-wire fee, before relying on any total here. Notarisation and certified translation are private-market costs with no official price.M03 has no total on this site, only a partial one made of the spread and the wire fee.Obtain the fee schedule from the local SAFE branch and from a notary in the jurisdiction where the documents are executed. A5Medium The BOC tariff page carries a 2014 effective dateThe published personal cross-border telegraphic-transfer tariff on boc.cn is labelled “收费标准 2014年11月1日起”. It may still be current, but the page itself does not state that it has been re-confirmed since 2014, and it warns that branches may publish different prices.The CNY 50/260/150 figures could be stale by an unknown amount. Because they are capped and small, even a doubling changes the USD 400,000 total by about USD 61.Ask the branch for its current printed tariff and compare. Low financial risk, so it does not block anything. V12
A6Medium The USD 50,000 tax-filing trigger has been partly superseded公告2013年第40号 carries a note that 公告2021年第19号 repealed its Articles 1(2), 2(2), 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and Annex 2 with effect from 2021-06-29. The USD 50,000 filing threshold sits in Article 1(1), which the note does not list as repealed, but the surrounding machinery has changed.The threshold as quoted is probably still live; the procedural detail around it (who files, in what form, electronically) may not be.Confirm the current threshold against 公告2021年第19号 before relying on the exact figure at the boundary. V08
A7Medium No official figure establishes the PRC inheritance-tax positionThis session found no official page imposing an inheritance tax, and equally no official page stating that inheritances are exempt. An absence of a charging provision is not the same as a published zero rate.The inheritance row in the tax table is blank rather than “0%”.Confirm with the tax authority that handled the estate. Do not treat the blank as zero. V09
A8Medium The personal-transaction exception to the currency-gain rule was not verified26 CFR 1.988-1 is cited here only to identify the provision. The commonly cited personal-transaction exception (s.988(e)) was not fetched and is not asserted anywhere on this site.No conclusion on U.S. tax on FX movements between the purchase and the remittance is offered.Read s.988(e) and the current Form 8949 instructions before claiming any de-minimis exclusion. A9Low Everything is priced off a single bank and a single minuteThe 0.4004% is Bank of China, 09:15:45 on 2026-11-09. Other banks quote differently, the rate moves intraday, and the page's own disclaimer says the counter rate governs.The spread column can move by a few hundredths of a percent between banks and during the day. The structure (linear, capped fee) does not change.Replace V10 with the actual quote at the moment of transacting; the model is designed to be re-anchored, not memorised. V10

Any single figure quoted to you as “your cost” would be fabricated.Take the sequence on the playbook page to a cross-border practitioner together with the account's actual history. V01 V14

B · Defects in the pages that were already published

Recorded here rather than edited, because those files belong to other sessions. None of them changes a number in this layer.

#DefectWhereWhy it mattersSource
D1Earlier pages assert the USD 50,000 figure is “not a cap” without the capital-account counterweightindex.html: “China's USD 50,000 annual individual convenience amount is a simplified processing threshold, not a blanket annual legal cap.” That is correctly sourced to SAFE Ningxia. But 国务院令第532号 Art. 17 requires SAFE registration for individual outbound investment and Art. 22 requires approval for capital-account payments, and Art. 52(2) can classify a long-resident foreign national as 境内个人.Read alone, the earlier sentence invites the conclusion that any balance can be moved by producing evidence. Evidence satisfies the current-account test; it does not open a capital account.V03 V01
D2The salary-repatriation route is labelled “secondary source” when it is primaryindex.html and methods.html describe the foreign-national salary route as “flagging — secondary source”.PBC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 13 is a primary instrument, and SAFE Tianjin quotes 汇发〔2020〕14号 Art. 59 verbatim. The route should carry a primary citation, not a caveat.V24 V23
D3Four layers of pages now cover the same findings in four formatsEditorial pages (routes/methods/tax/…), the generated docs/ dashboard, the deep-* pages, and now this layer.This is a maintenance risk rather than an error: the same proposition can drift out of sync in four places. This session resolves it by making this layer the only one that carries the price table and the execution steps, and by linking rather than restating.V10
