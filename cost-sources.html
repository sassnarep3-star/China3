V011 · Primary statute / regulation State Council of the PRC
中华人民共和国外汇管理条例 (Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations), State Council Order No. 532
 Art. 5 no restriction on current international payments; Art. 12 genuine-transaction test; Art. 17 individual outbound investment needs registration; Art. 22 capital-account payments need approval where prescribed; Art. 39/40/41/42/44/45 fines; Art. 52(2) defines 境内个人 as a person resident in China for 1 continuous year.Does not name any tax or charge on converting RMB to USD. Does not set the USD 50,000 figure. open ↗
V021 · Primary statute / regulation State Council of the PRC
中华人民共和国外汇管理条例, Chapter VII legal liability (same instrument, cited for the penalty articles)
 Art. 39 evasion: fine up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100%-equivalent if serious. Art. 40 illegal arbitrage: same range. Art. 42 illegal carriage of cash: up to 20%. Art. 45 private/illegal FX dealing: up to 30%.The articles state maxima. They do not state how a decision-maker will exercise discretion in a particular case. open ↗
V032 · Official guidance SAFE, Ningxia branch
2022年外汇业务政策释疑（八）— the USD 50,000 figure is not a cap
 Verbatim: China imposes no amount limit on genuine, lawful current-account and already-open capital-account FX purchases/sales; the USD 50,000 is a 年度便利化额度 (simplified-processing amount) and is 'not the limit'; above it, Art. 56/57 of 汇发〔2020〕14号 allow processing against source/use evidence.Does not say ordinary accumulated savings qualify. Does not make an unopened capital-account transfer lawful. open ↗
V042 · Official guidance SAFE, Tianjin branch
2024年个人外汇（三）— 经常项目外汇业务指引 Art. 64, outward remittance
 Verbatim, Art. 64 of 汇发〔2020〕14号: remitting foreign currency already held in a foreign-currency account of at most USD 50,000-equivalent per day is done on ID alone; above that, ID plus transaction evidence, and the bank checks that the purchase purpose matches the remittance purpose. Cash already held: at most USD 10,000-equivalent per day on ID alone.Concerns foreign currency already in the account. Does not authorise buying FX, and does not create a right to remit capital. open ↗
V051 · Primary statute / regulation People's Bank of China
个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法, PBC Announcement [2004] No. 16 (full text)
 The only general capital-account exit for an individual: 移民转移 (emigrant property transfer) and 继承转移 (inheritance transfer). Art. 6 phasing: first tranche at most one half, half the remainder after one year, the balance after two years; RMB 200,000 or less may go in one remittance. Art. 10: RMB 500,000 or less decided by the local SAFE branch, above that by SAFE headquarters. Art. 11: proceeds must go straight to the applicant's own overseas account, no domestic cash withdrawal. Art. 13: false materials are punished under Art. 40.Does not cover a person who has not emigrated and holds no foreign citizenship/permanent residence. Does not state any fee. open ↗
V072 · Official guidance SAFE, Tianjin branch
中华人民共和国外汇管理条例 (SAFE mirror, used to confirm the text is the in-force version)
 Second official copy of Order 532, confirming Art. 39/40 wording used above.Mirror only. open ↗
V081 · Primary statute / regulation State Taxation Administration / SAFE
国家税务总局 国家外汇管理局公告2013年第40号 — 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案
 A single outward payment above the USD 50,000 equivalent for services, income or current transfers to an overseas individual requires a 税务备案表 filed with the tax authority; the form is a filing, and the tax office may check whether the underlying tax was paid. Art. 3(13) exempts private travel/study/family visits.Is not a tax. Does not impose a rate. (Partly superseded by 公告2021年第19号 for listed clauses — flagged A6.) open ↗
V091 · Primary statute / regulation National People's Congress, via Shanghai tax bureau
中华人民共和国个人所得税法 (Individual Income Tax Law), consolidated text, status 全文有效
 Art. 2 lists the nine taxable income categories. Art. 3(3): interest, dividends, rent, property-transfer income and incidental income bear a flat 20%. Art. 6(5): the property-transfer base is proceeds minus original value minus reasonable expenses — 20% applies to the gain, not the principal. Art. 10(5) requires tax clearance before cancelling Chinese household registration on emigrating. Art. 16: foreign currency is converted at the RMB central parity rate.Contains no tax on converting currency. Salary already taxed is not taxed again on remittance. open ↗

V251 · Primary regulation SAFE

Method

  1. Fetch, don't recall. Every figure was read off the page during this session. The retrieval date is recorded in data/fx_anchor.json next to each value.
  2. Two instruments, not one. Where a proposition is contested (A1), both texts are quoted rather than the more convenient one.
  3. Blank beats plausible. A cell with no official source stays empty and is listed above. The build script fails if an UNVERIFIED row carries a value.
  4. Arithmetic is checked, not trusted. scripts/verify_cost.py re-derives all 40 × 6 cost cells from the anchor file and fails the build if any cell in the HTML disagrees by one cent.
  5. No number is typed into HTML. Every figure on these pages is emitted by scripts/build_cost.py from data/fx_anchor.json, so a rate change is one edit, not forty.
  6. Reject, don't stretch. Where a secondary source (a law firm note, a bank's marketing page, a comparison site) was the only thing available, it was not used. Two propositions that would have been convenient were dropped for exactly this reason: the CNH–CNY gap, and the U.S. dividend withholding rate.
