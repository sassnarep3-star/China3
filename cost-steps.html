Step by step, method by method

Each route below is written as the sequence of operations you would actually perform. Every step cites the instrument that governs it, so a bank officer or an adviser can check the step rather than take it on trust. Nothing here requires you to run a calculation: the prices are already in the cost tables.

Do this once, before any route. Ask the account-holding bank, in writing, three questions: (1) Is this account registered in your system as 境内个人 or 境外个人? (2) Which article will you process the purchase under — 59, 57, or a capital-account approval? (3) Give me the complete document checklist for my case. The answer changes which route is open to you, and it is the one fact no public source can settle: FX Regulations art. 52 defines 境内个人 to include a foreigner resident in China for one year, while PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 art. 37 defines the two categories by identity document. Both are in force.

M01 — Foreign individual: documented current-account purchase, then wire

Governing instrument: Guidelines (2020) art. 59

  1. Confirm classification in writing

    Ask the bank whether the account is 境内个人 or 境外个人 and which article it will use. Art. 59 applies to a 境外个人 converting lawful current-account RMB income earned in China.

    official basis →

  2. Assemble the source-of-funds file

    Art. 59 requires the ID plus evidence of the source of the RMB including tax documents (含税务凭证). Salary: contract, payslips, IIT withholding records. Property: sale contract, deed, 完税凭证. Business profit: distribution resolution, tax receipts.

    official basis →

  3. Check whether a tax filing is triggered

    If the single outward payment is the equivalent of USD 50,000 or more and falls in the categories listed in SAT/SAFE Announcement 2013 No. 40, file for a 备案表 with the local tax authority before the bank pays out.

    official basis →

  4. Get an all-in written quote

    Demand: customer rate and its timestamp, the reference rate used, the margin in basis points, conversion charge, wire fee, cable fee, whether full-amount credit is included, the correspondent charges, the receiving-bank fee, and the net USD expected.

    official basis →

  5. Execute the purchase and the remittance as one transaction

    The bank sells the FX and remits it. Do not split the payment and do not route it through another person's quota.

    official basis →

  6. Buy full-amount credit if you need a known net

    USD 25, or USD 15 if the receiving bank holds an account with BOC New York. This replaces an unpublished intermediary deduction with a published fixed charge.

    official basis →

  7. Keep the paper

    Purchase receipt (水单), remittance advice, approval letter and 核准件 if any, the 备案表, and the bank's written classification answer.

    official basis →

  8. File the U.S. side afterwards

    FBAR (FinCEN 114) due 15 April with automatic extension to 15 October; Form 8938 with the return if the threshold is met; report the CNY account interest as income; compute the § 988 gain on the conversion.

    official basis →

M02 — Global Smart Remittance premium

Governing instrument: BOC announcement 2017-11-09

  1. Same steps 1–3 as M01

    Classification, source file and any tax filing are identical — the product choice changes the fee, not the permission.

    official basis →

  2. Ask for the premium product by name

    中银全球智汇 is a telegraphic transfer with progress tracking, full passthrough of the remittance message including the reference, optional full-amount credit, and disclosure of the overseas bank's charge.

    official basis →

  3. Accept the tariff

    1.2‰ of the amount, minimum ¥200, maximum ¥1,000, plus the cable fee. On a large balance this is more than M01 because the cap is four times higher.

    official basis →

  4. Choose full-amount credit or not

    Full-amount credit means overseas bank charges are collected from you at a fixed amount instead of being deducted from the principal, so the beneficiary receives the full figure.

    official basis →

M03 — M01 plus full-amount credit

Governing instrument: BOC announcement 2017-11-09 note 2

  1. Run M01 exactly as written

    The underlying permission and documents are unchanged.

    official basis →

  2. Ask whether your receiving bank holds an account with BOC New York

    That single fact sets the charge at USD 15 instead of USD 25.

    official basis →

  3. Instruct full-amount credit on the payment

    Under a standard wire, overseas bank charges are deducted from the principal in transit. Under full-amount credit they are billed to you instead, at the published fixed amount.

    official basis →

  4. Verify the credit

    Compare the amount that left China with the amount that landed. If the difference is exactly the charge, the intermediary took nothing extra.

    official basis →

M04 — Domestic individual: the USD 50,000 convenience amount

Governing instrument: Guidelines (2020) arts. 54–57; 实施细则 art. 2

  1. Establish that you are a 境内个人 in this bank's system

    The convenience amount is available to 境内个人. A 境外个人 is processed under art. 59 instead. A foreigner holding a 外国人永久居留身份证 is treated as having the convenience amount.

    official basis →

  2. Present your ID and complete the purchase application

    The 个人购汇申请书 is where you declare the purpose; the "六不得" undertakings are on its face.

    official basis →

  3. Stay inside USD 50,000 per person per year

    This is a processing convenience amount, not a tax and not the only lawful limit. Exceeding it is not illegal — it just needs documents.

    official basis →

  4. If the balance is larger, plan the years

    At USD 50,000 a year a USD 400,000 balance needs eight years. Compare M01 and M05, which are not limited that way.

    official basis →

M05 — Emigrant property transfer

Governing instrument: PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 16

  1. Check the two threshold facts

    Art. 2: the route covers only a person who has emigrated from the mainland to a foreign country (or Hong Kong/Macau) and is now converting lawful property owned before acquiring emigrant status. Art. 8(4): you need the police-issued PRC household-registration cancellation certificate and settlement certification from a Chinese embassy or consulate.

    official basis →

  2. Settle PRC tax before cancelling the household registration

    IIT Law art. 13: a taxpayer who cancels PRC household registration because of emigration must complete a tax settlement before the cancellation. Doing it in the wrong order is the classic trap.

    official basis →

  3. Obtain the tax certificate for the property

    Art. 8(6): a tax certificate or tax-payment voucher issued by the competent tax authority where the property is located or the income arose.

    official basis →

  4. Apply to the right SAFE office

    Art. 5: the SAFE branch or office where your pre-emigration household registration was located. A central sub-branch may accept the file on its behalf.

    official basis →

  5. Submit the full file

    Art. 8: written application; the 移民财产对外转移申请人情况表 signed by you; the 个人财产对外转移外汇业务申请表; identity documents (cancellation certificate plus embassy certification); property-right documents such as the title copy, sale contract or relocation-compensation agreement; the tax certificate; anything else SAFE asks for. Notarise whatever is not already notarised.

    official basis →

  6. Know which office decides

    Art. 10: up to the equivalent of RMB 500,000 the local SAFE office decides and issues the approval letter and 核准件; above that the local office reviews and SAFE head office decides.

    official basis →

  7. Plan for the staging

    Art. 6: you must apply once for the whole balance. The first remittance may be up to half; after one year, up to half of what remains; after two years, the rest. A balance of RMB 200,000 or less may go in one payment after approval.

    official basis →

  8. Take the 核准件 to the bank

    The designated bank sells the FX and remits it directly to your account in your country of residence. Art. 11: it may not be withdrawn as cash inside China.

    official basis →

  9. For later tranches, bring the earlier paperwork

    Art. 8 final paragraph: from the second remittance onward you must present the earlier approval letter and the 核准件 issued for the previous payment.

    official basis →

  10. Never submit a document you cannot stand behind

    Art. 13: a false document or a repeat application on the same property is punished under FX Regulations art. 40 — up to 30%, or 30%–100% if serious.

    official basis →

M06 — Inheritance transfer

Governing instrument: PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 16, arts. 2 and 9

  1. Confirm you are the right kind of applicant

    Art. 2: a foreign citizen or a Hong Kong/Macau resident inheriting property located in the mainland.

    official basis →

  2. Apply where the deceased was registered

    Art. 5: the SAFE office at the deceased's household-registration location before death.

    official basis →

  3. Submit the inheritance file

    Art. 9: written application covering the reason, the relationship to the deceased, and a detailed written account of the source and realisation of the estate; the 个人财产对外转移外汇业务申请表; identity documents including embassy certification of overseas settlement, or the Hong Kong/Macau ID plus home-return permit or passport; proof of inheritance; the deceased's property-right documents and the tax certificate from the competent tax authority.

    official basis →

  4. Apply once per deceased person

    Art. 6: all property inherited from the same deceased person is applied for once and may be remitted in one or several payments; property from different deceased persons needs separate applications.

    official basis →

  5. Watch the same approval split and the same anti-fraud clause

    RMB 500,000 threshold per art. 10; art. 13 applies identically.

    official basis →

M07 — Balance already held in USD

Governing instrument: Guidelines (2020) art. 64; 实施细则 art. 15

  1. Confirm the account really holds USD

    If the balance is CNY, this route does not exist and you need a purchase under M01, M04 or M05 first.

    official basis →

  2. Remit on your ID up to USD 50,000 per day

    Art. 64: up to the equivalent of USD 50,000 accumulated in one day on ID alone; above that, ID plus transaction documents.

    official basis →

  3. Avoid taking it as cash first

    If you withdraw cash and then remit it, the limit drops to USD 10,000 a day and you need a customs-stamped declaration or the original withdrawal slip above that.

    official basis →

  4. Pay only the wire fee

    No conversion happens, so the FX margin is zero. This is the cheapest possible route — and the premise of the brief is that this is exactly what you cannot do, because the balance is in CNY.

    official basis →

M08 — Traveller's cash and overseas card withdrawal

Governing instrument: SAFE card-withdrawal notice; cash-carry guidance

  1. Do not treat this as a transfer mechanism

    It is listed because it is commonly proposed. At USD 400,000 it would take decades inside the annual cash-withdrawal cap.

    official basis →

  2. Respect the annual card-withdrawal cap

    RMB 100,000 per person per year of overseas cash withdrawal, and RMB 10,000 per card per day; exceeding it suspends the card for the rest of the year and the following year.

    official basis →

  3. Respect the border limits and the U.S. reporting duty

    Carrying more than USD 10,000 into or out of the United States requires a FinCEN Form 105 report. Failure to file is itself the offence.

    official basis →

  4. Price it honestly

    The cash rate is usually worse than the spot rate. On 2026-11-09 BOC quoted 现钞卖出价 and 现汇卖出价 identically at 669.19, so the table shows the same margin — but that equality is not guaranteed on the day you transact.

    official basis →

The two U.S. steps that decide whether any of this is cheap

  1. File the FBAR for every year you had the account.

    A U.S. citizen must file FinCEN Form 114 whenever the aggregate of foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any point in the year. It is filed with FinCEN, not with the return, and the automatic extension runs to 15 October. This is the single largest number in the whole analysis: the willful maximum is the greater of $165,353 or 50% of the balance.

    IRS FBAR page · 31 CFR 1010.821

  2. Report the income that created the balance, and the currency gain.

    Worldwide income includes the CNY interest even though China exempts savings interest since 2008-10-09. Separately, converting the RMB is a § 988 transaction: the exchange gain on a personal transaction is excluded, but only while it does not exceed USD 200 — above that the exclusion switches off and the gain is ordinary income. That is the only genuine "conversion tax" the research found, and it is a U.S. income tax on the currency movement, not a Chinese levy on converting.

    26 U.S.C. § 988(e) · 财税〔2008〕132号
