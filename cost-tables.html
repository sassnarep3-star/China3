The answer, before the tables

On the China side the honest, sourced cost of moving $400,000 lawfully is about $903 — roughly 0.226% — made up of $841.59 of bank spread and $61.27 of wire and cable charges. The number that can actually cost you six figures is not the conversion at all: it is a willful FBAR penalty, or a Chinese penalty on an unlawful route, both of which are percentage penalties on the whole amount.

So the least-penalty strategy is not a clever conversion trick. It is: use one lawful route, document the source, and fix the US reporting. That ordering is worth more than every basis point in the tables below.

IR-024 The spread is a one-day observation

CI04 is derived from the BOC board at 2026-11-09 09:15:45. Retail spreads have been deregulated since 2014 (S27), so every bank, branch and minute differs. Re-read the board before you rely on the figure; the historical search at the same site lets you re-derive it for any past date.

IR-025 The PRC 20% is a rate, not an amount

For property, dividends or rent the tax base is the gain or the gross receipt, neither of which is public for your file. The tables therefore show the rate and leave the amount to be computed from your own basis. No figure was invented.

IR-028 The US receiving fee is genuinely unknown

BOC's own tariff says overseas bank charges are passed on at actual cost, so a correspondent or receiving deduction exists — but no regulator publishes an amount, and it varies by US bank and account tier.
