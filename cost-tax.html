What the “considerable amount of taxes” actually is

Seven cost layers get called “the tax on converting”. Only two of them are money you pay for converting, and only one of them can reach six figures. Here they are separated, with the base each one applies to — because that is where the confusion starts.

The finding, stated plainly. No official source in this research imposes a tax, duty or charge on turning personal RMB into USD. The amounts people describe as a “conversion penalty” are, in official documents, one or more of: income tax on what created the money, the bank's price, the bank's fee, a filing requirement, or — most expensively — a U.S. reporting penalty for an account that was never declared.

The asymmetry, in one line. Converting USD 400,000 costs about USD 1,663. Mis-declaring it as willful can cost up to USD 200,000 — 120 times more. Every hour spent on the reporting file is worth more than every hour spent shopping for a better rate.
