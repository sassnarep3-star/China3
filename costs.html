Executive decision brief

  1. Prioritize a documented bank transfer if the money is lawful current-account income. SAFE permits overseas individuals to buy FX with identification and transaction-valued source materials including tax documents. Foreign permanent-resident ID holders have a convenience-quota provision. US citizenship alone does not establish the account's classification. SAFE: exact eligibility text
  2. Identify the money before choosing the channel. Earnings, property proceeds, inheritance, company money and previously imported savings are not interchangeable legal categories. The existing channel register explains conditional routes and their pros/cons; none is automatically available.
  3. Separate charges from taxes. A remittance tariff is not an income tax or statutory penalty. Tax documents can substantiate prior tax compliance; this source does not establish a universal tax on the transferred balance. Do not treat an unexplained bank deduction as tax. Official source
  4. Do not assume conversion has no US tax consequences. US tax determinations normally use USD and the relevant exchange rate. Actual tax needs transaction history and applicable tax rules; no universal percentage is quoted here. IRS guidance · Detailed tax research and reporting duties

What can actually be calculated?

BOC's public electronic-banking table states a cross-border wire charge of 1‰, minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260, plus CNY 150 for destinations outside Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan. It also expressly allows agreements, promotions and local differences. This is a published baseline, not a confirmed 2026 customer quote. Official source

For one eligible BOC wire to the US: F(A,r) = min(max(0.001 × A × r, 50), 260) + 150 CNY. Here A is USD wire principal before deductions, and r is the bank's applicable CNY-per-USD fee valuation rate. Neither r nor the FX execution rate is assumed. The arithmetic tariff component is CNY 200–410 per wire, excluding all other costs; this is not a bound on total cost. Multiple lawful installments incur their own fees. Do not split transactions to evade controls.

USD equivalent of this component is F(A,r)/r if that same rate is applicable. Total economic cost additionally needs actual FX spread, intermediary/recipient charges, applicable taxes and professional fees. No numeric all-in USD estimate can be supported without those inputs. The source supports the tariff only—not eligibility, conversion pricing, or total cost.
