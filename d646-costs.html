How each column is built.

Spread = amount × (6.6903 ÷ 6.67625 − 1) = 0.2104% — you pay the bank's selling rate rather than the middle rate. boc.cn S21

Wire fee = per transfer 0.1% of the CNY amount, floored at CNY 50, capped at CNY 260, plus CNY 150 cable, converted back at 6.6903. One wire per USD 50,000, because USD 50,000 is the documented ID-only convenience amount and each larger transfer needs documented evidence. boc.cn S20 · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

§988 gain = amount × (1 − 6.6903 ÷ 7.189) = 6.9370% of the dollars received, assuming the CNY was acquired at the 2024 IRS yearly average of 7.189. Change the basis and this column changes: it is an assumption, clearly labelled, not your figure. govinfo.gov S14 · irs.gov S13

Intermediary and U.S. receiving-bank fees have no published schedule and are left blank on every row. boc.cn S20

Why there is no "total" column. Adding a known bank cost to an unverified intermediary fee would produce a number that looks precise and is not. Worked example: USD 10,000

  1. CNY needed at the selling rate: 10,000 × 6.6903 = CNY 66,903.00 boc.cn S21
  2. Spread: 66,903 ÷ 6.67625 = USD 10,020.95, so the spread costs $21.04
  3. Wire fee: 0.1% of 66,903 = CNY 66.90, above the CNY 50 floor and below the CNY 260 cap, plus CNY 150 cable = CNY 216.90, i.e. $32.42 boc.cn S20
  4. Known bank cost: $53.47 = 0.535% of the amount
  5. §988 gain at the 2024 average basis: 10,000 × (1 − 6.6903 ÷ 7.189) = $693.70, ordinary income govinfo.gov S14 · irs.gov S13
  6. Tax at 24%: $166.49 — about 3.1× the entire published bank cost.

Worked example: USD 400,000

  1. Eight wires of USD 50,000 each, over eight calendar years at the convenience amount, or fewer wires if you can document a qualifying current-account purpose. safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03
  2. Spread over the whole amount: $841.79
  3. Wire fees: 8 wires at the CNY 260 cap + CNY 150 cable = CNY 3,280 → $490.26
  4. Known bank cost: $1,332.05 = 0.333%
  5. §988 gain at the 2024 basis: $27,747.95 — at 24% that is $6,659.51, at 37% $10,266.74.
  6. If the CNY was acquired at the 2025 average of 7.129 instead, the gain falls to $24,614.95 (6.15%).

Compare with the unlawful alternatives. A fine of 30% on USD 400,000 is USD 120,000, and 30%–100% on a serious case is USD 120,000–400,000 — against a published bank cost of $1,332.05. There is no amount at which bypassing the bank route is cheaper. safe.gov.cn S08
