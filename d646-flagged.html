The three that would change the recommendation

  1. D646-02 — what kind of money is it? Salary already taxed in China, sale proceeds, inheritance, or company funds. This single fact decides which lawful channel is available and whether any Chinese tax remains payable.
  2. D646-04 — what is your USD basis in the CNY? The §988 gain is measured against your acquisition rate. A basis at or below today's 6.6903 means no gain at all.
  3. D646-09 — were prior FBARs and Form 8938s filed? A missed information return can cost more than the entire conversion, and it must be dealt with before moving a large sum.
