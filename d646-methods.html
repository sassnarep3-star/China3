Every way to turn CNY into USD

16 routes, each with its official legal basis, its published capacity limit, the exact steps, and the pros and cons. 11 are lawful in at least some circumstances. The rest are unlawful and are listed only so they can be recognised and avoided.

Read the capacity column first. Several routes are perfectly lawful but can never move USD 400,000: card cash is capped at RMB 100,000 per person per year, cash carry at USD 10,000 per trip, card spending is consumption rather than conversion. Only documented remittance, the annual convenience amount over years, and the emigrant/inheritance property routes scale to a large sum. safe.gov.cn S06 · m.safe.gov.cn S07 · safe.gov.cn S02 · m.safe.gov.cn S09

Details, steps and trade-offs

M01 · Documented bank remittance (current account) Lawful

Published capacity: USD 50,000/person/year needs only ID; above that, transaction documents · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

Step by step

  1. Gather source documents for the CNY (bank statements, salary slips, contract, tax paid).
  2. Ask the holding bank, in writing, to classify the payment as current-account and list the required documents.
  3. Exchange CNY to USD at the bank's selling rate (S21) and book the outbound wire.
  4. Bank charges 0.1% min CNY 50 max CNY 260 + CNY 150 cable (S20).
  5. Above USD 50,000/day, supply the transaction documents the bank lists (S03).
  6. Above USD 50,000 single payment, a tax filing record may be required (S10).
  7. Keep the SWIFT advice, rate confirmation and fee receipt; declare FBAR/8938 in the U.S.

Pros

  • Lowest documented all-in cost of any lawful route
  • Fully lawful and audit-proof when the purpose is genuine
  • No third-party counterparty risk

Cons

  • Requires a bona fide current-account purpose; savings with no qualifying purpose are not automatically eligible
  • Bank reviews purpose consistency between purchase and remittance (S03)
  • Intermediary and U.S. receiving-bank fees are not published: UNVERIFIED

Sources: safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S10 · boc.cn S20 · boc.cn S21

M02 · Bank e-banking / mobile remittance Lawful

Published capacity: Same legal limits as the counter channel · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

Step by step

  1. Enable cross-border remittance in the bank's app and confirm the per-transaction limit shown.
  2. Complete the purpose declaration for each transaction.
  3. Book the wire at the published e-banking tariff (S20).
  4. Archive the electronic receipt and SWIFT confirmation.

Pros

  • Same published tariff, no branch visit
  • Rate and fee displayed before confirmation

Cons

  • App limits can be lower than the legal limit; the bank's own cap must be confirmed
  • Some purposes may still require a branch review

Sources: boc.cn S20 · boc.cn S21 · safe.gov.cn S03

M03 · Annual convenience amount, used year by year Lawful

Published capacity: USD 50,000/person/year with ID only · safe.gov.cn S02

Step by step

  1. Confirm the remaining convenience amount for the year with the bank.
  2. Remit up to USD 50,000 with ID only.
  3. Repeat in each subsequent calendar year; USD 400,000 takes 8 years.
  4. Do not use other people's quotas to accelerate (S04, S05).

Pros

  • Minimal paperwork
  • Spreads the s988 currency gain across tax years, which can lower the marginal rate
  • Lowest fee volatility

Cons

  • Very slow: 8 years for USD 400,000
  • Each year's conversion date is unknown in advance, so the FX outcome is not locked
  • The convenience amount can be adjusted by SAFE (S02)

Sources: safe.gov.cn S02 · govinfo.gov S14 · boc.cn S20

M04 · Documented current-account remittance above the convenience amount Lawful

Published capacity: No legal ceiling; authenticity review applies · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

Step by step

  1. Obtain the bank's written document checklist for the specific purpose.
  2. Provide the transaction evidence (contract, invoice, admission letter, medical bill, tax record).
  3. Bank performs authenticity review and purpose-consistency check (S03).
  4. Above USD 50,000 single payment, file the tax record where required (S10).
  5. Wire out and retain the full file.

Pros

  • The only route that can lawfully move a large sum quickly
  • No artificial splitting needed

Cons

  • Documents must be real; fabricated documents trigger penalty exposure (S08)
  • Bank discretion: the same documents may be accepted or refused by different branches
  • Genuine accumulated savings without a qualifying current-account purpose may still be refused

Sources: safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03 · safe.gov.cn S08 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S10

M05 · Emigrant property transfer (PBOC [2004] No.16) Lawful, narrow eligibility

Published capacity: Staged: first half, then half of remainder after 1 year, rest after 2 years · m.safe.gov.cn S09

Step by step

  1. Confirm you qualify: a mainland resident who has emigrated/settled abroad and cancelled hukou (S09 Art.2, Art.8).
  2. Apply once for the whole amount at the SAFE branch where your hukou was registered.
  3. Submit the identity, settlement and property-right documents, plus the tax certificate (S09 Art.8(6)).
  4. Receive approval; below CNY 500,000 the local branch decides, above it goes to SAFE headquarters (S09 Art.10).
  5. Bank sells FX and remits directly to your own overseas account; cash withdrawal is prohibited (S09 Art.11).
  6. Second and third tranches at 1 and 2 years (S09 Art.6).

Pros

  • Explicit legal channel for moving a whole estate, not just USD 50,000
  • Predictable staged schedule

Cons

  • Requires emigrating and cancelling Chinese hukou; a U.S. citizen who never held mainland hukou generally does not qualify
  • Tax certificate mandatory - the tax position must be settled first
  • Staged payouts mean the FX rate is not locked
  • One application only, for the entire amount

Sources: m.safe.gov.cn S09 · shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn S11

M06 · Inheritance property transfer (PBOC [2004] No.16) Lawful, narrow eligibility

Published capacity: One application per deceased person; one-off or instalments · m.safe.gov.cn S09

Step by step

  1. Liquidate the inherited mainland estate.
  2. Apply at the SAFE branch where the deceased's hukou was registered.
  3. Submit inheritance proof, the deceased's property-right documents and a tax certificate (S09 Art.9).
  4. Receive approval and remit to your own overseas account.

Pros

  • The only route designed for a foreign-citizen heir
  • Can be a single payout

Cons

  • Applies to inherited assets, not your own savings
  • Tax certificate and notarised documents required
  • Case-by-case review; no published fee

Sources: m.safe.gov.cn S09 · shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn S11

M07 · Company profit / dividend repatriation (if you own the entity) Lawful only if the CNY belongs to a company you own

Published capacity: Requires capital-account registration and tax clearance · safe.gov.cn S08 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S10

Step by step

  1. Confirm the funds are corporate, not personal.
  2. Settle corporate income tax and withholding tax on the dividend.
  3. Complete the tax filing record for the outbound payment (S10).
  4. Remit through the capital account after the bank's review (S08 Art.22).

Pros

  • Can move large amounts in a single transaction
  • Fully documented corporate route

Cons

  • Only works if you genuinely own the company - otherwise it is a disguised personal transfer
  • Corporate and withholding tax apply before remittance
  • Accounting and audit costs

Sources: safe.gov.cn S08 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S10

M08 · Foreign-currency cash withdrawal and carry Lawful up to USD 10,000

Published capacity: <=USD 5,000 no permit; 5,000-10,000 bank permit; >10,000 not permitted in principle · safe.gov.cn S06

Step by step

  1. Buy foreign-currency cash at the bank (cash selling rate).
  2. Up to USD 5,000: carry without a permit (S06).
  3. USD 5,000-10,000: obtain the carrying permit from the bank (S06).
  4. Above USD 10,000: requires a SAFE permit for listed special cases only (S06).
  5. On the U.S. side, file FinCEN Form 105 above USD 10,000 (S19).

Pros

  • Physical settlement, no counterparty
  • No wire fee

Cons

  • Hard ceiling of USD 10,000 per person per trip; 40 trips would be needed for USD 400,000
  • Physical loss/theft risk and insurance cost
  • Cash conversion uses the worse cash rate
  • Structuring repeated trips near the limit is exactly the conduct S04/S05 targets

Sources: safe.gov.cn S06 · cbp.gov S19 · safe.gov.cn S04

M09 · Overseas ATM cash withdrawal on a mainland bank card Lawful within caps

Published capacity: RMB 100,000/person/year; RMB 10,000/card/day · m.safe.gov.cn S07

Step by step

  1. Check the remaining annual card-cash allowance.
  2. Withdraw abroad within RMB 10,000/card/day.
  3. Stop at RMB 100,000/person/year; exceeding it suspends the facility for this and next year (S07).

Pros

  • No paperwork, immediate access
  • Useful for living costs rather than a lump sum

Cons

  • RMB 100,000/year is far below the target; USD 400,000 is impossible
  • ATM operator and issuer fees, plus a worse cash rate: UNVERIFIED
  • Borrowing or lending cards to evade the cap is prohibited (S07)

Sources: m.safe.gov.cn S07

M10 · Overseas card spending (POS) Lawful

Published capacity: No conversion to USD cash; consumption only · m.safe.gov.cn S07

Step by step

  1. Pay overseas merchants with the mainland card.
  2. Settle the card balance in CNY.

Pros

  • No wiring, no cash limit
  • Does not consume the convenience amount (S07)

Cons

  • You receive goods or services, not dollars in your account
  • Cannot fund a U.S. bank account
  • Merchant/issuer FX markup: UNVERIFIED

Sources: m.safe.gov.cn S07

M11 · Transfer to your own offshore account (HK/other) then convert Lawful only if the outbound leg is itself lawful

Published capacity: Outbound leg subject to the same SAFE rules · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

Step by step

  1. Complete a lawful outbound remittance (M01/M04) to your own account abroad.
  2. Convert offshore at the offshore rate.

Pros

  • Access to deeper offshore FX liquidity
  • Onshore/offshore rates can differ

Cons

  • Does not bypass anything: the binding constraint is the outbound leg
  • Two sets of fees
  • Onshore-offshore spread is market data, not a published official rate: UNVERIFIED

Sources: safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03

M12 · Borrowing other people's quotas / splitting (分拆) PROHIBITED

Published capacity: Zero lawful capacity · safe.gov.cn S04 · safe.gov.cn S05

Step by step

  1. Do not do this. SAFE operates a watch list for exactly this pattern (S04).
  2. Indicia include 5+ people remitting to one overseas person on the same or consecutive days (S05).

Pros

  • None that survive review

Cons

  • Explicitly prohibited by Huifa [2015] No.49 (S04)
  • Watch-list status for the year of listing plus 2 more years (S04)
  • Evasion penalties up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% if serious (S08)

Sources: safe.gov.cn S04 · safe.gov.cn S05 · safe.gov.cn S08

M13 · Underground bank / informal exchanger (地下钱庄) UNLAWFUL

Published capacity: Zero lawful capacity · safe.gov.cn S08

Step by step

  1. Do not do this.
  2. Illegal trading of foreign exchange carries confiscation plus a fine up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% if serious (S08 Art.45).
  3. Criminal liability where the conduct constitutes a crime (S08).

Pros

  • None - quoted 'no tax' rates are not a legal basis

Cons

  • Confiscation plus fine up to 30%-100% of the amount (S08)
  • Counterparty and seizure risk
  • U.S. tax and FBAR duties are unaffected by how the money moved

Sources: safe.gov.cn S08

M14 · Crypto / USDT over-the-counter conversion PROHIBITED in mainland China

Published capacity: Zero lawful capacity · m.safe.gov.cn S12

Step by step

  1. Do not do this.
  2. Fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat business is an illegal financial activity (S12).
  3. Offshore exchanges serving mainland residents are also unlawful (S12).

Pros

  • None that are defensible

Cons

  • Explicitly illegal financial activity (S12)
  • Banks and payment institutions must refuse to service it (S12)
  • U.S. consequences stack on top: s988 gain plus FBAR/8938 reporting

Sources: m.safe.gov.cn S12 · govinfo.gov S14

M15 · False documentation / misdeclared purpose UNLAWFUL

Published capacity: Zero lawful capacity · safe.gov.cn S08

Step by step

  1. Do not do this.
  2. Current-account FX payments must have a genuine, lawful transaction basis (S08 Art.12).
  3. Purchasing FX with false or invalid documents is illegal arbitrage, fined up to 30%-100% (S08 Art.40).

Pros

  • None

Cons

  • Fine up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% if serious (S08)
  • Destroys the audit trail you need for U.S. reporting
  • Potential criminal exposure (S08)

Sources: safe.gov.cn S08

M16 · Structuring sub-threshold transfers to avoid reporting UNLAWFUL

Published capacity: Zero lawful capacity · safe.gov.cn S04 · cbp.gov S19

Step by step

  1. Do not do this.
  2. The U.S. USD 10,000 Form 105 threshold applies to the total carried or sent at one time, and to families collectively (S19).
  3. Splitting to avoid quota or authenticity review is prohibited on the China side (S04).

Pros

  • None

Cons

  • Prohibited on both sides (S04, S19)
  • Seizure and civil or criminal penalties (S19)
  • Watch-list consequences (S04)

Sources: safe.gov.cn S04 · cbp.gov S19
