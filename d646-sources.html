China FX regulator

S01 · SAFE spokesman press conference on the Detailed Rules for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange

Issuer: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) · Published: 2007-01-05

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html

Verbatim excerpt: the annual quota for individual foreign exchange purchases be drastically raised from USD 20,000 to USD 50,000

What it establishes: Origin of the USD 50,000 annual individual convenience amount.

S02 · 2022 foreign-exchange policy Q&A (8): is there a hard USD 50,000 annual cap?

Issuer: SAFE Ningxia branch · Published: 2022-09-29

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html

Verbatim excerpt: 年度便利化额度不是个人每年购汇或者结汇的限额，只是超过等值5万美元的购汇或结汇不能仅凭本人有效身份证件办理

What it establishes: The USD 50,000 figure is a convenience threshold, not an absolute legal limit.

S03 · 2024 individual FX Q&A (3): outbound remittance of foreign exchange for current-account expenses

Issuer: SAFE Tianjin branch · Published: 2024-02-29

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0229/2402.html

Verbatim excerpt: 外汇账户内外汇汇出境外当日累计等值5万美元以下（含）的，凭本人有效身份证件办理；超过上述金额的，凭本人有效身份证件、有交易额的相关材料办理

What it establishes: Above USD 50,000 per day, documents evidencing the transaction amount are required; purpose consistency is reviewed.

S04 · Huifa [2015] No.49 - further improving individual foreign-exchange administration (splitting ban, watch list)

Issuer: SAFE · Published: 2015-12-31

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2015/1231/5442.html

Verbatim excerpt: 个人在办理外汇业务时，应当遵守个人外汇管理有关规定，不得以分拆等方式规避额度及真实性管理

What it establishes: Splitting transactions to avoid quota/authenticity review is prohibited; watch-list for 3 years.

S05 · Notice on further improving individual settlement/sale of FX business (splitting characteristics)

Issuer: SAFE Hebei branch · Published: 2015-05-04

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/2015/0504/415.html

Verbatim excerpt: 5个以上不同个人同日、隔日或连续多日分别购汇后，将外汇汇给境外同一个人或机构

What it establishes: Defines the objective indicia of prohibited splitting (e.g. 5+ people remitting to one overseas person).

S06 · Huifa [2003] No.102 - interim measures on carrying foreign-currency cash across the border

Issuer: SAFE / General Administration of Customs · Published: 2003-08-28

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2003/0828/5444.html

Verbatim excerpt: 出境人员原则上不得携带超过等值10000美元的外币现钞出境

What it establishes: Cash carry: <=USD 5,000 no permit; 5,000-10,000 bank permit; above USD 10,000 not permitted in principle.

S07 · Huifa [2017] No.29 - regulating large overseas ATM cash withdrawals on bank cards (press Q&A)

Issuer: SAFE · Published: 2017-12-30

Link: http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/8129.html

Verbatim excerpt: 个人持境内银行卡在境外提取现金，本人名下银行卡（含附属卡）合计每个自然年度不得超过等值10万元人民币

What it establishes: Card cash abroad capped at RMB 100,000/person/year and RMB 10,000/card/day.

S08 · State Council Order No.532 - Regulations on Foreign Exchange Administration (penalties)

Issuer: SAFE · Published: 2008-08-06

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html

Verbatim excerpt: 有违反规定将境内外汇转移境外，或者以欺骗手段将境内资本转移境外等逃汇行为的，由外汇管理机关责令限期调回外汇，处逃汇金额30%以下的罚款；情节严重的，处逃汇金额30%以上等值以下的罚款

What it establishes: Evasive outflow (逃汇) fine up to 30% of the amount, or 30%-100% if serious; also Art.45 for illegal FX trading.

China central bank

S09 · PBOC Announcement [2004] No.16 - interim measures on the transfer abroad of individual property

Issuer: People's Bank of China (PBOC) · Published: 2004-11-16

Link: http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2004/1116/5461.html

Verbatim excerpt: 移民转移必须一次性申请拟转移出境的全部财产金额，分步汇出。首次可汇出金额不得超过全部申请转移财产的一半

What it establishes: The 2004 text only: staged payouts (Art.6), tax certificate required (Art.8(6)), and the Art.10 head-office threshold. BOTH the staging and the threshold are cancelled by S22; read the two together.

S22 · SAFE circular 汇发〔2014〕2号 - further improving and adjusting capital-account FX policy (as amended)

Issuer: SAFE · Published: 2014-01-10, in force 2014-02-10

Link: https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0903/2594.html

Verbatim excerpt: 六、简化个人财产转移售付汇管理（一）…取消财产转移总金额超过等值人民币50万元报国家外汇管理局备案的要求。（二）取消移民财产转移分次汇出的要求。申请人向原户籍所在地外汇局办理移民财产转移核准手续后，银行可在核准件审批额度内一次或分次汇出相关资金。

What it establishes: Cancels both the RMB 500,000 head-office reporting threshold (S09 Art.10) and the forced staged payout over two years (S09 Art.6); the bank may now remit once or in instalments within the approved amount.
