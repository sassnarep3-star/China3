Getting money out of China, with the least tax and penalty exposure

A U.S. citizen with personal CNY in a mainland China bank account. This page puts the single most important findings first, then links to the method list, the full USD 10,000–400,000 cost ladder, the tax and penalty breakdown, the verification log and everything still unconfirmed.

The headline. On the lawful bank route the published conversion cost is small: 0.210% of the amount as the bank's spread, plus at most CNY 260 + CNY 150 per wire. For USD 10,000 that is $53.47 (0.535%). For USD 400,000 it is $1,332.05 (0.333%).

The large number is not a Chinese tax and not a bank fee. It is the U.S. tax on the currency gain under 26 U.S.C. §988. If the CNY was acquired at the 2024 IRS yearly average of 7.189 and converted at today's Bank of China selling rate of 6.6903, the gain is 6.94% of the dollars you receive — about $693.70 on USD 10,000 and $27,747.95 on USD 400,000 — taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate.

The five findings that matter

1. "Considerable taxes" is almost certainly a U.S. tax, not a Chinese one

The Individual Income Tax Law taxes nine enumerated categories of income — wages, remuneration, author's remuneration, royalties, business income, interest/dividends/bonuses, property rental, property transfer and incidental income. Converting your own CNY into USD is not one of them. No SAFE, PBOC or STA document reviewed imposes a tax on the conversion itself. What is taxable is the U.S. side: §988 treats the disposition of foreign currency — including a foreign-currency bank deposit — as a currency transaction, and the resulting gain is ordinary income. shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn S11 · govinfo.gov S14

2. The §988 personal-transaction exclusion is almost irrelevant at these amounts

§988(e)(2) switches off the recognition rule for an individual's personal transaction, but only if the gain does not exceed USD 200. At today's rate that means the whole exclusion disappears above roughly USD 2,883 converted. Every rung of the USD 10,000–400,000 ladder is above it. govinfo.gov S14

3. Timing is the only lever that changes the tax, and it cuts both ways

The gain is measured against the rate at which you acquired the CNY. Converting in several calendar years, at the USD 50,000 convenience amount per year, does not reduce the gain per dollar — but it does let you use lower brackets each year and it avoids stacking a single-year gain on top of a single-year income. The ladder shows five brackets instead of guessing your one rate. govinfo.gov S14 · irs.gov S17

4. Almost every "faster" route is a fine, not a fee

Splitting across other people's quotas is expressly prohibited and results in a multi-year watch list. Underground exchange, crypto/USDT settlement, false documents and structuring are all unlawful, with fines up to 30% of the amount — 30% to 100% if serious — plus confiscation. That is five to fifteen times the entire published bank cost, and it does not reduce the U.S. tax by a cent. safe.gov.cn S04 · safe.gov.cn S08 · m.safe.gov.cn S12

5. Report, don't restructure

The FBAR and Form 8938 are information returns: filing them costs nothing and does not tax the money. Not filing them is the single largest financial risk in this whole picture — up to USD 16,536 per non-willful violation and USD 165,353 per willful one, against a USD 10,000 account trigger. ecfr.gov S15 · irs.gov S16

What to do, in order

  1. Establish the character of the money. Salary already taxed in China, sale proceeds, inheritance, or company funds? This decides eligibility and decides the Chinese tax, and it is the one fact nobody else can supply.
  2. Establish your USD basis and acquisition date for the CNY. Without it the §988 gain cannot be computed — the ladder's 2024-average assumption is a placeholder, and stated as one.
  3. Ask the holding bank, in writing, to classify the payment and list the documents it requires. Above USD 50,000 per year the ID-only convenience amount stops applying; above USD 50,000 per single payment a tax filing record may also be needed. safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn S03 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S10
  4. Choose the lawful channel that matches the facts: documented current-account remittance, the annual convenience amount spread over years, or — only if the eligibility really applies — the emigrant or inheritance property transfer. m.safe.gov.cn S09
  5. Get the all-in quote in writing before booking: customer rate, spread, tariff, cable, intermediary and receiving-bank charges, and expected net USD.
  6. File the U.S. side. FBAR by 15 April (automatic extension to 15 October), Form 8938 with the return if the threshold is met, FinCEN Form 105 if cash above USD 10,000 crosses the border. irs.gov S16 · cbp.gov S19

Do not use: other people's quotas, underground exchangers, crypto/USDT, false invoices or purpose declarations, or structured sub-threshold transfers. Each is separately unlawful, each is penalised as a percentage of the whole amount, and none of them changes the U.S. tax. safe.gov.cn S04 · safe.gov.cn S08 · m.safe.gov.cn S12 · cbp.gov S19
