What was rejected or corrected during this pass

Corrected: earlier pages describe the §988 personal-transaction exclusion loosely. The statute's actual condition is a USD 200 gain ceiling (S14), which fails at every rung of this ladder. This pass states the test, not a summary of it.

Corrected: a 15% capital-gain rate is sometimes applied to this gain. §988(a)(1)(A) makes it ordinary; the capital election is limited to forward/futures/option contracts (S14). No capital rate is shown in the ladder.

Not carried forward: the 2026-11-09 board stamp of 6.6919 is replaced by today's 6.6903 (S21). Both are genuine; only one is current. See flag D646-11.

Left blank on purpose: intermediary/correspondent fees, U.S. receiving-bank fees, offshore CNY/CNH basis, card ATM operator fees. No official schedule was found for any of them.

What this pass did not verify

The 30+ pages already in the repository were not re-derived line by line this time. Their own validators pass on main, and their figures remain on their own pages, unchanged. This refresh adds a current, independently computed set alongside them; where a rate has moved, the difference is recorded as D646-11 rather than silently rewritten.
