U05Whether a currency gain is a 'personal transaction'
medium		§ 988(e)(1) applies the section only to transactions that are not an individual's personal transaction, and § 988(e)(3) makes a transaction non-personal to the extent its expenses fall under §162 or §212. Which side a savings balance falls on is a legal judgement this site does not make.A U.S. tax professional, with the dates and rates at which the RMB was acquired.S27 US Code S43 IRS
