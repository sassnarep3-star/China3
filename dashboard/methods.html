Every lawful route, and the ones to avoid

Fourteen lawful routes or conditional scenarios and six prohibited ones, each tied to its official or first-party source. Which lawful route is open to you depends on where the money came from and which ID your account is held under.

A foreign passport holder is a 境外个人 under the SAFE rules. The USD 50,000-a-year amount that Chinese citizens use on ID alone does not apply to buying foreign currency on a passport; only holders of the Foreign Permanent Resident ID get it. A foreigner who has lived in China for a year may be classed differently by the bank: see U10.

Prohibited: do not use

These are the shortcuts people are usually offered when a bank asks for documents. Each one is named in an official rule or enforcement notice.

RouteWhy it is prohibitedPenaltySource
P01Using relatives' or friends' USD 50,000 amounts (splitting, 'ant moving')
Prohibited		SAFE treats it as evading the quota. The people involved go on the watch list for the year and the next two years; the organiser can be fined as 逃汇.Fine up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% in serious cases; criminal liability possible.S10 SAFE S22 SAFE S17 State Council
P02Private swaps, money changers, apps, underground banks
Prohibited		Only banks and licensed exchange outlets may convert currency. 'I pay you RMB in China, you pay me dollars abroad' is private FX trading.Warning, confiscation of gains, fine up to 30%; 30%-100% in serious cases; criminal liability possible.S21 SAFE S17 State Council
P03False documents or a false purpose to buy foreign currency
Prohibited		Buying FX with false or invalid documents is 非法套汇.Bank must reverse the conversion; fine up to 30%, 30%-100% in serious cases; untrue documents alone: up to RMB 50,000 for an individual.S17 State Council S12 PBOC
P04Buying cryptocurrency or stablecoins with RMB to move value out
Prohibited		Exchanging legal tender for virtual currency in China is an illegal financial activity under the February 2026 notice.Activity is banned outright; funds have no legal protection.S23 PBOC
P05Carrying more than USD 10,000 in cash out of China, or entering the U.S. without declaring it
Prohibited		Above USD 10,000 needs a SAFE permit that is in principle not granted. In the U.S., undeclared amounts over USD 10,000 can be seized.China: warning and fine up to 20% of the amount. U.S.: seizure and forfeiture, civil or criminal penalties.S20 SAFE S17 State Council S35 CBP
P06Borrowing or lending bank cards to get around the overseas cash-withdrawal cap
Prohibited		Expressly forbidden by the 2017 SAFE notice.Punished under the Foreign Exchange Regulation.S19 SAFE S17 State Council
