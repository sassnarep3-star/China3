Questions to answer before any decision

Nothing on this site can pick a route for you until these are answered. Each one is stated with what is already known from an official page, what is not known, and the cheapest way to discover it. No answer is assumed.

8of them high priority

Why gates rather than a checklist: what the money legally is (gate 2) decides which document list the bank applies, and the account's identity category (gate 1) decides which half of the rule book applies. Getting gate 4 (the U.S. side) right will not help if gate 2 is wrong.

Every question here is about facts only you, your bank and your tax adviser hold. The Methods page shows what each answer unlocks, and the Costs page prices each route at a published rate.

Gate 1 — identity of the account

QuestionWhat we know (sourced)How to find outSource
Q01Is the Chinese bank account recorded under your foreign passport or under a Chinese ID?
high		The PBOC Measures classify by the ID document produced when the account was opened (Art. 37). The State Council Regulation, Art. 52, separately calls a foreigner who has lived in China for one continuous year a 境内个人. The two definitions do not line up.Ask the branch in writing for the account subject category (账户主体类别) and keep the reply. Free of charge: 95566 or any branch counter.S17 State Council S16 SAFE
Q02How long have you lived in China in the current or last tax year; are you a Chinese tax resident?
medium		Individual Income Tax Law Art. 1: resident if domiciled in China or present 183 days in a tax year; residents pay on worldwide income, non-residents on China-source income.Count days from entry stamps and your residence permit; confirm with the local tax office (12366).S20 SAFE

Gate 2 — what the money is

QuestionWhat we know (sourced)How to find outSource
Q03What is the money, line by line: salary, business profit, a home sale, rent, a gift, an inheritance, or money you brought in from abroad?
high		Art. 59 requires source-of-funds material including tax evidence for income earned in China; Art. 65 needs only ID for FX already in the account; the 2004 announcement covers only pre-departure property and inheritances.Sort the bank statements by counterparty for the last 3-5 years and label every deposit. This is the single most useful document you can produce for the bank.S04 SAFE S07 SAFE S08 SAFE
Q04Has Chinese individual income tax already been paid or withheld on the money that is in the account?
high		Art. 3: 3%-45% on wages and service income, 20% flat on dividends, interest, rent, property gains and incidental income. Savings-deposit interest accrued from 2008-10-09 is exempt.Pull a 完税证明 (tax payment certificate) for the last five years from the local tax office or the 个人所得税 app. It shows what was withheld, by whom, and when.S20 SAFE S21 SAFE
Q05Is any part of the balance a gift, or money whose origin you cannot document?
high		Art. 59 requires source material. SAFE's answers describe income and property, not gifts. The Regulation lets the bank refuse a transaction whose authenticity it doubts (Art. 68 of the 2020 guidelines).Ask the branch in writing before converting anything. In parallel, the person who gave the money may be able to remit it themselves under one of the documented routes.S04 SAFE S07 SAFE

Gate 3 — what the bank quoted

QuestionWhat we know (sourced)How to find outSource
Q06What exactly did the bank say would be charged: a tax, a fee, a spread, or a refusal to convert? Which office said it, and in writing?
high		State Council Regulation Art. 5: no restriction on current international payments and transfers. No tax on the conversion itself was found in any source read.Ask for the quote in writing with the tariff item or legal basis named. If a tax is asserted, ask for the assessment document (完税凭证 / 税务事项通知书).S16 SAFE S04 SAFE S18 PBOC
Q07Does the branch actually operate the route you qualify for, and at what rate and fee?
medium		S02 note 1: 另有协议约定、价格优惠、地方性收费差异的，按照协议或当地公示的内容收取相关费用.Get a dated written quote for the whole amount, including the correspondent and receiving bank. Compare at least two banks.S01 BOC S02 BOC

Gate 4 — the US side

QuestionWhat we know (sourced)How to find outSource
Q08When and at what rate did you acquire the RMB? Do you have the records?
high		§988(c)(1)(C): disposing of non-functional currency is a §988 transaction. IRS publishes yearly average rates (2024: 7.189, 2025: 7.129 CNY/USD).Reconstruct from bank statements and payslips by year, and use the IRS yearly average for each year, consistently.S25 STA S26 STA
Q09Is moving this balance a 'personal transaction' for §988(e) purposes, and is any gain in the realistic range below USD 200?
medium		§988(e)(2) text: the exclusion does not apply if the gain would exceed $200.Run the figure with your tax preparer before you convert, not after.S25 STA
Q10Have you filed FBARs and Form 8938 for every year the account existed?
high		FBAR threshold USD 10,000 aggregate at any time in the calendar year; Form 8938 thresholds USD 50,000/75,000 for an unmarried filer living in the US, with a USD 60,000 penalty ceiling.Check your filed returns and the BSA E-Filing system. If anything is missing, get advice before you move the money: the move does not create the obligation, but it makes the account visible.S27 US Code S28 IRS S29 IRS S30 FinCEN

Gate 5 — the route

QuestionWhat we know (sourced)How to find outSource
Q11Were you ever a mainland resident with a Chinese hukou that has since been cancelled?
high		The 2004 announcement covers 移民 (emigrants) and 继承人 (heirs) only; approval is at the former hukou.The police station that held your hukou issues the cancellation certificate; the embassy or consulate certifies your settlement abroad.S08 SAFE S09 SAFE S10 SAFE
Q12Do you hold the PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card (not a visa, not a residence permit)?
medium		Art. 59 second sentence: 持有外国人永久居留身份证的境外个人适用购汇年度便利化额度.Check the card; the number is printed on it.S05 SAFE S06 SAFE
Q13Is any part of the balance already in a foreign-currency account inside China?
medium		Art. 65(1): 外汇账户内外汇汇出，凭本人有效身份证件办理.Look at the account statement: USD and CNY sub-accounts are listed separately.S07 SAFE

Q14Do you need the whole amount at once, or can it move over several years?
medium		USD 50,000 a year per person on the PR-ID card; RMB 100,000 a year per person on overseas ATM withdrawals.Decide the cash need first; it eliminates routes faster than the cost tables do.S06 SAFE S13 SAFE
