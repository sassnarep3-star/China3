Step by step, for each lawful route

Each step names the official rule behind it. Banks can ask for more than the rule's minimum, so get the branch's document list in writing before you start.

L01 · Documented income earned in China (salary, fees, other current-account income)

The RMB is your own lawful income from China and you can show where it came from and that tax was dealt with. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Ask the bank that holds the account to confirm it is recorded under your foreign passport (a 境外个人 account). The bank sets this from the ID used when the account was opened.S18 PBOC
Step 2Collect the documents that show where the RMB came from and how much: for salary, the employment contract and payslips; for other income, the contract or payment record. The rule calls these 有交易额的购汇资金来源材料.S04 SAFE
Step 3Get tax evidence for that income from the Chinese tax office: a tax payment certificate (完税证明), a tax-exemption certificate, or the tax filing form. Bank-deposit interest earned since 9 October 2008 is exempt.S11 SAFE S26 STA
Step 4For a single payment above USD 50,000 in a listed category (work pay, dividends, property-sale income and others) the tax filing rule (对外支付税务备案) comes into play; ask the tax office which form of tax evidence it will issue. The filing is a record, not a tax.S16 SAFE S11 SAFE
Step 5If tax on the income was never withheld, pay it. The law makes the taxpayer liable when the payer did not withhold.S25 STA
Step 6Take passport, source documents and tax evidence to the bank and buy USD. The bank applies its selling rate at that moment.S04 SAFE S01 BOC
Step 7Wire the USD to your own account in the United States. Bank of China's published personal fee is 0.1% (RMB 50-260) plus RMB 150 cable.S02 BOC
Step 8Keep every document. On the U.S. side: FBAR if your foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the year, Form 8938 above its thresholds, and report any currency gain.S30 FinCEN S29 IRS S27 US Code

L02 · Change back unused RMB with the original exchange slip

You brought foreign currency into China, converted it to RMB less than 24 months ago, and still have the bank's exchange slip (兑换水单). Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Find the bank's original exchange slip (兑换水单) from when you converted foreign currency into RMB. It must be dated within the last 24 months.S09 SAFE
Step 2Take your passport and the slip to the bank. You can change back up to the unused RMB from that conversion.S09 SAFE
Step 3The bank sells you USD at its selling rate.S01 BOC
Step 4Wire the USD to your own U.S. account, or keep it in a foreign-currency account and wire later (see L03).S02 BOC S08 SAFE
Step 5Without a slip, only USD 500 equivalent a day can be changed back on the passport alone.S09 SAFE

L03 · Wire out foreign currency that is already in your Chinese foreign-currency account

Part of the balance is still USD (or another foreign currency), for example money wired in from abroad and never converted. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Check with the bank how much of the balance is held as foreign currency in a foreign-currency account in your name.S18 PBOC
Step 2With your passport, instruct the bank to remit it abroad. For a foreign individual, remittance from the FX account is done on valid ID; unused FX that was wired in can go back the way it came.S08 SAFE S18 PBOC
Step 3Pay the wire fee: 0.1% (RMB 50-260) plus RMB 150 cable at Bank of China's published personal rate.S02 BOC
Step 4U.S. side: the account still counts for FBAR and Form 8938 for the year.S30 FinCEN S29 IRS

L04 · Proceeds from selling a home you owned in China

The RMB is what you received for selling residential property registered in your name. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Complete the sale and the title transfer at the housing authority. The remittance can only be done after the transfer of ownership is registered.S15 SAFE
Step 2Pay the taxes of the sale and obtain the tax certificate. Income tax on a property transfer is 20% of the gain (price minus original cost and reasonable expenses).S25 STA S15 SAFE
Step 3Take to the bank: passport; the transfer contract registered with the housing authority and the registration proof; the tax certificate (above USD 50,000 this is normally the tax filing form).S15 SAFE S16 SAFE
Step 4Ask for no more than the sale price minus the taxes of that sale (your share if co-owned). The bank checks the amount against the tax certificate.S15 SAFE
Step 5The bank converts and wires the USD to your account abroad.S01 BOC S02 BOC

L05 · Inheritance transfer (继承财产转移)

You are a foreign citizen who inherited assets in China from someone whose household registration was there. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Obtain proof of your right to inherit (for example a notarised inheritance certificate) and the property documents of the person who died.S12 PBOC
Step 2Turn the inherited assets into RMB and get a tax certificate from the tax office where the estate's property is located.S12 PBOC
Step 3Get a certificate of your residence abroad issued or certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate.S12 PBOC
Step 4Apply once, for the whole estate, to the SAFE office of the place where the decedent's household registration was: written application, application form, ID, inheritance proof, property and tax documents.S12 PBOC S13 SAFE S14 SAFE
Step 5After SAFE issues the approval (核准件), take it to the bank. The bank buys USD and remits it directly to your own account in your country of residence, in one or several transfers. Cash withdrawal is not allowed.S12 PBOC S13 SAFE
Step 6U.S. side: if the bequest from the foreign estate is more than USD 100,000 in the year, file Form 3520 by the due date. The form reports the receipt; a late filing can cost 5% a month, up to 25%.S32 IRS S33 US Code

L06 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)

You used to live in mainland China, emigrated, and the assets were yours before you obtained emigrant status. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Confirm you fit the definition: a person who moved from mainland China to another country, moving property owned in China before obtaining emigrant status.S12 PBOC
Step 2Gather: proof from the public security bureau that your Chinese hukou was cancelled; a settlement certificate issued or certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate; property documents; tax certificates from where the property or income arose.S12 PBOC
Step 3Apply once, for everything you intend to move, to the SAFE office of your former hukou location (written application, the applicant form and the application form).S12 PBOC S13 SAFE S14 SAFE
Step 4After approval, the bank may remit the approved amount in one transfer or in instalments. The 2004 staging rule (half, then half a year later) was removed in 2014.S13 SAFE
Step 5The bank remits directly to your own account in your country of residence.S12 PBOC

L07 · Annual USD 50,000 convenience amount with a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID

You hold the PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card (not just a visa or residence permit). Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Confirm you hold the PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. A work or residence permit is not the same document.S05 SAFE
Step 2With that card, buy up to USD 50,000 equivalent per calendar year at the bank or in its app. No source documents are needed inside the yearly amount.S05 SAFE S06 SAFE
Step 3Wire the USD to your own account abroad.S02 BOC S08 SAFE
Step 4For anything above the yearly amount, use the documented-income route (L01). Do not use other people's amounts (P01).S06 SAFE S10 SAFE

L08 · Send the RMB itself abroad as cross-border RMB, convert outside the mainland

The RMB is lawful current-account income (the PBOC example is a foreign employee's salary) and your bank offers personal cross-border RMB remittance. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Ask your bank whether it handles personal cross-border RMB remittance for current-account income. The PBOC's 2018 notice supports it; individual banks decide whether to offer it.S24 PBOC
Step 2Prepare the same income and tax evidence as for L01. The bank's exact document list was not verified in this pass.S24 PBOC
Step 3Remit the RMB to an account in your own name outside the mainland that can receive RMB.S24 PBOC
Step 4Convert the RMB to USD at that bank. Its rate and fees are not officially published, so the cost is left blank on this site.S24 PBOC

L09 · Dividend from your own Chinese company

The money sits in a company you own, not in a personal account. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Confirm the company has distributable profit and has paid its taxes.S13 SAFE
Step 2Pass a board or shareholder profit-distribution resolution.S13 SAFE
Step 3The company withholds 20% individual income tax on the dividend.S25 STA
Step 4For a remittance above USD 50,000, complete the tax filing and give the bank the original filing form with the resolution.S13 SAFE S16 SAFE
Step 5The bank converts and remits the after-tax dividend to you.S01 BOC
Step 6U.S. side: the dividend is taxable income; Chinese tax paid may be claimed as a foreign tax credit on Form 1116.S34 IRS

L10 · Cash withdrawals abroad with a mainland UnionPay debit card

You have a UnionPay debit card on the RMB account and travel outside the mainland. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Make sure the RMB account has a UnionPay debit card that works abroad.S19 SAFE
Step 2Withdraw cash at ATMs outside the mainland: at most RMB 10,000 per card per day, and RMB 100,000 per person per calendar year counting all your cards.S19 SAFE
Step 3Bank of China charges RMB 12 plus 1% of each withdrawal, at most RMB 112. The network exchange rate and any fee from the ATM owner are extra and not published in one place.S03 BOC
Step 4Stop at the yearly cap: going over it suspends overseas withdrawals for the rest of that year and the next. Using someone else's card is prohibited (P06).S19 SAFE
Step 5Bringing more than USD 10,000 in cash into the U.S. must be declared on FinCEN Form 105.S35 CBP

L11 · Carry foreign-currency cash out in person

You can lawfully buy the foreign currency first (L01, L02 or L07) and want it as banknotes. Cost table · Route details

StepWhat to doSource
Step 1Buy the foreign currency lawfully first (L01, L02 or L07). Carrying cash does not replace the document rules.S04 SAFE S09 SAFE S05 SAFE
Step 2Up to USD 5,000 per trip: customs lets it through. USD 5,000-10,000: ask the bank for a carrying permit (《携带外汇出境许可证》) when you withdraw the cash.S20 SAFE
Step 3Do not carry more than USD 10,000: a SAFE permit is needed and is in principle not granted.S20 SAFE
Step 4On arrival in the U.S., declare if you (or your family or group together) carry more than USD 10,000.S35 CBP
