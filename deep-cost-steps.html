Execution playbook

Exactly how each route works, in order

Same thirteen routes as the cost model. Each step names the document or the office, so the sequence can be checked against the bank's own checklist before any money moves.

Do this before choosing a route. Ask the account-opening branch two questions in writing: (1) is this account classified 境内个人 or 境外个人, and (2) under which article will it process a purchase and remittance of this size? The answer decides whether the USD 50,000 convenience amount binds or whether you proceed on source documents — the single most important fork in this project (flagged as IR-007).

L01 Foreign individual: current-account income, FX purchase + wire (Art. 59)

No fixed annual amount applies. ID + source-of-funds documents including tax evidence. Single payment above USD 50,000 also needs the tax filing form.

  1. Collect the paper trail for the source of the money: employment contract and payslips, or the sale contract, the PRC tax receipt and the bank statements showing the RMB arriving.
  2. Ask the account-opening branch, in writing, whether the account is classified 境内个人 or 境外个人, and which article it will process under (Art. 59 for a foreign individual).
  3. Obtain the tax evidence: 完税证明, 免税证明, or the 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表 if a single payment exceeds USD 50,000.
  4. Request an all-in written quote: customer rate, the reference rate and its timestamp, spread, wire fee, cable fee, guarantee fee, and the exact USD the US account will receive.
  5. Instruct one payment. Keep the bank's 购汇/付汇 documents; you need the date and the rate for your US return and for Form 8938/FBAR.
  6. Report on the US side: FBAR by 15 April (automatic extension to 15 October), Form 8938 with the return, and the interest or gain on the money in income.

Sources: C06 ↗ C07 ↗ C32 ↗ C03 ↗

L02 Chinese-resident individual: non-quota current-account purchase (Art. 57)

No fixed annual amount applies, but the purchase must be for a genuine current-account purpose with documents.

  1. Confirm the account is a 境内个人 account (PRC resident ID), because the convenience amount and Art. 57 only work for that classification.
  2. Assemble the documents for the specific current-account purpose; the purchase must match a real transaction, not a savings transfer.
  3. If a single payment exceeds USD 50,000, file the 税务备案表 before the bank will remit.
  4. Get the written all-in quote and check the spread against the measured 0.42% benchmark on this site.
  5. Instruct, keep the documents, and report the account and the income in the US.

Sources: C05 ↗ C32 ↗ C03 ↗

L03 Annual convenience amount (USD 50,000 per person per year)

ID only, no transaction documents, but capped at the equivalent of USD 50,000 per calendar year and not for capital-account uses.

  1. Use it only for the amount inside USD 50,000 per calendar year, per person.
  2. Buy FX with ID only; no transaction documents, but the 个人购汇申请书 representations still apply.
  3. Do not split a larger balance across relatives or across banks to stay under the figure: that is the 分拆 pattern SAFE fines under Art. 39.
  4. For a USD 400,000 balance the route needs eight calendar years, so run it alongside a no-ceiling route rather than instead of one.
  5. Report every year in the US, including the years when nothing moves.

Sources: C05 ↗ C06 ↗ C03 ↗

L04 Account already holds USD: wire out without converting

No conversion spread because the money is already in USD. Outward remittance evidence rules still apply.

  1. Confirm the account balance is already USD (or another foreign currency), not CNY.
  2. Provide the outward-remittance evidence the bank requires; above USD 50,000 in a day the bank will ask for transaction documents.
  3. Pay the wire and cable fees, and decide whether to buy the full-amount guarantee so the US bank does not deduct from principal.
  4. Keep the original conversion documents too: the US basis and the FBAR values depend on them.

Sources: C03 ↗ C29 ↗

L05 Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)

One application for the whole amount; first remittance at most 50%, then at most half the remainder after one year, remainder after two years. Local SAFE approves up to CNY 500,000; above that SAFE headquarters.

  1. Check eligibility first: this route is for a natural person who emigrated from mainland China and is moving property owned before acquiring emigrant status. Money earned after emigrating does not qualify.
  2. Assemble: written application, 移民财产对外转移申请人情况表, ID, cancelled 户籍 certificate, proof of overseas settlement authenticated by the Chinese embassy/consulate, property title and sale documents, and the PRC tax receipts.
  3. Apply once, to the SAFE branch where the 户籍 was registered. Up to CNY 500,000 is approved locally; above that it goes to SAFE headquarters.
  4. Remit in the staged pattern: at most 50% first, at most half the remainder after one year, the rest after two years (a one-off is allowed if the total is at most CNY 200,000).
  5. Note that the bank must remit directly to your own overseas account; cash withdrawal is not allowed under this route.
  6. Budget for three FX conversions and three wire fees over two years, plus two years of exchange-rate exposure.

Sources: C08 ↗ C03 ↗

L06 Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移)

One application for the whole inherited amount, then one or several remittances. Notarised inheritance and title documents.

  1. Confirm you are a foreign citizen or HK/Macau resident inheriting mainland assets.
  2. Assemble the notarised inheritance proof, the deceased's title documents, your ID and the tax receipts.
  3. Apply once to the SAFE branch where the deceased was registered; the whole inherited amount is applied for at once.
  4. Remit in one or several payments after approval; keep every approval letter.
  5. Report the receipt in the US: Form 3520 above USD 100,000, and FBAR/8938 for the account while it is open.

Sources: C08 ↗ C03 ↗

L07 Foreign individual: proceeds of selling mainland property

Bank handles the purchase and remittance on documents after the property-transfer tax is cleared. PRC IIT of 20% applies to the gain, not to the conversion.

  1. Sell the property and settle the PRC individual income tax on the gain (20% of proceeds minus original value and reasonable expenses).
  2. Obtain the tax clearance and the property-transfer registration documents.
  3. Take the sale contract, tax receipts and ID to the bank for the FX purchase and remittance.
  4. Above USD 50,000 per payment, file the 税务备案表 first.
  5. Keep the gain calculation: the US will tax the same gain, with a foreign tax credit limited by the FTC limitation.

Sources: C06 ↗ C12 ↗ C32 ↗ C03 ↗

L08 Carry USD cash out of China

Up to USD 5,000 no permit; USD 5,000-10,000 a bank 携带证; above USD 10,000 only in listed special cases with a SAFE permit. Physical loss and travel costs are excluded from the numbers.

  1. Up to USD 5,000 equivalent: no permit, but customs declaration rules still apply.
  2. USD 5,000–10,000: obtain a 携带证 from a designated FX bank before departure.
  3. Above USD 10,000: only the listed special cases can obtain a SAFE permit; assume it is not available for a normal individual.
  4. Do not structure trips to move a large balance: unlawful carrying is fined up to 20% under Art. 42 and can be treated as smuggling.
  5. Declare the cash on entry to the US if it exceeds USD 10,000 (FinCEN 105).

Sources: C11 ↗ C03 ↗

L09 Overseas ATM withdrawals on a mainland card

CNY 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year across all cards, and CNY 10,000 equivalent per card per day. Breach suspends the channel for the year and the next.

  1. Confirm the card is a mainland-issued card and that overseas cash withdrawal is enabled.
  2. Stay inside RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day and RMB 100,000 per person per calendar year across all cards.
  3. Expect the issuer fee plus any US operator fee on every withdrawal, and a worse rate than a wire.
  4. Do not lend or borrow cards to get around the cap: that is explicitly prohibited and triggers a suspension.
  5. Treat this as spending money abroad, not as a way to move a balance.

Sources: C10 ↗ C03 ↗

L10 RMB cross-border settlement, converted offshore (CNH)

The conversion happens in the offshore market, so the CNY/CNH basis is added to the cost. The offshore basis is not an official daily series; the high case uses a historical stress observation.

  1. Confirm the RMB payment itself is a lawful current-account payment (salary, services, rent) with documents.
  2. Remit RMB to your own offshore account; the onshore bank charges its RMB remittance tariff.
  3. Convert CNY to USD offshore, where the rate follows CNH rather than the onshore fix.
  4. Measure the CNH basis on the day; the model's high case uses a 1.94% historical stress observation, not today's price.
  5. Keep both legs documented for the US return; the conversion is a personal transaction, so the USD 200 rule applies to the currency gain.

Sources: C25 ↗ C03 ↗

The same six controls on every route

  1. One route, one application. Never split across relatives, banks or years to avoid a threshold; that is the 分拆 pattern SAFE fines under Art. 39 (C22).
  2. Source, not purpose. The bank's job is to verify where the RMB came from, including tax evidence (C07).
  3. Written all-in quote. Customer rate, reference rate and timestamp, spread, every fee, and net USD to the beneficiary.
  4. Keep the paper. The purchase documents are the evidence for FBAR, Form 8938 and the US basis.
  5. Report in the US whether or not money moves. FBAR above USD 10,000 aggregate; Form 8938 at the applicable threshold (C17).
  6. Never use a prohibited route. The fine is 30%–100% of the amount plus confiscation, and can be criminal (C09, C24).
