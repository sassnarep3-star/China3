The answer in one line

Converting your own CNY principal costs roughly 0.4%–1.0% through a lawful bank route, and that percentage falls as the amount rises because the wire fee is capped at CNY 260. The tax you are worried about is not a conversion tax: it is (a) PRC income tax on whatever created the money, and (b) US reporting penalties, which are fixed statutory amounts far larger than the FX cost. The expensive mistake is choosing an unlawful route: 30%–100% of the amount, plus confiscation.

7 · Method and limits

Arithmetic

CNY principal = USD amount × 6.67785
Spread cost = USD amount × spread %
Wire fee = min(max(0.001 × CNY per payment, CNY 50), CNY 260) + CNY 150, ÷ 6.6919
Full-amount guarantee = USD 25 per payment
Total = spread + wire + guarantee + documents
Effective % = total ÷ USD amount × 100

Payments: one, unless the route is capped (USD 50,000 a year; USD 10,000 a trip; RMB 100,000 a year at ATMs) or staged by the 2004 transfer rules (50% / 25% / 25%).

What this is not

  • Not a bank quote. Your bank's rate is set by the bank; the customer spread was deregulated on 2014-07-01 (C14).
  • Not a tax calculation. Tax depends on the source of the funds and your basis (table 4).
  • Not a prediction of the CNY/CNH basis: the high case uses a 2011 stress observation (C25).
