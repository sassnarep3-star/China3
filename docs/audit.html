Irregularities flagged, not silently fixed

  • Third-party statute mirrors. Two of the 27 sources are not on a .gov domain because the official servers returned errors for those sections. They are labelled third-party in the register and itemised as I05 on the flagged page.
  • One dead URL encountered during research. The 2008 gov.cn URL originally holding the FX Regulations had been renumbered; the working replacement is used and the dead one is not cited.
  • A superseded regulation. Most published commentary on Chinese crypto rules cites 银发〔2021〕237号, which was repealed on 6 February 2026. The site cites the replacement.
  • A partially amended source. The SAFE page for the 2007 implementing rules carries an amendment note covering two articles. Neither is one relied on here, but the page does not certify the list is exhaustive — itemised as I16.
