Playbook

The order of operations that keeps the penalty at zero.

Do things in this order. The order is not cosmetic. Doing step 1 after step 4 can convert a 5% penalty into a 50% one, and doing step 4 before step 3 can make the largest Chinese channel unavailable to you permanently.

  1. Characterise the money — four buckets only

    Everything downstream depends on this. Sort the balance into: (A) salary and other current-account income already taxed in China; (B) proceeds of a property sale, on which 20% PRC tax is due on the gain; (C) an inheritance; (D) foreign currency you personally exchanged into CNY and never used.

    Each bucket has exactly one designated route, and the routes do not substitute for each other. Money that does not fit any bucket is the hardest case, and that is what the fallback ladder in step 7 is for.

    S01S02S09

  2. Fix US reporting before a single dollar moves

    This is the highest-value hour you will spend. Two filings are in play: an FBAR for every year the aggregate balance exceeded USD 10,000, and Form 8938 for every year the threshold was met.

    If you missed them and the failure was not willful, the streamlined procedures were built for this. Living in the US, the price is 5% of the highest aggregate balance and you emerge with no information-return or FBAR penalties. Living abroad and meeting the non-residency test, the price is nothing.

    Do it first because the penalty is computed on the balance, not on the gain — and because moving money through an illegal Chinese channel afterwards is exactly the fact pattern that turns "non-willful" into "willful".

  3. Get your FX category in writing from the branch

    Ask, in writing, whether the account is registered as 境内个人 or 境外个人. The individual FX rules define 境外个人 as a foreign passport holder; the FX Regulations define 境内个人 to include foreigners resident in China for a year or more. Branches apply one or the other.

    If you are 境外个人, the USD 50,000 annual purchase cap is not the provision aimed at you — Art. 13 is, and it asks for source documents instead. If you are 境内个人, the cap binds and your route set narrows.

    S01S05S02

  4. Sequence the Chinese tax clearance before cancelling household registration

    This is the trap that catches people. The emigrant transfer route requires a certificate that your PRC household registration has been cancelled. But the IIT Law requires a tax settlement to be completed before that cancellation. If you cancel first, you have created the document you need while destroying the sequence you needed it in.

    Order: tax clearance → cancellation certificate → embassy-issued or authenticated proof of settlement abroad → SAFE application.

    S09S03S04

  5. Assemble the document file once, not three times

    Written application; applicant information form; business application form; passport; cancelled household registration certificate; embassy-issued or authenticated proof of foreign settlement; proof of the property rights; tax certificate or tax-payment certificate from the competent tax authority. Anything not notarised that needs to be notarised should be notarised now — the SAFE branch will not accept a promise to produce it later.

    File the application with SAFE where your pre-emigration household registration was located, not where you live now and not where the money is.

    S03S04

  6. Apply once for the whole amount, then take the staged payout

    The application must cover the entire amount you intend to move; only the remittance is staged. First tranche up to 50%, a further 50% of the remainder after one year, the balance after two years. Below RMB 200,000 in total, one payment is allowed.

    Approval is by the local SAFE branch up to RMB 500,000 and by SAFE headquarters above it. The bank must wire the money to your own account abroad — cash is expressly prohibited on this route.

    S03S04

  7. If the emigrant route is closed, take the fallback ladder

    Work down this list in order; each step only applies if the bucket fits.

    (B) Property proceeds — Art. 22 permits conversion and remittance of a non-resident's proceeds from selling China residential property with SAFE approval, after the 20% tax on the gain and the clearance certificate.
    (A) Current-account income — Art. 13 permits a 境外个人 to convert lawful current-account RMB income on production of documents including tax documents. No annual quota, transaction by transaction.
    (D) Unused exchanged cash — the original exchange memo lets you convert it back within 24 months.
    Small amounts / 境内个人 — the USD 50,000 annual facility, year after year.
    Living costs — card spending abroad is lawful for genuine purchases and does not consume the annual quota.
    Someone else's money — if a relative gifts it to you instead, that is a different question: more than USD 100,000 in a year from a foreign person triggers Form 3520 on the US side.

    S01S02S06S22

  8. Report the arrival correctly

    Principal is not income. The conversion can still produce a US consequence: the USD 200 personal-transaction exclusion for foreign-currency gain switches off entirely once the gain on that transaction exceeds USD 200. Keep the bank's exchange confirmation and the counterparty's remittance advice for every tranche — they are your cost basis evidence if the number is ever questioned.

    If PRC tax was withheld at source, claim it on Form 1116. The treaty caps China's dividend and interest rates at 10%, but the saving clause means it never reduces your US tax; only the credit does.

    S24S20S27

  9. What not to do, in one place

    Crypto and stablecoins — since 6 February 2026, RMB-to-virtual-currency conversion is expressly illegal financial activity; investments contrary to public order are void and the loss lands on you.
    Underground banks and offsetting pairs — 30%–100% fines, and prosecution as illegal business operation from RMB 5,000,000 of turnover or RMB 100,000 of gains.
    Smurfing with relatives' quotas — prohibited outright.
    False trade or service invoices — illegal arbitrage of FX, plus Chinese invoicing offences, plus a US return that cannot explain the source.
    Carrying cash — capped at USD 10,000 even with permits, 20% penalty above, and a FinCEN Form 105 obligation at USD 10,000 on arrival.
    Renouncing citizenship to escape the tax — a separate and much larger question with its own mark-to-market rule; nothing in this research supports it as a saving.

Two things this playbook cannot do for you

The household-registration certificate

Only the public security authority where the registration was held can issue it, and only you can find out whether the record still exists. If it does not, the no-ceiling route is gone and the fallback ladder is the whole answer.

The branch's classification

Only the bank can tell you which category your account is registered under. Ask for it in writing before you rely on any single route in this site.
