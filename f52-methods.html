Step by step — exactly how each route works

F01 — step by step

  1. Ask the holding branch to confirm, in writing, which personal category (境外个人/境内个人) and ID your account is recorded under (Q08). F-S05 F-S08
  2. Assemble source materials with transaction amounts for the income: contract, payslips/payment records (Q06). F-S04
  3. Obtain tax evidence: tax-payment certificate, exemption certificate, or tax-filing form; settle any unpaid IIT first (Q07). F-S04 F-S07
  4. Present ID + source materials + tax evidence at the bank and buy USD at the bank's selling rate of the day (today's BOC illustration: 6.6911). F-S04 F-S01
  5. Wire to your own U.S. account; BOC published fee 1‰ (CNY 50–260) + CNY 150 cable. Ask for the charge option and keep the quote (Q11). F-S02
  6. In the U.S.: confirm FBAR/Form 8938 history (Q12), record the §988 gain with your acquisition basis (Q14), keep every document. F-S12 F-S18

F02 — step by step

  1. Locate the original exchange slip (兑换水单); check it is within 24 months of the exchange date and note the unused amount. F-S09
  2. Present passport/ID + the slip at the bank; buy back up to the unused RMB amount (USD 500/day on ID alone if the slip is lost). F-S09
  3. Buy USD at the selling rate of the day, then wire as in F01 step 5 (or hold as FX and use F03 later). F-S01 F-S02
  4. Record the §988 position (your original acquisition rate is on the slip history) and keep the paperwork. F-S18

F03 — step by step

  1. Confirm with the bank how much of the balance is already foreign currency in an FX account in your name (Q09). F-S01
  2. Instruct the remittance to your own U.S. account with your ID; no conversion occurs, so no spread is charged. F-S02
  3. Pay the published wire fee (1‰, CNY 50–260, + CNY 150 cable at BOC) and obtain the intermediary/receiving-fee disclosure (Q11). F-S02
  4. Complete the U.S. reporting check (FBAR/Form 8938) for the account's history. F-S12

F04 — step by step

  1. Complete the sale: registered transfer contract + registration proof from the real-estate authority (operating-guide text read earlier — confirm the branch's list in writing). F-S07
  2. Pay the sale taxes and obtain the tax certificate; the remittable amount is capped at price minus those taxes. F-S07
  3. Present ID + contract + registration proof + tax certificate; buy USD and wire in one remittance (bank cost as F01). F-S01 F-S02
  4. Keep the gain computation (proceeds minus original value and reasonable costs at 20%) for both tax files. F-S07

F05 — step by step

  1. Confirm eligibility and the competent SAFE office (decedent's last hukou location) with the bank/SAFE in writing (Q08). F-S16
  2. Gather inheritance proof, property documents and the estate tax certificate (full PBOC list read in an earlier pass — get the branch's current list). F-S07
  3. Apply for SAFE approval; after approval, buy USD and wire to your own account in your country of residence (bank cost as F01; approval timing blank). F-S01 F-S02
  4. U.S. side: Form 3520 Part IV if the bequest exceeds USD 100,000 in the tax year; FBAR/8938 check. F-S16 F-S12

F06 — step by step

  1. Confirm you held mainland residence and cancelled hukou on emigration; identify the former-hukou SAFE office (Q08). F-S07
  2. Gather property/income documents and tax certificates for each asset (branch's current list in writing). F-S07
  3. Apply for SAFE emigrant-transfer approval; after approval, buy USD and wire to your own account abroad (bank cost as F01; timing blank). F-S01 F-S02
  4. U.S. side: FBAR/8938 history and §988 basis records (Q12/Q14). F-S12 F-S18

F07 — step by step

  1. Confirm eligibility: Chinese ID, or Foreign Permanent Resident ID — a U.S. passport alone does not qualify for purchases (Q02). F-S05
  2. Each calendar year, present ID at the bank and buy up to USD 50,000 equivalent; no source documents inside the amount. F-S06
  3. Wire that year's purchase (wire fee repeats yearly) or hold as FX and consolidate under F03 — ask the bank which it allows. F-S02
  4. Repeat for the number of years on the Costs page (8 years at USD 400,000). Never borrow another person's amount (X01). F-S20 F-S08

F08 — step by step

  1. Ask your bank, in writing: does it offer personal cross-border RMB remittance for your income type, with what documents and what fee? (All blank today.) F-S04
  2. Ask the receiving U.S. bank, in writing: will it accept an RMB remittance, at what conversion rate and fee? (Blank today.) F-S02
  3. Only proceed with both written quotes in hand; convert offshore and record the achieved rate against today's BOC board. F-S01
  4. Complete the same U.S. reporting/§988 record-keeping as F01. F-S12 F-S18

F09 — step by step

  1. Confirm genuine distributable profit: audited company accounts and a profit-distribution resolution (Q17). F-S07
  2. Confirm the applicable rate with the tax office: statutory 20%, or the 10% treaty ceiling with U.S.-residency/beneficial-ownership proof (procedure unverified). F-S07 F-S13
  3. Complete the outbound-payment tax filing, pay the tax, and obtain the tax evidence the bank requires. F-S07
  4. Buy USD with the after-tax amount and wire (spread + wire on the net — exactly how the Costs page prices F09). F-S01 F-S02
  5. U.S. side: report the dividend, and ask your adviser about foreign-tax-credit treatment of the Chinese tax (Q15). F-S18

F10 — step by step

  1. Check your per-person yearly overseas-withdrawal usage across all cards (cap CNY 100,000 equivalent; CNY 10,000 per card per day). F-S10
  2. Abroad, withdraw on the UnionPay card; BOC charges CNY 12 + 1% (max CNY 112) per withdrawal from 2026-11-11. The conversion rate applied and any ATM-owner fee are unpublished — record what you actually got. F-S03 F-S01
  3. Deposit the cash in the U.S. promptly; declare over USD 10,000 on entry (FinCEN Form 105). Repeat within caps only — never borrow cards (X06). F-S10

F11 — step by step

  1. First buy the USD lawfully under F01, F02 or F07 — carrying cash never replaces the purchase documents. F-S04 F-S09 F-S06
  2. Per trip, carry at most USD 5,000 equivalent without a permit (USD 5,000–10,000: obtain the bank carrying permit first; over USD 10,000: do not attempt). F-S11
  3. Declare amounts over USD 10,000 when entering the U.S. (FinCEN Form 105; family/group combined). F-S11
  4. Deposit on arrival and keep purchase + permit + declaration records together. F-S12

Conversion penalty, decomposed
