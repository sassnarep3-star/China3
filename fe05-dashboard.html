Start with the source of your money. A U.S. passport alone does not establish a $50,000 annual FX purchase entitlement. For a foreign individual’s lawful current-account income, SAFE requires ID and source evidence including tax documentation. SAFE guidance

Best first move

Ask your bank to classify your account and identify the correct route, then collect evidence and obtain an all-in quote. Sale proceeds, inherited property and emigrant property have different procedures; do not borrow other people’s quotas or use informal exchange.

Exchange rate spread and bank fees are costs, not necessarily penalties. Tax depends on income, history and U.S. currency basis; the transfer amount alone cannot determine it.
