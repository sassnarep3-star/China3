StepWhat to doSource
1Settle which rule lets you convert, using the route on the main dashboard that matches where the money came from. Banks ask for supporting documents: Agricultural Bank's page says so for amounts above its daily threshold. See Methods and Steps.G03 ABC
2Ask the branch for its selling rate for your amount on the day, in writing. The published board is a reference only; the transaction rate is what you get.G02 BOC G06 CMB
3Buy the dollars into your own foreign-currency account (现汇, not 现钞). A cash-to-wire conversion fee applies only if the account holds banknotes.G05 CMB
