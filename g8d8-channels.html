Channels other than a bank wire

Two providers publish terms for sending money out of mainland China. Wise sends a foreigner's taxed salary to an account in the same name, up to 300,000 CNY per transfer. Western Union pays out cash abroad through partner banks. Neither price could be read, so every cost cell is blank.

Steps

ChannelStepWhat to doSource
Wise, sending from CNY1Confirm you qualify: not a mainland Chinese national, you work or worked in China, and you have a tax record from the Chinese tax authority showing tax paid in the last 3 years.G18 Wise
Wise, sending from CNY2Open a personal Wise account and start a transfer from CNY. For the first transfer Wise verifies you, which can take 1 to 2 working days, and asks for the tax record.G18 Wise
Wise, sending from CNY3Name the recipient: it must be another account you own, either a bank account outside China or your Wise account.G18 Wise
Wise, sending from CNY4Pay by bank transfer from a Chinese bank account in your own name (up to 300,000 CNY per transfer) or from Alipay (up to 50,000 CNY per transfer). Money sent from an account in another name is refunded.G18 Wise
Wise, sending from CNY5Repeat until you reach your overall limit. To raise it, upload further tax records when Wise prompts you.G18 Wise
Western Union at a partner bank1Go to a partner bank branch (Bank of China, China Everbright Bank, Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, Postal Savings Bank of China, or one of the smaller partner banks) with your passport. Ask first whether U.S. passport holders and the United States are accepted at that branch.G19 Western Union
Western Union at a partner bank2Fill out the transfer details with the receiver's complete information and hand over the money and your ID. The bank may require you to hold an account with it.G19 Western Union
Western Union at a partner bank3Pay the transfer fee and keep the receipt with the Money Transfer Control Number (MTCN).G19 Western Union
Western Union at a partner bank4Give the MTCN only to your receiver, who collects the cash at a Western Union agent location abroad.G19 Western Union

Not channels

Underground banks, private swaps and splitting a transfer across other people's quotas are prohibited. The fines SAFE has published for them are on the Enforcement page.
