What SAFE has fined

In four published bulletins, 24 individuals who bought or sold foreign exchange through an underground bank were fined a median of ¥0.824 for every dollar moved, about 12.3% at today's rate. The lawful bank cost on $400,000 is 0.23%. Every underground-bank case also went on the central bank's credit record.

Fine per dollar moved

The bulletins print the amount in U.S. dollars and the fine in RMB. The percentage converts at 6.6911 RMB per dollar as a yardstick F15. The legal ceiling for ordinary cases is 30% of the amount.
