Who pays the fees: the three charge options

As published by China Merchants Bank. Other banks' wording was not read.

OptionHow it worksEffect on costSource
SHA (shared)The sender pays the Chinese bank's fees; they are debited from the sender's RMB account.Sending-bank fee and cable only. Banks along the route and the receiving bank may still deduct from the amount. F10G05 CMB
BEN (receiver pays)The Chinese bank's fees are converted at the day's rate and taken out of the amount sent. Fee waivers for premium customers no longer apply.The amount that arrives is smaller by the sending bank's fees. F10G05 CMB
