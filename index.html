Tax and penalties depend on the money’s origin and how you move it.

A U.S. citizen with a mainland bank balance asks the same question every time: what is the cheapest lawful route from CNY to USD, and where does the “tax penalty” actually come from? This page answers it in the order that matters: the cost of the transfer first, the reporting risk second, the alternatives last.

Read this with the safety review. The independent review at a15-review.html (10 Nov 2026) raises four cautions this page also carries: the USD 50,000 convenience amount is not automatic for a foreign passport holder; a Chinese tax certificate is evidence, not a new tax; whether converting your CNY is “personal” for §988 purposes is fact-specific and unknown here; and every figure below is a China-side cost that still needs a written bank quote. Automated source-link checks do not substitute for that quote.

The short answer

  1. Converting your own after-tax principal costs about 0.5342% at $10,000 and 0.2253% at $400,000 — $53.42 and $901.30 on today's Bank of China board. That is bank spread plus the 1‰ wire fee and its cable charge. There is no Chinese “conversion tax” in any instrument checked here.
  2. The expensive number is a reporting penalty, not an exchange rate. A willful FBAR violation is capped at the greater of USD 165,353 or 50% of the balance — USD 200,000 on a $400,000 account 31 USC 5321 — 31 U.S.C. § 5321(a)(5)(C). Fix U.S. reporting before optimising anything else.
  3. Choose the route by the documents you can produce, not by the fee table. A documented current-account income remittance and an emigrant property transfer cost the same at the bank; both differ from the USD 50,000 convenience amount, which is a per-person, per-year processing allowance SAFE — 2022年外汇业务政策释疑（八） — not a legal ceiling and not a tax.

Wire fee
1‰, cap ¥260

Plus a cable fee of CNY 150 outside Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan. The CNY 260 cap bites at about $38,862 of transfer, so the fee stops growing there.

BOC tariff — 中国银行电子银行个人服务转账汇款费率表
FBAR trigger
> $10,000

Aggregate value of foreign accounts exceeds $10,000 at any time in the calendar year. It is a reporting trigger, not a tax — the penalty is what costs money.

FinCEN — Report Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts
Willful FBAR ceiling
50% of balance

Greater of the inflation-adjusted USD 165,353 or 50% of the account balance. On $400,000 that ceiling is $200,000 — about 222× the cost of moving the money lawfully.

31 USC 5321 — 31 U.S.C. § 5321(a)(5)(C)
U.S. currency-gain rule
$200

A personal transaction in foreign currency recognises no §988 gain unless the gain exceeds $200. Whether your conversion is a “personal transaction”, and the CNY cost basis, are fact-specific — this is not a finding that your conversion is untaxable.

The emigrant property transfer is no longer remitted in stages. PBC Order [2004] No. 16 art. 6 says an emigrant applies once for the whole estate and receives it in stages: half, then half the remainder after a year, then the rest after two years (2004 text — 中国人民银行公告[2004]第16号). The later SAFE circular 汇发〔2014〕2号 §6(2) expressly cancels that requirement — 取消移民财产转移分次汇出的要求 … 可一次或分次汇出 (2014 circular — （已修改）国家外汇管理局关于进一步改进和调整资本项目外汇管理政策的通知 汇发〔2014〕2号) — and §6(1) of the same circular cancels the over-RMB-500,000 head-office filing.

The cost model on cost-tables.html prices route M03 as three separate FX purchases, which is how it reaches $1,025.39 at $400,000. On the law as it now reads, that route costs the same as any other single documented wire — $901.30 at today's board on $400,000, before the optional $25 OUR charge. The M03 row has been left as published (it is a faithful price of the 2004 text) and is listed with this correction on Verify 2026 and in COORDINATION.md.

What is still blank, and why

Nothing below is estimated or filled with an industry average. Each item stays blank and is listed on the Flagged for Review page with the official source a bank visit should resolve it against.

  • Other banks' spreads. The 0.2100% here is one BOC board reading at one timestamp. ICBC, CMB and the rest publish their own; so does your branch on the day.
  • Receiving and correspondent fees. A U.S. bank's incoming-wire fee and any intermediary deduction are blank. BOC's USD 25 (or USD 15 via its New York branch) full-amount-received charge is published only in its trade-finance tariff and is not shown as included here.
  • The separate questions raised by the safety review — whether the convenience amount applies to you at all, what your CNY cost basis is for §988, and whether late FBAR years need a corrective procedure — are fact-specific and left open.
  • The tax base on a property sale. The 20% rate is verified; the deductible-cost and valuation rules that produce the base are transaction-specific.
  • Eligibility. Whether your balance qualifies as documented current-account income, emigrant property or something else is a bank/SAFE determination on documents this research does not have.
  • Current CNH basis and card-network rates. No official retail quotation exists to price them.
