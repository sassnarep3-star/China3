Executive summary

There is no tax for simply moving your own money out of China

Converting your own CNY to USD and sending it abroad is not a taxable event. The real issues are China's annual foreign-exchange quota ($50,000 per person per year), bank fees and FX spreads, and U.S. reporting duties (FBAR / Form 8938). Non-compliance — not the transfer itself — is where the penalties live.

Best route by source of funds: post-tax China salary → bank salary repatriation (no annual cap, needs contract/work permit/tax proof). Otherwise → bank FX purchase + wire up to $50,000/year; above $50,000 with supporting documentation (emigration, medical, tuition, family maintenance, inheritance) via a SAFE-authorized bank.

Avoid: underground banking ("huayi"), crypto P2P, "smurfing" (splitting quotas through friends/family), falsified trade invoices, undeclared cash above $10,000. These carry fines, multi-year FX bans, frozen accounts, and possible criminal liability.
