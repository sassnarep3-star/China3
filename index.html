What is the “considerable tax”?

1 · FX spread

About 0.2100% one way against BOC’s own bid/ask midpoint at the 2026-11-10 07:32:39 board timestamp. It is a dated benchmark, not your guaranteed rate.

BOC board

2 · Wire charges

Personal cross-border TT: 1‰, ¥50 minimum / ¥260 maximum + ¥150 cable. Agreements, discounts and local differences can apply.

BOC personal tariff

3 · Source-income tax

Tax recordal for listed single payments above $50,000 is a documentary gate, not automatically a second tax. Unpaid underlying tax depends on the income.

STA / SAFE rule

4 · U.S. currency gain

Moving principal is not automatically tax-free. For a qualifying personal transaction, Pub. 525 says FX gain over $200 is capital gain; §212/business facts require separate §988 analysis.

IRS Pub. 525 · §988

Do this in order

  1. Get the bank’s classification and written checklist. A U.S. passport alone does not prove how the account is registered.
  2. Match authentic records to the balance. Salary, property sale, inheritance, company profit, gift and original FX conversion use different evidence.
  3. Resolve tax evidence and required recordal/SAFE review. Never invent a purpose or backfill documents.
  4. Obtain a live itemized quote. Rate/timestamp, spread, wire, cable, charge-bearer option, intermediary and receiving fees, expected net USD.
  5. Use one lawful route and retain the full trail. Do not split among relatives or disguise the transaction.
  6. Review U.S. filings. Worldwide income, §988 basis/holding facts, FBAR, Form 8938 and foreign-gift reporting are separate from China’s bank approval.
