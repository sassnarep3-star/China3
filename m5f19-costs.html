The convention, stated once. To buy USD X you spend CNY X × ask. Against a hypothetical zero-spread midpoint you overspend CNY X × (ask − mid). That overspend is an opportunity cost borne in the CNY you pay. It is not a second deduction from the USD you receive: buying USD X at the ask delivers USD X. Earlier layers of this repository deducted both, which is the double-count that sessions a502 and a058 flagged. Two denominators are reported and never mixed: (ask−mid)/ask = 0.209421% and (ask−mid)/mid = 0.209860%.

Primary schedule — one wire, spread against the ask

USD 10,000 to USD 400,000 in USD 10,000 increments. This is the text-supported case: Guidelines Art. 65(1) states no amount threshold for an overseas individual remitting FX from an FX account, and Art. 64(1) permits a domestic individual to exceed USD 50,000 in a day on documents. Nothing in either article requires staging across years.

Reading the table

  1. CNY spent is what leaves your RMB account: transfer × ask. It is not a cost, it is the consideration.
  2. Spread is the opportunity cost of paying the ask rather than a hypothetical midpoint. It is what the bank earns.
  3. Wire fee is 1‰ of the remittance amount with a CNY 50 floor and a CNY 260 ceiling. The ceiling binds above about USD 38,892 of transfer, which is why the all-in percentage falls as the amount grows.
  4. Telecom is a flat CNY 150 per transaction outside Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. It is the only purely fixed element, so it is what staging multiplies.
  5. Total is the sum of the three. It excludes intermediary and beneficiary-bank charges, which no official source reviewed publishes, and it excludes any tax on the underlying income.
These are models, not quotes. BOC’s own declaration on the board page says the table is for reference only, that the executable rate is the one in its online banking, mobile banking, smart counter or branch counter, and that BOC accepts no responsibility for results from using the table. Tariff note 1 lets agreements, promotions and local charging differences override the published charges.
