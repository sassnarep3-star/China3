G01 irregularity The repository applies a DOMESTIC-INDIVIDUAL rule to a U.S. citizen. Multiple merged pages state…

Finding. The repository applies a DOMESTIC-INDIVIDUAL rule to a U.S. citizen. Multiple merged pages state that a personal wire needs no approval 'up to USD 50,000/day on ID' and cite SAFE Tianjin 2024-02-29. That page's own question is 境内个人外汇汇出境外用于经常项目支出 — a DOMESTIC individual remitting FX abroad — and its answer quotes Guidelines Art. 64. The instrument governing a passport-holder is Art. 65(1), which this session fetched and which states NO amount threshold for FX remitted from an FX account, and Art. 59 for the purchase leg. Sessions a502 (merged PR #36) and a058 (open) flagged the same defect; it is still unfixed on main at 9bc2cde.

Recommended action. Correct the source data AND the generator, not only the HTML: replace the Art. 64 citation with Art. 59 + Art. 65(1) for an overseas individual, and delete 'ID only up to USD 50,000/day' as a general statement.

Finding. Guidelines Art. 65 — the article that actually governs an overseas individual remitting FX out of an FX account — appears NOWHERE in the repository. A grep across all .md, .html and .csv files for '第六十五条', 'Article 65' and 'art. 65' returns zero matches, while Art. 64 is cited repeatedly. G03 irregularity The '8 wires over 8 years' scenario has no basis in the text of Art. 64 or Art. 65. Art. 64(1) s…

Finding. The '8 wires over 8 years' scenario has no basis in the text of Art. 64 or Art. 65. Art. 64(1) sets a USD 50,000 PER DAY identity-document threshold for a domestic individual — above it, documents are required, not a prohibition and not a multi-year wait. Art. 65(1) sets no threshold at all for an overseas individual. SAFE Ningxia 2022 states in terms that there is no amount limit on genuine, lawful current-account purchase and settlement. The scenario is still legitimate AS A SCENARIO if a bank or SAFE branch requires staging, but it must not be presented as what the regulation requires.

Recommended action. Relabel the 8-wire figure as a conditional staging scenario, not as a regulatory requirement, and show the single-wire figure as the text-supported case.

G05 irregularity 'No separate China exit tax was identified' is true as far as it goes but understates a real gat…

Finding. 'No separate China exit tax was identified' is true as far as it goes but understates a real gate. IIT Law Art. 13 para 4 requires a taxpayer who cancels a Chinese hukou because of emigrating abroad to complete a TAX CLEARANCE BEFORE the cancellation, and Art. 10(5) makes the cancellation a filing trigger. Because 汇发〔2014〕2号 §6 routes the emigrant property transfer through the ORIGINAL hukou location, that clearance gates the entire emigrant route. The repository does record the sequencing in data/taxes.json and docs/playbook.html, but the headline on index.html does not carry it.

Recommended action. Carry the clearance-before-cancellation gate into any headline treatment of the emigrant route, not only into the playbook subpage.

G06 irregularity The universal claim 'there is no PRC tax on converting your own CNY to USD' outruns its sources.…

Finding. The universal claim 'there is no PRC tax on converting your own CNY to USD' outruns its sources. What IIT Law Art. 2 actually establishes is that currency conversion is not one of the nine CLOSED CATEGORIES of taxable personal income. That is not the same as a universal exemption: tax depends on how the money was earned, on residence status under Art. 1, and on whether earlier PRC tax was paid and evidenced. Sessions a502 and a058 both flagged this; it remains on merged pages.

Recommended action. Restate as: no instrument reviewed taxes the act of exchange; the nine categories in Art. 2 are closed and do not include it; tax on the underlying income is a separate question that must be settled on the facts.

G08 limitation The classification of the user is UNKNOWABLE from the brief, and it changes the answer. Order 53…

Finding. The classification of the user is UNKNOWABLE from the brief, and it changes the answer. Order 532 Art. 52(2) counts a foreigner continuously resident in China for a full year as a DOMESTIC individual; PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 37(2) counts any passport-holding foreign citizen as an OVERSEAS individual. PBOC Art. 28 makes the bank's record at account opening operative. The brief supplies no account documents, no residence history, no source-of-funds history, no amount and no tax filings.

Recommended action. Ask the account-opening bank which category the account carries before choosing a route. Every conclusion in this edition is conditional on that answer.

G13 observation 31 CFR 1010.821(b) states that the adjusted maxima do NOT limit the total penalty where a penalt…

Finding. 31 CFR 1010.821(b) states that the adjusted maxima do NOT limit the total penalty where a penalty may be imposed for each day a violation continues. Combined with §5321(b)(1)'s six-year assessment window, §5321(d)'s confirmation that civil and criminal penalties are not exclusive, and §5321(f)'s repeat-violator uplift of up to three times the profit gained or twice the maximum penalty, the 'USD 200,000 on a USD 400,000 account' figure on the repository's landing page is a PER-VIOLATION maximum, not a ceiling on total exposure.

Recommended action. Present the willful figure as per-violation and note the stacking provisions alongside it.
