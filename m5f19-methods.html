Lawful routes

M01 Bank FX purchase + outward wire — overseas individual with documented current-account RMB income Lawful

Who it is for. 境外个人 (a US citizen classified on passport)

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2020〕14号 Art. 59 (purchase) + Art. 65(1) (remittance); PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 13

Documents required. Valid ID + amount-bearing source-of-funds documents for the purchase, INCLUDING a tax certificate

Ceiling stated in the text. No ceiling stated in Art. 59 or Art. 65(1)

Pros.

The only route in the reviewed instruments that both converts and remits on documents rather than on a quota; no annual cap appears in the text; a single wire is permitted because Art. 65(1) states no amount threshold; the tax certificate you need is evidence of tax already paid, not a new conversion tax

Cons and limits.

The bank decides whether your documents are sufficient and may refuse under Art. 68; the tax certificate must exist, so unpaid PRC tax on the underlying income blocks the route; branch practice varies because much of the guidance is branch-level Q&A

Source: www.safe.gov.cn ↗

第十三条　境外个人在境内取得的经常项目项下合法人民币收入，可以凭本人有效身份证件及相关证明材料在银行办理购汇及汇出。

Article 13: An overseas individual's lawful current-account RMB income obtained onshore may be purchased into FX and remitted out at a bank, on the person's valid identity document plus relevant proof materials. No amount ceiling appears in the article.

F10 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 59

第五十九条　境外个人在境内取得的经常项目合法人民币收入，凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的购汇资金来源材料（含税务凭证）在银行办理购汇。持有外国人永久居留身份证的境外个人适用购汇年度便利化额度。

Article 59: An overseas individual's lawful current-account RMB income obtained onshore is purchased into FX at a bank on the person's valid identity document plus amount-bearing source-of-funds documents for the purchase (INCLUDING A TAX CERTIFICATE). An overseas individual holding a Foreign Permanent Residence Identity Card is subject to the annual convenience amount for FX purchase.

F11 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 65

第六十五条　境外个人经常项目外汇汇出境外，按下列规定在银行办理：（一）外汇账户内外汇汇出，凭本人有效身份证件办理；（二）持外币现钞汇出，当日累计等值1万美元以下（含）的，凭本人有效身份证件办理；超过上述金额的，凭本人有效身份证件、经海关签章的《海关申报单》或原存款银行外币现钞提取单据办理。

Article 65: An overseas individual remitting current-account FX abroad does so at the bank as follows — (1) remittance of FX from within an FX account: on the person's valid identity document (NO AMOUNT THRESHOLD IS STATED); (2) remittance of foreign cash: up to USD 10,000 equivalent cumulative per day on the identity document, above that also a customs-stamped declaration form or the original deposit bank's foreign-cash withdrawal slip.

F14 · 2022年外汇业务政策释疑（八） · answer

目前，我国对于真实、合法的经常项目和已开放的资本项目下购汇和结汇都没有金额限制。……年度便利化额度不是个人每年购汇或者结汇的限额，只是超过等值5万美元的购汇或结汇不能仅凭本人有效身份证件办理。

SAFE Ningxia: at present the PRC imposes NO AMOUNT LIMIT on FX purchase and settlement under genuine, lawful current-account transactions or under already-opened capital-account transactions. The annual convenience amount is not a limit on how much an individual may purchase or settle per year; it means only that a purchase or settlement above USD 50,000 equivalent cannot be handled on the person's valid identity document alone.

F17 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 68

第六十八条　银行办理个人经常项目外汇业务，应对交易单证的真实性及其与外汇收支的一致性进行合理审核。……银行有权对违反外汇管理规定、真实性存疑的交易予以拒绝。对拒绝办理的业务，银行应向个人准确说明拒绝原因及申诉渠道。

Article 68: In handling personal current-account FX business the bank must reasonably review the authenticity of the transaction documents and their consistency with the FX receipt/payment. The bank has the right to refuse a transaction that violates FX rules or whose authenticity is doubtful, and must accurately explain the reason for refusal and the appeal channel.

M02 Bank FX purchase + outward wire — domestic individual above the convenience amount, on purpose documents Lawful

Who it is for. 境内个人 (a Chinese citizen, or a foreigner continuously resident 1 year on the Order 532 reading)

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2020〕14号 Arts. 54, 56, 57, 64(1)

Documents required. Valid ID + amount-bearing documents on the PURPOSE of the purchase funds + a completed 个人购汇申请书; above USD 50,000/day on the remittance leg, amount-bearing materials

Ceiling stated in the text. USD 50,000 per person per year is a DOCUMENTATION threshold, not a cap (SAFE Ningxia 2022)

Pros.

Works for any amount provided the documents exist; the purchase above the convenience amount expressly does not occupy the quota; e-banking and counter are both permitted under Art. 55

Cons and limits.

Art. 57 makes you personally and legally responsible for the accuracy of the 购汇申请书; Art. 64(1) requires the purchase purpose and the remittance purpose to be consistent, and the bank reviews that; Art. 62 makes splitting a watch-list offence

Source: www.safe.gov.cn ↗

第六十四条　境内个人外汇汇出境外用于经常项目支出，按下列规定在银行办理：（一）外汇账户内外汇汇出境外当日累计等值5万美元以下（含）的，凭本人有效身份证件办理；超过上述金额的，凭本人有效身份证件、有交易额的相关材料办理。境内个人办理外汇汇出业务时，应配合银行购汇用途与付汇用途一致性审核；（二）持外币现钞汇出当日累计等值1万美元以下（含）的，凭本人有效身份证件在银行办理；超过上述金额的，凭本人有效身份证件、经海关签章的《海关申报单》或本人原存款银行外币现钞提取单据、有交易额的相关材料办理。

Article 64: A domestic individual remitting FX abroad for current-account expenditure — (1) FX from within an FX account: up to USD 50,000 equivalent cumulative PER DAY on the identity document; above that, identity document plus amount-bearing relevant materials. The person must cooperate with the bank's review that the FX purchase purpose and the remittance purpose are consistent; (2) foreign cash: up to USD 10,000 equivalent cumulative per day on the identity document, above that also customs declaration or original cash withdrawal slip plus amount-bearing materials.

F13 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 54

第五十四条　个人经常项目外汇业务应具有真实、合法的交易背景。个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度便利化额度管理，便利化额度分别为每人每年等值5万美元。

Article 54: Personal current-account FX business must have a genuine and lawful transaction background. FX settlement by individuals and FX purchase by DOMESTIC INDIVIDUALS are managed under an annual convenience amount; the convenience amounts are respectively USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year.

F15 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 56

第五十六条　境内个人凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的结汇资金来源材料，在银行办理不占用年度便利化额度的经常项目结汇。境内个人凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的购汇资金用途材料，在银行办理不占用年度便利化额度的经常项目购汇。

Article 56: A domestic individual may, on the identity document plus amount-bearing documents on the SOURCE of settlement funds, carry out current-account FX settlement that does not occupy the annual convenience amount; and on the identity document plus amount-bearing documents on the PURPOSE of the purchase funds, carry out current-account FX purchase that does not occupy the annual convenience amount.

F16 · Current-Account FX Business Guidelines (2020), 汇发〔2020〕14号 · Art. 62

第六十二条　个人不得以分拆等方式规避便利化额度管理及真实性管理。外汇局对规避管理的个人实行“关注名单”管理。……（三）“关注名单”内个人的关注期限为列入“关注名单”的当年及之后连续2年。在关注期限内，“关注名单”内个人办理个人结售汇业务，应凭本人有效身份证件、有交易额的相关材料在银行办理。

Article 62: Individuals shall not evade convenience-amount management or authenticity management by splitting or other means. SAFE applies 'watch list' management to persons who evade — (1) a person who LENDS their own convenience amount to help others evade receives a risk-warning letter through the bank, and on repetition is placed on the watch list; (2) a person who BORROWS another's convenience amount or otherwise evades is placed on the watch list; (3) the watch period is the year of listing plus the following two consecutive years, during which that person's settlement/purchase requires identity document plus amount-bearing materials and strict bank authenticity review.

M03 Remit foreign currency you ALREADY hold in an FX account — no purchase, no spread Lawful

Who it is for. 境外个人: Art. 65(1), ID only. 境内个人: Art. 64(1), ID up to USD 50,000/day, ID + documents above

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2020〕14号 Arts. 64(1), 65(1); PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Arts. 13, 14

Documents required. Valid ID (overseas individual). Domestic individual above USD 50,000/day also needs amount-bearing materials

Ceiling stated in the text. Art. 65(1) states NO amount threshold for an overseas individual

Pros.

Cheapest route that exists: with no purchase there is no bid/ask spread at all, so the only published cost is the wire fee and the telecom charge; an overseas individual needs nothing but ID

Cons and limits.

Only available if the balance is already in foreign currency; a CNY balance cannot use it; the remittance must still be current-account in character

Source: www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗

M04 Annual convenience amount — FX purchase on identity document alone Lawful within its own scope

Who it is for. 境内个人 for purchase; 境外个人 ONLY if holding a Foreign Permanent Residence Identity Card. All individuals for SETTLEMENT (USD->CNY)

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2020〕14号 Arts. 54, 55, 59 para 2; PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Arts. 9, 40

Documents required. Valid ID only, inside the amount

Ceiling stated in the text. USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year, expressly NOT a limit on the total you may transact

Pros.

The simplest paperwork of any route: counter or e-banking on ID alone; SAFE Ningxia states in terms that it is not an annual ceiling

Cons and limits.

A US citizen without a Chinese permanent-residence ID does not get it for PURCHASE under Art. 59 para 2 — this is the single most commonly mis-stated point in the repository's earlier layers; above the amount you are back to producing documents anyway

M05 Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) Lawful, approval required

Who it is for. A person emigrating who is cancelling a Chinese household registration

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2014〕2号 §6(1),(2),(4); PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 26

Documents required. Application to the SAFE branch at the ORIGINAL hukou location; property-right documents (notarisation abolished in 2014); the household-registration cancellation certificate

Ceiling stated in the text. No amount ceiling stated; the RMB 500,000 head-office filing requirement was abolished in 2014

Pros.

Since 2014 a SINGLE lump-sum remittance is permitted — the earlier installment requirement was abolished; the head-office filing above RMB 500,000 was abolished; notarisation of property documents and of agency agreements was abolished

Cons and limits.

Unavailable to a US citizen who never held a Chinese hukou; gated by the IIT Law Art. 13 para 4 tax clearance, which must be completed BEFORE the hukou is cancelled; approval is discretionary and evidence-heavy

Source: www.safe.gov.cn ↗

M06 Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移) Lawful, approval required

Who it is for. An heir of a decedent who held a Chinese household registration

Governing instrument. 汇发〔2014〕2号 §6(1),(3)

Documents required. Application to the SAFE branch at the decedent's PRE-DEATH hukou location; since 2014 inheritances from different decedents may be combined in one application

Ceiling stated in the text. No amount ceiling stated

Pros.

Combined applications permitted since 2014; one lump sum or installments at the applicant's choice within the approved amount

Cons and limits.

Requires a Chinese decedent with a hukou; approval is discretionary; the underlying estate must be documented

Article 62: Individuals shall not evade convenience-amount management or authenticity management by splitting or other means. SAFE applies 'watch list' management to persons who evade — (1) a person who LENDS their own convenience amount to help others evade receives a risk-warning letter through the bank, and on repetition is placed on the watch list; (2) a person who BORROWS another's convenience amount or otherwise evades is placed on the watch list; (3) the watch period is the year of listing plus the following two consecutive years, during which that person's settlement/purchase requires identity document plus amount-bearing materials and strict bank authenticity review.

F50 · 31 U.S.C. §5321 · §5321(a)(4)

(4) Structured Transaction Violation.— (A) Penalty authorized.—The Secretary of the Treasury may impose a civil money penalty on any person who violates any provision of section 5324. (B) Maximum amount limitation.—The amount of any civil money penalty imposed under subparagraph (A) shall not exceed the amount of the coins and currency (or such other monetary instruments as the Secretary may prescribe) involved in the transaction with respect to which such penalty is imposed.

For a violation of 31 U.S.C. §5324 (structuring transactions to evade reporting requirements) the civil money penalty may not exceed the AMOUNT OF THE CURRENCY INVOLVED IN THE TRANSACTION. Structuring is therefore penalised up to 100% of the amount structured, on the U.S. side, independently of the PRC anti-splitting rules.
