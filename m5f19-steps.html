M01 Bank FX purchase + outward wire — overseas individual with documented current-account RMB income 8 steps
  1. Step 1. Establish, in writing from the account-opening bank, which subject category your account carries in the personal FX monitoring system — 境内个人 or 境外个人. PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 28 makes the identity documents you supplied at opening decisive, and the two definitions in the reviewed instruments conflict.
    www.gov.cn ↗
  2. Step 2. Assemble the amount-bearing source-of-funds documents for the RMB you intend to convert: the employment or service contract, the payment records showing the money actually arrived, and the PRC tax certificate. Guidelines Art. 59 requires the tax certificate expressly (含税务凭证).
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  3. Step 3. Confirm the amount is lawful CURRENT-ACCOUNT income. Art. 59 reaches 经常项目合法人民币收入 only. If the money is savings, a capital gain or something else, stop — a different route applies (see C6-C8).
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  4. Step 4. Ask the bank for a WRITTEN quotation for the FX purchase and the outward wire, and for its intermediary and beneficiary charge options. The published board at S01 is expressly 仅供参考 and BOC disclaims responsibility for results based on it; the tariff note 1 at S02 allows agreements, promotions and local differences to override the published charges. Every cost figure in this edition is a model built on those two published pages, not a quote.
    www.boc.cn ↗
  5. Step 5. Submit the purchase application. Do not split the amount across days, people or accounts: Guidelines Art. 62 forbids evasion by splitting and puts you on a watch list for the year plus two more, and 31 U.S.C. §5321(a)(4) permits a U.S. structuring penalty up to the full amount involved.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  6. Step 6. Remit the purchased FX. As an overseas individual, Guidelines Art. 65(1) requires only your valid identity document for a remittance out of an FX account, and states no amount threshold — so one wire is permitted and the fixed RMB 150 telecom charge is paid once rather than eight times.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  7. Step 7. Keep the bank's refusal or approval record. Art. 68 obliges a bank that refuses a transaction to explain the reason and the appeal channel accurately; that record is your evidence if you need to escalate to the local SAFE branch.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  8. Step 8. Before the calendar year ends, check the U.S. side: if the aggregate value of your foreign financial accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at ANY time in the year, FinCEN Form 114 is due 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October, filed electronically through the BSA E-Filing System and not with your tax return. Records are kept five years.
    www.irs.gov ↗
M03 Remit foreign currency you ALREADY hold in an FX account — no purchase, no spread 4 steps
  1. Step 1. Confirm the balance is already denominated in USD or another foreign currency inside an FX account. If it is in CNY this route does not apply and you are back to M01 or M02.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  2. Step 2. If you are an overseas individual, present your valid identity document and instruct the outward remittance. Guidelines Art. 65(1) states no amount threshold for FX remitted from within an FX account, and requires nothing beyond the ID.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  3. Step 3. If you are a domestic individual, Guidelines Art. 64(1) applies instead: up to USD 50,000 equivalent cumulative PER DAY on ID alone; above that, ID plus amount-bearing relevant materials, and you must cooperate with the bank's consistency review of the purchase purpose against the remittance purpose.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  4. Step 4. Choose ONE wire rather than several. With no purchase there is no spread, so the only published costs are the 1 permille wire fee (floor RMB 50, ceiling RMB 260) and the RMB 150 telecom charge — both of which are per transaction, so splitting multiplies the fixed element for no benefit.
    www.boc.cn ↗
M05 Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) 5 steps
  1. Step 1. Complete the PRC TAX CLEARANCE FIRST. IIT Law Art. 13 para 4 requires a taxpayer who cancels Chinese household registration because of emigrating abroad to complete the tax clearance BEFORE the cancellation, and Art. 10(5) makes the cancellation itself a filing trigger. This ordering is the trap: step 2 cannot happen before step 1.
    www.chinatax.gov.cn ↗
  2. Step 2. Cancel the Chinese household registration and obtain the cancellation certificate.
    www.chinatax.gov.cn ↗
  3. Step 3. Apply to the SAFE branch at your ORIGINAL household-registration location — 汇发〔2014〕2号 §6(1) assigns approval there, not to the branch where the money now sits.
    www.safe.gov.cn ↗
  4. Step 4. Submit the property-right documents. §6(4) abolished the requirement to notarise them, and also abolished notarisation of the agency agreement and the agent's identity proof.
    www.safe.gov.cn ↗
  5. Step 5. Once approved, instruct the bank. §6(2) abolished the installment requirement, so within the approved amount the bank may remit in ONE SUM; the RMB 500,000 head-office filing requirement was also abolished by §6(1). Confirm the approval is still unamended — 汇发〔2023〕28号 Annex 4 changed only §3 of the notice.
    www.safe.gov.cn ↗
M07 Proceeds of selling onshore real property you own 5 steps
  1. Step 1. Sell the onshore property. Under IIT Law Art. 15 para 2 the registration authority must inspect the IIT payment certificate for the transfer before registering it, so the tax is unavoidable and comes first in time.
    www.chinatax.gov.cn ↗
  2. Step 2. Compute and pay IIT at 20% on the GAIN — Art. 6(5) sets the taxable amount as the proceeds less the original value of the property and reasonable expenses; Art. 3(3) sets the rate for property-transfer income at a proportional 20%.
    www.chinatax.gov.cn ↗
  3. Step 3. Apply to SAFE for approval of the purchase-and-remit. PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 22 conditions the conversion and remittance of an overseas individual's onshore property-sale RMB on SAFE approval (经外汇局核准).
    www.gov.cn ↗
  4. Step 4. Obtain the bank's written quotation, then purchase and remit. The published board is reference-only and BOC disclaims results based on it.
    www.boc.cn ↗
  5. Step 5. On the U.S. side, assess whether the PRC IIT paid qualifies for a foreign tax credit, and whether the currency movement produces a §988 gain. §988(e)(1)-(2) excludes gain on a PERSONAL disposition of nonfunctional currency unless the gain exceeds USD 200, and §988(e)(3) removes that protection to the extent allocable expenses meet §162 or §212. Whether your facts are personal is not determinable from the material reviewed.
    www.govinfo.gov ↗
M17 Quota splitting across several people (分拆) 4 steps
  1. Step 1. DO NOT DO THIS. This row exists so the option is on the record with its consequences rather than being silently omitted.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  2. Step 2. China: Guidelines Art. 62 forbids evading convenience-amount or authenticity management by splitting. The borrower of a quota is placed on the watch list; the lender receives a risk-warning letter and is placed on the watch list on repetition. The watch period is the year of listing plus the following two consecutive years, during which every settlement or purchase requires ID plus amount-bearing materials and strict bank review.
    www.mofcom.gov.cn ↗
  3. Step 3. China: Order 532 Art. 39 (unlawful outward transfer of onshore FX or capital) and Art. 40 (illegal FX arbitrage) each carry a fine of up to 30% of the amount, rising to between 30% and 100% of the amount where the circumstances are serious, plus criminal liability where a crime is constituted.
    www.safe.gov.cn ↗
  4. Step 4. United States: 31 U.S.C. §5321(a)(4) permits a civil money penalty for a §5324 structuring violation of up to the full amount of the currency involved in the transaction. §5321(b)(1) gives the Secretary six years from the date of the transaction to assess, §5321(d) confirms a civil penalty may be imposed even where a criminal penalty is imposed for the same violation, and §5321(f) allows an ADDITIONAL penalty for a repeat violator of up to the greater of three times the profit gained or loss avoided, or twice the maximum penalty.
    www.govinfo.gov ↗
