Recommended approach (least cost & risk): (1) If the money is post-tax salary → use method 2 (uncapped, legal). (2) Otherwise → use method 1 up to $50,000/year. (3) For amounts above $50,000 → gather supporting documentation (emigration, medical, tuition, family maintenance, inheritance) and go through a SAFE-authorized bank. (4) On the U.S. side: file FBAR if aggregate foreign accounts >$10,000 at any time; consider Form 8938; claim FEIE/Foreign Tax Credit on any Chinese-taxed income.
