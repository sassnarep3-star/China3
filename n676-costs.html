How each number is built

Rate cost = amount × (selling − our mid) ÷ selling, where our mid = (6.6630 + 6.6911) ÷ 2 = 6.67705. That is 0.20998% of every dollar.
Wire fee = 1 per mille of the RMB handed over, floored at RMB 50 and capped at RMB 260, plus a RMB 150 telegraph charge (RMB 80 to HK, Macau or Taiwan).
Total = the two components, each rounded once, then added.
