high N676-F01 · Two data layers still instruct a reader to stage an emigrant transfer over two years, which 汇发〔2014〕2号 cancelled

What was found
data/cost_steps.csv rows 20-22 (method M03, steps 5-7) read 'Take tranche 1 — up to half', 'Take tranche 2 — up to half the remainder, after 1 year' and 'Take tranche 3 — the balance, after 2 years … Total elapsed time from first payment: two years'. data/d646_methods.csv row 6 (M05) states 'Staged: first half, then half of remainder after 1 year, rest after 2 years' and 'Yes, but staged over 2+ years'. Neither file contains the string 2014 or 汇发〔2014〕2号 anywhere (grep count 0 in both).
Why it is blank or wrong
汇发〔2014〕2号 §6(2) cancels the staged remittance: 银行可在核准件审批额度内一次或分次汇出相关资金. §6(1) also cancels the RMB 500,000 head-office filing. A reader following those rows would wait two years for money that can lawfully be remitted in one lump. (data/reference_inputs.csv R30, data/cost_inputs.csv CI29 and data/verify_2026.csv V08 record the same 2004 text, but those files do also carry the 2014 cancellation elsewhere, so they are lower risk.)
How to close it
The owning sessions should correct or mark those rows superseded. This session did not edit their files; the request is logged in COORDINATION.md. The n676 pages state the 2014 position only, and quote both the 2004 text and the 2014 cancellation side by side in the source register so the supersession is visible.
Source
N08 SAFE (republished by Tianjin branch) N07b People's Bank of China

high N676-F02 · Nine different headline totals for the same two transfers are live at once, with no superseded banner and no board stamp on most pages

What was found
Counted across the 98 published HTML files on this tree: the $10,000 wire appears as $53.38 (36 files), $53.42 (35), $53.45 (10), $53.47 (7) and $50.93 (2); the $400,000 wire appears as $901.30 (17), $901.20 (11), $901.81 (1) and $901.09 (1).
Why it is blank or wrong
Each figure is arithmetically correct for the intraday BOC board snapshot its layer was priced from. The defect is that a reader landing on any one page has no way to tell which snapshot it used or that a newer one exists. This is a presentation defect, not an arithmetic one.
How to close it
Every cost page should carry the board stamp it was priced from in its heading, and older layers should carry a superseded banner. The n676 pages carry the stamp. A cross-layer banner is a shared-file change, so it is logged as a request in COORDINATION.md rather than made here.

medium N676-F03 · BOC's 'Middle Rate' column is not the mid-point of its own buying and selling rates

What was found
On 2026-11-11 10:25:02 the board shows buying 6.6630, selling 6.6911 and 'Middle Rate' 6.7191. (6.6630 + 6.6911) / 2 = 6.67705, not 6.7191.

Any page that calls the board's middle column a 'fair value' or uses it as a mid-point is mislabelling it. The n676 pages compute their own mid-point, state that they do, and record the board's column separately.
How to close it

medium N676-F05 · The USD 50,000 figure is used for two unrelated rules across the tree

What was found
One is the individual annual convenience amount (汇发〔2020〕14号 art. 55, ID-only purchases, card holders only). The other is the institutional profit-remittance document-review threshold in 汇发〔2014〕2号 §5(1), which applies to companies.
Why it is blank or wrong
Conflating them leads to the belief that a U.S. individual may remit USD 50,000 a year on ID alone, which art. 59 does not permit without a Foreign Permanent Resident ID.
How to close it
Keep the two labelled distinctly on every page. The n676 methods table separates them (M03 vs M09).
