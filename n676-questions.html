critical Q01 · Where did the money in the Chinese account come from?

Why it decides the answer
This single fact chooses the route. Documented Chinese income goes under art. 59 on ID plus source and tax documents. A home sale, an inheritance and a pre-emigration estate each have their own instrument and their own document list. A gift has no verified remittance route at all. Money you cannot document has none either.
What is already known
The six lawful routes and the exact document each one needs are established (see Methods).
What is missing
Which of the six the balance actually is. Nothing in the brief says.
How to get it
Pull the account's own history: every credit over the last 24 months with its counterparty, plus the underlying contract, invoice, payroll record, sale deed, inheritance document or gift letter. Ask the account branch in writing to list what it holds on file for you.
Who asks
You, then the bank branch in writing

critical Q02 · What exactly are the 'considerable taxes' you were told about?

Why it decides the answer
The brief is the only evidence for them. No tax, no rate, no assessment and no office is named. If they are unpaid Chinese individual income tax on the underlying income, they are owed whether or not the money ever moves, and 3-45% or 20% dwarfs every bank cost on this site. If they are a bank's paraphrase of a documentation requirement, there may be no tax at all.
What is already known
Decree 532 art. 5 does not restrict current-account transfers, and no official source read here taxes the conversion or the wire itself. The PRC rates that could apply are 3-45% and 20%.
What is missing
The name of the tax, the rate, the amount, and which tax office said it.
How to get it
Ask the branch for the written rule it is applying, citing the document number. If a tax is claimed, ask the local 主管税务机关 for a written 完税凭证 or a written statement of what is outstanding. Do not accept a verbal figure.
Who asks
You, at the branch and the local tax office

critical Q03 · How is your account registered — as a 境内个人 or a 境外个人?

Why it decides the answer
The two instruments define 境内个人 differently. Decree 532 art. 52(2) counts a foreigner continuously resident in China for a full year as a 境内个人; the current-account guidelines route a 境外个人 through art. 59 instead. Which one the bank used when it opened the account decides whether you are dealing with art. 54's convenience amount or art. 59's document requirement.
What is already known
Both definitions exist and both instruments are in force. Art. 59 governs a 境外个人; arts. 54-57 govern a 境内个人.
What is missing
Which classification the bank actually recorded for this account. It is not visible from outside.
How to get it
Ask the branch in writing which of arts. 54-57 or art. 59 of 汇发〔2020〕14号 it will apply to your account, and on what basis it classified you. Get the answer on paper before you file anything.
Who asks
You, at the account branch

high Q04 · Was Chinese individual income tax actually paid on this money?

Why it decides the answer
The bank will not buy dollars for a foreign individual without tax evidence under art. 59. If the tax was never paid, settling it is a precondition, not a penalty for moving the money.
What is already known
Art. 59 requires 有交易额的购汇资金来源材料（含税务凭证）. Art. 15 of the IIT law makes the tax certificate a condition of registering a property transfer.
What is missing
Your own filing history in China, and whether an employer withheld.
How to get it
Request your 个人所得税 filing records from the local 主管税务机关 or from the 自然人电子税务局 app, for every year the money accrued.
Who asks
You, with the tax authority

high Q05 · Is any part of the balance already foreign currency?

Why it decides the answer
Dollars sitting in a Chinese foreign-currency account do not need converting. That removes the entire 0.21% spread and drops the bank cost to the wire fee alone — $32.42 on $10,000 instead of $53.42, and $61.28 on $400,000 instead of $901.20.
What is already known
Route R2 is priced and verified. The wire fee is the same tariff either way.
What is missing
Whether you hold an FX account at the bank and what is in it.
How to get it
Check the account list in mobile banking, or ask the branch for a statement of every account in your name and its currency.
Who asks
You, then the bank

high Q06 · Do you hold a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID card (外国人永久居留身份证)?

Why it decides the answer
If you do, the USD 50,000 annual convenience amount applies to you and a documented purchase is not needed for that slice. If you do not — which is the normal case for a U.S. passport holder — every purchase needs source and tax documents regardless of size.
What is already known
The rule is explicit: 持有外国人永久居留身份证的境外个人适用购汇年度便利化额度.
What is missing
Whether you hold the card.
How to get it
Look at your documents. If you have lived in China long-term, ask whether you are eligible; eligibility is a separate process and is not retroactive.
Who asks
You
Source
N03 SAFE Tianjin branch

high Q07 · Which bank, and what is its actual selling rate and fee schedule?

Why it decides the answer
Every number on this site is Bank of China's published board and published personal tariff. A different bank quotes a different spread, and a branch may apply local variations the tariff itself allows.
What is already known
BOC: 1 per mille, floor RMB 50, ceiling RMB 260, plus RMB 150 telegraph. Board stamped 2026-11-11 10:25:02.
What is missing
Your bank's live quote for your amount, and whether it charges the OUR surcharge.
How to get it
Ask your bank for a written quote covering the selling rate, the wire fee, the telegraph charge and any intermediary deductions, for the exact amount and date you intend to move.
Who asks
You, at your bank

high Q08 · What did the intermediary and the receiving bank actually deduct?

Why it decides the answer
This is the largest unknown cost on the whole transfer and no bank publishes it. It is left blank on every page rather than estimated.
What is already known
Nothing. No official source read here publishes correspondent or receiving-bank charges.
What is missing
The actual deduction on your first real wire.
How to get it
Choose 'OUR' (sender pays) on the first wire if your bank offers it, then reconcile the received amount against the sent amount. The difference is your true intermediary cost, and you can then decide whether to route the rest differently.
Who asks
You, after the first wire

high Q09 · When, and at what rate, was the RMB actually acquired?

Why it decides the answer
The U.S. currency gain is measured against your real basis, not against an IRS average. The averages are used here only because no basis was supplied. The true figure could be a gain or a loss.
What is already known
§988 makes the gain ordinary income and the $200 personal-transaction threshold is the only relief. At $10,000 with a 2024 basis the modelled gain is $692.59, so the threshold is passed.
What is missing
The date and rate of every credit into the account.
How to get it
Match each credit to the spot rate on that date. IRS's own page says it has no official rate and accepts any posted rate used consistently.
Who asks
You, with a U.S. tax adviser

high Q10 · Have FBAR, Form 8938 and Form 3520 been filed for every open year?

Why it decides the answer
These are the penalties that can exceed the whole balance. The FBAR maximum is $16,536 non-willful and $165,353 willful per violation, assessed per year, and the general willful BSA range runs to $286,184. A foreign gift over $100,000 is separately reportable on Form 3520 Part IV.
What is already known
The thresholds and maxima are verified from eCFR and IRS.
What is missing
Which years you held over $10,000 aggregate in foreign accounts, and whether you received a reportable gift or bequest.
How to get it
Reconstruct foreign-account balances year by year from statements, then have a U.S. adviser assess whether the Streamlined or Delinquent FBAR procedures fit. Do not move the money before this is settled.
Who asks
You, with a U.S. tax adviser
Source

medium Q11 · If it is a home sale, is the transfer registered and is the tax certificate issued?

Why it decides the answer
Art. 15 of the IIT law requires the registration authority to check the individual income tax payment certificate before it registers the transfer. Without it the sale is not complete and there is nothing to remit.
What is already known
The requirement is in the statute. The remittance route for property proceeds is the emigrant/inheritance framework.
What is missing
The registration certificate and the 完税凭证.
How to get it
Get both from the local 不动产登记机构 and 主管税务机关. The registered contract is also what the bank will ask for.
Who asks
You, with the registration office

medium Q12 · If it is an inheritance or a pre-emigration estate, has hukou cancellation happened?

Why it decides the answer
Both routes require a household-registration cancellation certificate, and the IIT law requires Chinese tax to be settled before the cancellation. The application goes to the SAFE branch at the emigrant's former hukou or the decedent's last hukou.
What is already known
Since 2014 the bank may remit the approved amount in one lump; the RMB 500,000 head-office filing and the two-year staging are both cancelled.
What is missing
The hukou cancellation certificate, proof of settlement abroad, and the SAFE approval itself.
How to get it
Apply through the public security household-registration office first, then to the SAFE branch. Ask that branch for its written service standard and any fee — neither is published.
Who asks
You, with SAFE and the PSB

medium Q13 · Is the money inside a company rather than in your personal account?

Why it decides the answer
A dividend route exists but it is a different question entirely: corporate tax, a 20% dividend withholding, a board resolution and a tax filing form, then the remittance. None of the personal routes above applies to it.
What is already known
The institutional profit-remittance rule requires a board resolution and the tax filing form above USD 50,000.
What is missing
Whether the balance is corporate.
How to get it
Check whose name the account is in. If it is the company's, this site's personal routes do not apply and a corporate adviser is needed.
Who asks
You

medium Q14 · How fast do you need the money?

Why it decides the answer
Speed changes the route. One wire settles in days. A SAFE approval for an emigrant or inheritance transfer has no published service standard, so it cannot be scheduled. The convenience-amount route can take one calendar year per USD 50,000.
What is already known
One wire is the fastest verified path. Approval timelines are not published anywhere read.
What is missing
A real service standard from the SAFE branch that would hear your application.
How to get it
Ask the branch in writing. Treat any verbal timeline as unverified.
Who asks
You, with SAFE

medium Q15 · Are you a U.S. citizen living abroad, and does the FEIE apply?

Why it decides the answer
The FEIE ceiling is $132,900 for 2026 and $130,000 for 2025, but it covers EARNED income only. It does nothing for a dividend, a rent receipt, a property gain or the §988 currency gain — which are the likely components of this balance.
What is already known
The ceilings and the earned-income scope are verified from IRS.
What is missing
Your tax home, your qualifying days, and the composition of the balance.
How to get it
Have a U.S. adviser run the bona fide residence or physical presence test against your actual days, then map each component of the balance to its category.
Who asks
You, with a U.S. tax adviser

medium Q16 · Is any of the money traceable to a gift from a Chinese person?

Why it decides the answer
A gift has no verified remittance route on the Chinese side — the bank needs source documents that a gift does not generate. On the U.S. side it is separately reportable above $100,000 from a nonresident alien or foreign estate.
What is already known
No verified Chinese route; the U.S. Form 3520 thresholds are verified.
What is missing
Whether a gift is involved and its size.
How to get it
Get the donor's written statement and any transfer record. Then ask the branch in writing what it would accept — do not assume the answer.
Who asks
You, then the branch

low Q17 · Would routing through Hong Kong, Macau or Taiwan be cheaper?

Why it decides the answer
The telegraph charge on that leg is RMB 80 instead of RMB 150, which saves about $10.46 per wire. But the onward leg from Hong Kong to the U.S. is a second transfer with its own charges, and none of them are published here.
What is already known
The RMB 80 figure is in the same published BOC tariff. Route R4 is priced for the first leg only.
What is missing
The onward leg's cost and any reporting consequence of the intermediate stop.
How to get it
Price the full two-leg route with the receiving bank before using it. The first-leg saving alone is not a reason to route through a third jurisdiction.
Who asks
You, with both banks

low Q18 · Should the balance move in one wire or several?

Why it decides the answer
One wire is cheapest because the 1 per mille fee is capped at RMB 260, so the marginal cost of a bigger single wire is zero above about $38,857. Eight $50,000 wires cost $1,330.12 on $400,000 against $901.20 for one.
What is already known
The cap and the resulting ladder are verified. Splitting a quota across DIFFERENT people is prohibited and is treated as evasion.
What is missing
Whether your bank or the receiving bank imposes a per-transfer limit.
How to get it
Ask both banks for their single-transfer and daily limits in writing.
Who asks
You, with both banks
