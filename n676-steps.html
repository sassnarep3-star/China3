Step by step

Exactly how each route is executed, in order, with the official source behind each step.

M01 Documented income earned in China (salary, fees, other current-account income) Lawful

  1. Settle the U.S. side before you move anything.

    Reconstruct foreign-account balances year by year and have a U.S. adviser assess FBAR, Form 8938 and any Form 3520 exposure. The FBAR maximum is $16,536 non-willful and $165,353 willful per violation; moving the money first does not reduce it.

    N12 FinCEN / eCFR N14 Internal Revenue Service

  2. Assemble the source-of-funds file.

    Employment contract or service agreement, payroll or invoice records, and the tax evidence — a tax payment certificate, an exemption certificate or the filing form. Art. 59 requires documents showing the transaction amount and including tax certificates.

    N05 SAFE Tianjin branch

  3. Ask the branch in writing which article it will apply.

    Art. 59 for a 境外个人, or arts. 54-57 if the bank classified you as a 境内个人. The two definitions of 境内个人 differ and the bank's classification is not visible from outside.

    N06 State Council (via SAFE) N03 SAFE Tianjin branch N04 SAFE Ningxia branch

  4. Buy the dollars at the counter or in mobile banking.

    You pay the bank's selling rate. On the 2026-11-11 10:25:02 board that is 6.6911 against a buying rate of 6.6630, so 0.20998% of every dollar is the rate cost.

    N01 Bank of China

  5. Instruct one wire for the whole amount.

    The fee is 1 per mille with a RMB 50 floor and a RMB 260 ceiling, plus a RMB 150 telegraph charge. Above about $38,857 the fee is pinned at the ceiling, so one wire is always cheapest.

    N02 Bank of China

  6. Choose the charge option deliberately.

    'OUR' makes you pay the intermediary charges up front and is the only way to learn what they actually are. Intermediary and receiving-bank charges are not published anywhere and are left blank on this site.

    N02 Bank of China

  7. Reconcile the received amount against the sent amount.

    The difference is your true intermediary cost. Record it; it is the one number on this page that no official source can give you.

    N02 Bank of China

M02 Foreign currency already in your Chinese FX account Lawful

  1. Confirm what is actually in the FX account.

    Ask the branch for a statement of every account in your name and its currency. Dollars already there need no conversion.

    N01 Bank of China N02 Bank of China

  2. Wire it out on your passport.

    No purchase, so no spread. The wire fee is the same 1 per mille / RMB 50-260 plus RMB 150 telegraph.

    N02 Bank of China

  3. Do not top the FX account up from RMB first.

    That would be a conversion and would attract the 0.20998% spread. Only the dollars genuinely already there avoid it.

    N01 Bank of China

M03 Annual USD 50,000 convenience amount on a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID Lawful — only for card holders

  1. Confirm you actually hold the 外国人永久居留身份证.

    Without the card this route does not exist for you; the annual convenience amount is expressly limited to card holders.

    N03 SAFE Tianjin branch

  2. Plan one calendar year per USD 50,000.

    Above the annual amount you either need source documents (which makes this M01) or you buy year by year. Eight wires on $400,000 costs $1,330.12 against $901.20 for one.

    N03 SAFE Tianjin branch N02 Bank of China

  3. Never use anyone else's amount.

    Splitting a quota across people is treated as evasion under Decree 532 art. 39 and puts the people involved on a watch list for the year and the next two.

    N06 State Council (via SAFE)

M04 Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) Lawful — needs SAFE approval

  1. Cancel the Chinese household registration and settle tax first.

    The IIT law requires tax to be cleared BEFORE the hukou is cancelled. Get the cancellation certificate from the public security office.

    N09b State Taxation Administration N07 People's Bank of China

  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at your former hukou location.

    Written application, applicant form, ID, cancellation certificate, settlement-abroad proof certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate, property-right documents, and the tax payment certificate.

    N07 People's Bank of China

  3. Note what 2014 removed.

    No RMB 500,000 head-office filing. No staged remittance — the bank may remit once or in instalments inside the approved amount. No notarisation of property documents or of the agency agreement.

    N08 SAFE (republished by Tianjin branch)

  4. Instruct the bank with the approval in hand.

    The bank must remit straight to your own account abroad; cash withdrawal in China is not permitted. Bank cost is the same 0.20998% spread plus the wire fee.

    N07 People's Bank of China N02 Bank of China

M05 Inheritance transfer (继承财产转移) Lawful — needs SAFE approval

  1. Gather the inheritance evidence.

    Proof of inheritance, the decedent's property-right documents, and a tax payment certificate from the tax authority where the property sits. Notarisation is no longer required.

    N07 People's Bank of China N08 SAFE (republished by Tianjin branch)

  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at the decedent's last hukou location.

    Since 2014 several inheritances can be combined at one decedent's hukou location instead of applying separately.

    N08 SAFE (republished by Tianjin branch)

  3. Remit to your own account abroad.

    Directly to the beneficiary's account in the country of residence. No cash in China.

    N07 People's Bank of China

M07 Wire to Hong Kong, Macau or Taiwan as a first leg Lawful

  1. Price the whole two-leg route before you start.

    The first leg saves about $10.46 per wire on the telegraph charge (RMB 80 not RMB 150). The onward leg from Hong Kong to the U.S. is unpriced here and could easily cost more than that.

    N02 Bank of China

M09 Dividend from your own Chinese company Lawful — corporate route

  1. Treat this as a corporate question, not a personal one.

    Corporate income tax first, then a 20% dividend, then the remittance on a board resolution and the tax filing form. Nothing on this site prices the corporate leg.

    N08b SAFE (republished by Tianjin branch) N09 State Taxation Administration
