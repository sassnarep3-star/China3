What this adds up to

If Chinese tax is already paid and the U.S. filings are current

The cost is the bank's: $53.42 on $10,000 and $901.20 on $400,000 — plus whatever the intermediary deducts, which nobody publishes.

N01 Bank of China N02 Bank of China

If Chinese tax was never paid on the income

3% to 45% or a flat 20% of the income, owed regardless of whether the money moves. That is the "considerable amount of taxes" in the brief — and it is not a charge for the transfer.

N09 State Taxation Administration

If the U.S. filings were missed

Up to $16,536 per unfiled FBAR year, $165,353 if willful, and a §988 ordinary-income gain on top. Settle these before moving the money, not after.

N12 FinCEN / eCFR N11 U.S. Government Publishing Office
