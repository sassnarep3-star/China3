The "conversion penalty" — broken down

There is no Chinese tax on converting CNY to USD, and no U.S. tax on the act of moving your own money. The table lists every real cost/penalty component, with the amount where an official source confirms it — otherwise left blank and flagged UNVERIFIED.

Illustrative comparison (estimates only — costs blank/unverified): converting $50,000 via a bank with a 1% FX spread and a $30 wire fee would cost roughly $530 (blank, unverified). A single non-willful FBAR violation can cost up to $16,536; a willful violation can exceed half the account balance. Using legal channels and filing U.S. reports is far cheaper than any penalty.

Penalty amounts are inflation-adjusted annually; check 31 CFR 1010.821 for current figures at the time of filing.
