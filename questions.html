The short answer

1. The conversion is not the taxed event

No official page read today taxes converting RMB into USD or wiring it out. What the bank checks is whether the money is genuine and lawful and whether Chinese tax on it has been dealt with. The large numbers in this problem are Chinese income tax on the money behind the balance, and U.S. income tax on the currency gain.

F10 F04 F09 · Tax page

2. The bank's own charge is small - about 0.2-0.5 %

$53.42 on $10,000 (0.53 %) and $901.20 on $400,000 (0.23 %): the exchange margin plus the RMB 260 wire cap and the RMB 150 cable fee. Fees charged by the receiving or intermediary bank are not published anywhere and are excluded.

F01 F02 · Ladder

3. A U.S. passport alone does not get the $50,000 facility

The annual USD 50,000 convenience amount is for domestic individuals and for foreign individuals holding a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID. Everyone else converts under art 59 with identity plus source-of-funds documents including tax evidence.

F04 F05 F06 · Methods

4. Six facts decide everything, and only you have them

Where the money came from, which currency it is, how much, when it was acquired, your Chinese residence status, and your U.S. reporting history. Until those six are written down, no route can be recommended - which is why this page is organised as questions rather than as a recommendation.

A05Are you a 'domestic individual' (境内个人) in Chinese FX terms?

F52C-U15		Chinese rules define 境内个人 as a Chinese citizen, or a foreigner who has resided continuously in China for one year (diplomats excluded). A06Do you hold a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID card (外国人永久居留身份证)?

A07Did you emigrate from China and cancel your Chinese hukou?

A08Is the money an inheritance?
open needs your facts
F52C-U17		Then the inheritance transfer (继承财产转移) route applies: one application covering the whole estate share, filed with SAFE where the deceased was registered, with notarised relationship and title documents and the deceased's tax certificate.Gather: death certificate, notarised proof of your relationship to the deceased, proof of the estate share, title documents for the assets, and the deceased's 完税凭证. A09Is it the price of a home you sold in China?
open needs your facts		Then the property route applies, and 20 % Chinese individual income tax is charged on the gain (sale price minus original cost and reasonable expenses), not on the price. A10Do you hold Chinese tax evidence for the income behind the balance?

A11What is your U.S. filing position, and have you reported this account before?

F52C-U02		The U.S. exposure is not the conversion; it is the reporting history. A12Which bank, which branch, which city?

A13Is this a 'considerable tax' you have already been quoted, or a fear?

B. Chinese-side rules (16 questions)

QuestionAnswer we haveHow to settle itSource
B01Is there a Chinese tax on converting RMB into USD, or on wiring it out?
verified sourced answer		No official page read today imposes a tax on the conversion or the wire. The Regulation says current international payments and transfers are not restricted, but requires a genuine and lawful transaction basis, and the bank must reasonably review the documents, including tax evidence.Nothing to obtain. The tax that matters is on the income behind the money, and on any gain - see B15 and C01.F10 F04 F09
B02Is USD 50,000 a hard annual cap?
verified sourced answer		No. SAFE states that genuine current-account and opened capital-account purchases and sales have no amount limit, and that the annual convenience amount 'is not the annual limit' - it only means that above it you cannot transact on ID alone.Nothing to obtain. But see B03 for who holds the convenience amount at all.F05
B03Does a U.S. passport holder get the USD 50,000 convenience amount?
verified sourced answer		Only if you also hold a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID card. Otherwise the art 59 rule applies: passport plus source-of-funds documents including tax evidence.See A06.F04 F06
B04What documents will the bank actually ask a foreign individual for?
verified sourced answer		Your valid ID document, and documents showing the source of the funds with the transaction amount, including the tax evidence. For service-trade, income and current-transfer items SAFE names the acceptable tax documents.Ask the branch for its own checklist for your purpose code before you queue.F04 F09
B05What counts as 'tax evidence'?
verified sourced answer		One of: the 《服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表》 (including the electronic filing information), a 完税证明 (tax payment certificate), or a 免税证明 (tax exemption certificate).Obtain whichever matches your facts - see A10.F09
B06Can I change unused RMB back into foreign currency?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U09		Yes, with your ID and the original exchange slip, valid for 24 months from the exchange date. Up to USD 500 a day (USD 1,000 at port-area outlets before departure) needs only your ID.See A14.F08
B07How much foreign cash can I carry out of China?
verified sourced answer		Up to USD 5,000: customs releases it. USD 5,000-10,000: a 《携带外汇出境许可证》 from the bank. Above USD 10,000: a permit from SAFE, and in principle not allowed except for special destinations.Ask the bank for the permit before travelling; budget the trip cost, which no official page prices.F13
B08How much can I take out with a mainland bank card abroad?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U06		RMB 100,000 equivalent per calendar year across all your cards including supplementary cards, and RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day. Exceeding the annual cap suspends overseas cash withdrawal this year and next.Nothing to obtain. Note the bank's own per-withdrawal fee: RMB 12 + 1 %, capped at RMB 112 on UnionPay from 2026-11-11.F12 F03
B09What happens if I use relatives' or friends' convenience amounts?
verified sourced answer		The FX regulator puts people who borrow another person's convenience amount, or otherwise evade the facility and the authenticity checks, on a watchlist for the rest of that year and the next two years. Evasion and arbitrage carry fines of up to 30 % of the amount.Nothing to obtain. This is the route to avoid: it converts a documentation problem into a penalty record.F07 F10
B10What is the penalty for private or 'underground' FX dealing?
verified sourced answer		Warning, confiscation of the proceeds, and a fine of up to 30 % of the amount; 30 % up to the equivalent amount where the circumstances are serious; criminal liability where it constitutes a crime.Nothing to obtain.F10
B11What is the penalty for breaching the cash-carry rules?
verified sourced answer		A warning and a fine of up to 20 % of the amount; where a law or regulation gives customs the power to punish, customs does so.Nothing to obtain.F10 F13
B12Can I use cryptocurrency or stablecoins to move the value out?
verified sourced answer
F52C-R02		No. The 2026 joint notice states that virtual-currency business activity is illegal financial activity, that RMB-to-virtual-currency exchange inside China is strictly prohibited and will be shut down, and that financial institutions may not open accounts, transfer funds or settle for it. That notice repealed the 2021 notice.Nothing to obtain.F16
B13How does an inheritance or emigrant property transfer actually work?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U17		One application for the whole amount, filed with SAFE where the emigrant's hukou was registered or where the deceased was registered. Emigrant transfers go out in stages: up to half first, up to half of the remainder after one year, the rest after two years; up to RMB 200,000 can be paid in one go once approved. Up to RMB 500,000 is approved by the local SAFE branch, above that by SAFE head office.Ask that SAFE branch for its current document list; the 2004 measure lists the core set (application, two forms, identity and settlement proof, title documents, tax certificate).F11
B14Can the bank pay the money to someone else, or hand me cash?
verified sourced answer		For emigrant and inheritance transfers, no: after selling the FX the bank must wire it directly to the applicant's own account in the country of residence, and must not pay foreign cash inside China.Have the receiving account details (your own U.S. account) ready in the application.F11
B15What Chinese tax applies to the money itself?
verified sourced answer		Individual income tax: 3 %-45 % progressive on wages and the other comprehensive items, 5 %-35 % on business income, and a flat 20 % on interest, dividends, rent, property-transfer gains and incidental income. Bank deposit interest has been exempt since 2008-10-09.If any of it was never taxed, ask the tax bureau for an assessment before the bank asks you for the certificate.F14 F15
B16Do I have to settle Chinese tax before giving up my hukou?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U17		Yes. A taxpayer who cancels a Chinese hukou because of emigration must complete a tax settlement before the cancellation, and the law makes emigration a trigger for filing a return.Ask the tax bureau for the settlement and keep the certificate; the transfer application needs it.F14

C. U.S. side (9 questions)

QuestionAnswer we haveHow to settle itSource
C01Does the U.S. tax me for converting RMB into USD?
verified sourced answer		Converting is a disposition of non-functional currency, which is a §988 transaction. The currency gain or loss is computed separately and treated as ordinary income or loss.Compute it from your own basis - see A04 and C03.F17
C02Is there a personal-transaction exception?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U10		Yes: no gain is recognised on a personal transaction, but the exception stops applying once the gain exceeds USD 200, and it does not cover a transaction to the extent that expenses allocable to it meet §162 (other than travel) or §212 (other than taxes).If the gain is small (under USD 200) the exception matters. At the amounts in the brief the gain exceeds it by orders of magnitude - see the currency-gain column.F17
C03How is the gain computed, and at what rate?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U10		You translate foreign currency at the rate prevailing when you received or acquired it. The IRS states it has no official exchange rate and generally accepts any posted rate used consistently, and it publishes yearly averages - 2024: 7.189, 2025: 7.129 CNY per USD.Use the rate and date of your own acquisition, not the yearly average, if you have the documents. The yearly average is only the illustration used here.F18 F17
C04Must I report the Chinese account to the U.S.?
verified sourced answer		Two separate duties. FBAR (FinCEN 114) if the aggregate value of your foreign financial accounts exceeds USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year. Form 8938 if you are a specified individual over the threshold: for a single filer living in the U.S., more than USD 50,000 on the last day or USD 75,000 at any time; the thresholds rise for joint filers and for those living abroad.Check each year separately. The FBAR is filed with FinCEN, not with the return.F19
C05What are the penalties if I did not report?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U02		FBAR: USD 16,536 for a non-willful violation and USD 165,353 for a willful one, as adjusted for penalties assessed on or after 17 January 2025. Form 8938: up to USD 10,000, plus USD 10,000 for each 30 days after notice, capped at USD 60,000, with criminal exposure in addition.Establish the facts of each year before deciding how to regularise; that is a question for a U.S. preparer, not for a website.F20 F19
C06If the money is a gift, what must I file?
verified sourced answer
F52C-U03		Form 3520 Part IV if the aggregate from a nonresident alien or foreign estate exceeds USD 100,000 in the year, or from foreign corporations or partnerships exceeds USD 20,116 (2025) or USD 20,573 (2026). Failure carries 5 % of the gift per month, capped at 25 %, with a reasonable-cause defence.Keep donor identity, relationship and bank records; separately identify each gift over USD 5,000.F21 F22
C07If I carry the cash into the U.S., what must I file?
verified sourced answer		A report above USD 10,000 on FinCEN Form 105, filed electronically with CBP; the threshold applies to the group total, not per person. Failing to file risks seizure and forfeiture, and civil or criminal penalties.File before or at the border, not afterwards.F23
C08Does the U.S.-China treaty change the tax on a dividend?
unverified no official answer
F52C-U11		Not verified in this pass: the treaty article on dividends was not read today, so no treaty rate is stated and none is used in any figure.Read Article 9 of the U.S.-China income tax treaty, then confirm with a preparer. Until then the dividend row is priced at the statutory 20 % only.F14 F17
C09Can I credit the Chinese tax I paid against my U.S. tax?
unverified no official answer
F52C-U12		Not verified in this pass. Whether the Chinese tax is creditable, and how the §988 gain interacts with it, was not read from an official page today, so nothing is claimed here.Read IRS Publication 514 (Foreign Tax Credit for Individuals) and the Form 1116 instructions; if the Chinese tax was on wages, the credit usually follows the wage income.F17
