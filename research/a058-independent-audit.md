# A058 independent review — critical source scope and cost presentation

Review date: 2026-11-10. Scope is the specific claims and artifacts below, not a line-by-line verification of the entire repository. This is general information, not legal or tax advice. The source links were opened independently; a passing local script does not prove a legal interpretation or make a historical bank quote current.

## High priority: FX purchase is not the same as a foreign-currency remittance

`research/s775-executive-summary.md` item 4 and `s775-dashboard.html` tell a U.S. citizen that a personal wire needs no approval up to USD 50,000/day on identification, while S775 M01 labels the route "Lawful - unrestricted". This is not established for this user. The [2024 SAFE Tianjin guidance on currency purchases by overseas individuals](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html) says their lawful current-account RMB income is purchased with ID and amount-bearing source evidence including tax documentation, and notes the annual convenience allowance for foreign permanent-residence ID holders. The [SAFE Tianjin $50,000/day remittance guidance](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0229/2402.html) explicitly concerns *domestic individuals* remitting foreign currency from an FX account for current-account expenses, not a universal right to purchase USD or send any funds on ID alone. The [2025 SAFE explanation](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/1113/2926.html) also distinguishes the applicable annual allowance. Citizenship alone does not establish the user's regulatory classification, eligibility, account purpose or documents. Do not split transactions to evade controls.

## High priority: modelled spread is not necessarily deducted from USD delivered

The live `s775-cost-10k-400k.html` says CNY needed for $10,000 is 66,903 at its assumed 6.6903 CNY/USD ask and simultaneously labels $9,946.58 as "net" after subtracting a spread benchmark and a fee. Under the model's own ask assumption 66,903 CNY buys $10,000 before wire deductions; the model's spread is an opportunity cost relative to a hypothetical midpoint, not a second automatic deduction. Whether the wire fee is deducted from principal or separately charged, and any intermediary/recipient charges, are not established by that model. The amount actually received is therefore **unverified**, not the displayed benchmark number. Rates and charges must be obtained from the executing banks; [BOC's rate board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/) is not an executable quote for this user. The midpoint convention and denominator also matter: with ask 6.6903 and bid 6.6622, (ask−mid)/ask is approximately 0.2100%, while (ask−mid)/mid is approximately 0.2104%; they describe different baselines, not interchangeable universal fees. These are arithmetic illustrations based solely on the published model's historical assumptions, not independent verification of the snapshot.

## High priority: do not state a universal PRC tax conclusion

The categorical "No PRC tax on conversion" in `s775-dashboard.html` and the S775 executive summary is broader than the evidence cited there establishes. No individual tax assessment or source documents were supplied. The [SAFE guidance for overseas individuals](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2016/1209/420.html) requires relevant transaction and tax documentation for specified current-account payments; it does not establish that every tax liability is zero. Income, gain, prior unpaid tax and associated reporting depend on facts and governing law. Do not present proof of source tax payment as a newly levied conversion tax or assume no tax at all.

## U.S. reporting and tax boundary

The [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/understand-how-to-report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts) explains the FBAR threshold is *more than* $10,000 aggregate at any time during the year, not $10,000 per account. The [statute, 26 USC §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title26/html/USCODE-2023-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm), differentiates nonfunctional-currency transactions and personal transaction rules. A personal basis, character, reporting history and applicable tax cannot be determined from the user's description. The [IRS explanation of Form 1116](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/practical-considerations-foreign-tax-credit-form-1116-youtube-video-text-script) notes that foreign tax credits are subject to limits; they do not automatically eliminate all double taxation.
