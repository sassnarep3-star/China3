## Evidence checked

- [SAFE Tianjin 2024](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html) says qualifying foreign individuals' lawful RMB current-account income purchases require valid ID and source materials including tax evidence; foreign permanent-resident-ID holders qualify for the purchase convenience amount. [SAFE Tianjin 2025](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/1113/2926.html) distinguishes domestic individuals and foreign permanent-resident-ID holders. The homepage's unqualified “per person” claim risks implying this facility automatically applies to every U.S. passport holder. Request S119 correct homepage.
- [SAFE Shanghai 2016](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2016/1209/420.html): evidence for specified foreign individual current-account remittances includes tax records, not proof of a new tax on converting an account.
- [BOC personal e-banking tariff](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html): 1‰, ¥50 min, ¥260 max, ¥150 telecom for transfers outside HK/Macau/Taiwan; local or contractual variation possible. [BOC trade-finance notice](https://www.boc.cn/cbservice/bi2/201711/t20171109_10646775.html): US ordinary-wire full-amount option $15 if recipient bank holds account with BOC New York, $25 otherwise, subject to stated intermediary conditions. The cited page is in corporate finance, and does not demonstrate this option applies to the personal tariff. Schedule dashboard presents $25 as personal BOC OUR and adds it to illustrative costs. Cross-session review X-05 recognizes related uncertainty; do not present the $78.41 figure as verified personal all-in cost. Request S119 add clear global warning in index and ask S436 owner to correct schedule and generated data.
- [26 USC §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title26/html/USCODE-2023-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm): dispositions of nonfunctional currency can have foreign-currency gain/loss, ordinarily characterized under the section; exceptions apply. A model without the person's basis, acquisition and transaction dates and tax status cannot provide actual tax.
- [FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts) states aggregate foreign-account value must **exceed** $10,000 at any time, not be equal to $10,000, for FBAR filing to be triggered. The homepage table's wording “USD 10,000 at any time” is imprecise. Request S119 correction.
- [IRS FEIE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/figuring-the-foreign-earned-income-exclusion): $130,000 is for 2025; $132,900 is for 2026, if qualified. [IRS foreign tax credit](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit) precludes a credit for taxes on excluded income. No inference of user eligibility.
- [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/money-monetary-instruments): currency and monetary instruments over $10,000 must be reported, and family/group totals aggregate. China exit rules are separate. [SAFE Xiamen guide](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf) prohibits borrowing quotas to split transactions.
