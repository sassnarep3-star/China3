# B9B3 executive summary — 2026-11-11

**Question:** how can a U.S. citizen with CNY in a PRC bank account convert and transfer it to USD with minimal lawful tax/conversion penalties?

1. **Eligibility before price.** The source of the CNY decides the lawful route. SAFE Art. 59 permits a foreign individual to buy FX against valid ID, amount-bearing source-of-funds documents and tax evidence; Art. 65 permits the FX-account outward wire. [BS06](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2020/art_0dd36157a03f494db7571002c7f785b8.html) [BS07](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html) [BS13](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2021/1102/1777.html) [BS18](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5194375/content.html)
2. **No conversion tax was found.** Decree 532 Art. 5 does not restrict genuine current-account transfers; no official page read imposes a tax on the act of converting one's own CNY. PRC IIT, if any, attaches to the underlying income/gain (3%-45% wages, 20% flat categories), and tax evidence is required for large payments. [BS19](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html) [BS28](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html) [BS18](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5194375/content.html)
3. **The USD 50,000 figure is not a hard cap and not a U.S.-passport entitlement.** It is an annual convenience/documentation threshold; for FX purchase by an 境外个人, only PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders get it on ID alone. [BS08](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html) [BS09](https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html) [BS07](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html) [BS12](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/0613/2811.html)
4. **Known bank cost at the 2026-11-11 10:40:02 board:** $10,000 → **$53.42** (0.5342%); $400,000 → **$901.18** (0.2253%). This is the published single-wire BOC benchmark only, not an executable quote or all-in total. [BS01](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/enindex_1619.html) [BS02](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html) [BS05](https://www.boc.cn/pbservice/pb4/200806/t20080626_760.html)
5. **U.S. side can dominate.** Worldwide income, FBAR >$10,000, Form 8938, Form 3520, §988 ordinary currency gain/loss, and prior non-willful correction procedures all need review; PRC income tax may be creditable. [BS47](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/u-s-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad) [BS36](https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts) [BS35](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements) [BS40](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person) [BS33](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title26/html/USCODE-2023-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm) [BS42](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/streamlined-filing-compliance-procedures) [BS41](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit) [BS32](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf)
6. **Prohibited shortcuts.** Quota splitting, private/app/underground FX swaps, crypto/stablecoin/RWA conversion, false documents and undeclared excess cash are prohibited; Decree 532 fines reach 30% (30%-100% if serious). [BS12](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/0613/2811.html) [BS19](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html) [BS23](https://www.safe.gov.cn/guangdong/2020/0304/1668.html) [BS24](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/2017/0414/774.html) [BS25](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html) [BS49](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/money-monetary-instruments)
