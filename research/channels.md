# T4a — All CNY → USD conversion channels

Session `session-c703e38c` · reviewed 2026-11-07 (UTC)
Scope: `research/channels.md`. Structured data: `data/channels.csv` (35 channels × 8 attributes,
one official link per row). Rendered at `deep-channels.html`.

## Approach

"As many as possible" was interpreted as: every distinct route that an official Chinese or
U.S. source describes for turning a CNY balance held in a mainland bank into USD value
available outside China, **including routes that are prohibited** — because the user asked
for pros and cons of "all the ways" and because ruling out illegal routes is itself a
finding. Each route is classified `Lawful`, `Lawful (approval)`, `Lawful (closed loop)`,
`Lawful (institutional)`, `Lawful (registration)`, `Lawful (offshore leg)`, or `PROHIBITED`.
A route is never described as available to the user unless his/her ID classification and
source-of-funds facts are known (IR-014).

## A. Lawful onshore bank routes (1–10)

1. **Annual convenience-amount purchase + remittance** — 境内个人, USD 50,000/person/year,
   ID only, with a completed 《个人购汇申请书》. Simple, but limited to domestic individuals
   and to permitted purposes. [S01](https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html)
2. **Current-account purchase above the convenience amount (not quota-consuming)** — no
   official amount limit for genuine, documented current-account needs; ID + 有交易额的
   购汇资金用途材料; bank authenticity review. [S01]
3. **Remit FX already held in a foreign-currency savings account** — same-day cumulative
   ≤USD 50,000 ID only; above needs transaction materials; avoids a conversion spread if
   the money is already FX. [S02](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0229/2402.html)
4. **Foreign-individual purchase from lawful RMB income + tax certificate** — 指引第59条;
   no annual quota; the bank checks the RMB source. **Best fit for the stated identity**,
   conditional on IR-007. [S03](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html),
   [S43](https://www.safe.gov.cn/chongqing/file/file/20190311/87806e211bb74eb4a44e71395c31cda9.pdf)
5. **Reconvert unused RMB from a prior conversion** — original exchange memo, valid 24
   months; small amounts (≤USD 500/day; ≤USD 1,000 at domestic departure-side border points)
   with ID only. [S04](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0430/2464.html)
6. **Return unused inward FX along the original route** — ID only. [S05](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2006-11/30/content_5712728.htm)
7. **Proceeds from sale of domestic real estate by a foreign individual** — SAFE approval;
   capital-account route. [S05]
8. **Emigration / inheritance property transfer** — dedicated PBOC 2004 rules; ≥RMB 500k
   needs SAFE head-office approval; ≥RMB 200k emigration transfers are staged over two years.
   [S17](https://www.safe.gov.cn/guangdong/2021/0323/1994.html),
   [S18](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2004/1116/4134.html)
9. **Service-trade / income / current-transfer purchase by a foreign individual** — with
   税务备案表 / 完税证明 / 免税证明. [S44](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2021/1102/1777.html)
10. **Company profit / dividend repatriation** — if the money is corporate, not personal;
    no official amount limit with board resolution, tax filing, audited accounts and prior-loss
    make-up. [S20](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2017/0126/76.html)

## B. Cross-border RMB and offshore-conversion routes (11–15)

11. **Individual current-account cross-border RMB settlement** (salary and other lawful
    income) — bank applies 展业三原则; conversion happens offshore. [S38](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4158143/index.html)
12. **Offshore CNH conversion (e.g. Hong Kong)** — the FX leg is offshore; no onshore
    individual FX quota; exposed to CNY/CNH basis. [S40](http://gdjr.gd.gov.cn/gdjr/zwgk/jrzcfg/content/post_3520533.html),
    [S52](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/publication-and-research/quarterly-bulletin/qb202503/E_Half-yearly_202503.pdf)
13. **跨境支付通 (2025)** — mainland IBPS ↔ Hong Kong FPS direct link; bilateral local-currency
    remittance, real-time, lower cost; limited scenarios/quotas, not a bulk capital exit.
    [S55](https://hangzhou.pbc.gov.cn/hangzhou/125264/5778339/index.html)
14. **Wealth Management Connect 南向通** — RMB 1m individual quota, RMB 150bn each direction,
    GBA eligibility, RMB closed loop; investment products only, funds return to the mainland;
    not a cash-out. [S39](https://guangzhou.pbc.gov.cn/guangzhou/129196/4332364/4332374/4343888/index.html)
15. **QDII / 代客境外理财** — institutional approved quota; an investment channel, not a
    personal cash transfer. [S41](http://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2019/0214/1098.html)

## C. Other lawful, narrow or approval-based routes (16–24)

16. ODI by a domestic individual (SAFE approval/registration). 17. Personal foreign-trade /
    cross-border e-commerce FX settlement (transaction materials → no quota consumption).
    18. Carry foreign banknotes out (≤USD 5,000; 5,000–10,000 with a permit). 19. Overseas
    ATM cash withdrawal (RMB 100,000/person/year). 20. Overseas card spending (no quota, but
    no transferable USD). 21. Remit foreign banknotes (≤USD 10,000/day). 22. Delegate purchase
    to a close relative / another person (指引第61条). 23. Personal FX settlement account for a
    registered trader (managed like an institution, not subject to the annual total).
    24. Domestic individual's lawful capital-account income from abroad → settlement with SAFE
    approval (an inbound rule, listed for completeness).

Sources: [S05], [S42](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2026/0402/2412.html),
[S36](http://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/2019/0521/1366.html), [S35](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hubei/2019/0305/851.html),
[S35b](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/2018/0409/1010.html), [S02], [S54](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/1223/2957.html),
[S16](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2019/1213/1198.html).

## D. Prohibited routes — documented to rule them out (25–35)

25. Quota splitting / 蚂蚁搬家 / borrowing others' quotas. 26. Underground banks, 换汇黄牛,
朋友圈换汇, unlicensed websites/apps. 27. Virtual currency / USDT / stablecoin conversion.
28. False transaction documents / fake invoices to fraudulently purchase FX. 29. False
declaration of purchase purpose (e.g. tourism → overseas property). 30. Lending one's own
quota. 31. Using convenience-amount FX for overseas property, securities, life insurance or
investment-return insurance. 32. Trade over-/under-invoicing. 33. Undeclared / excess cash
carrying. 34. Offshore exchanges providing virtual-currency services to mainland residents.
35. RWA tokenisation / RMB-pegged stablecoin issuance without approval.

Sources: [S08](https://www.safe.gov.cn/guangdong/2020/0304/1668.html),
[S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/2016/0328/615.html), [S11](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/0613/2811.html),
[S29](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html),
[S07](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html), [S09](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20210402/30fab7a9646f417ca9d2f67ff4c1e5f0.pdf),
[S12](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2015/1231/5442.html), [S53](https://www.safe.gov.cn/neimenggu/file/file/20200228/fa90770027ed4d919de981c09bc25751.pdf),
[S56](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/shanxi/c106408/c7616553/content.shtml).

None of these is recommended. Their common feature is that they convert an administrative
documentation problem into a 30%–100% fine plus possible 非法经营罪 liability — economically
and legally worse than any lawful route.

## Pros/cons discipline

Every `pros` and `cons` cell in `data/channels.csv` is grounded in the linked official text
(e.g. a limit, an approval requirement, an explicit prohibition or an explicit facilitation
policy). No convenience, speed, success probability or fee is asserted without an official
basis. Where a route's eligibility for this user is unknown, the `Who` / `Limit` cells state
the official condition and the item is cross-flagged (IR-014, IR-017).
