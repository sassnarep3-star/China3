# R1 — China-side conversion and remittance routes

**Research date:** 2026-11-07  
**Scope:** A U.S. citizen holds CNY in a Chinese bank and wants USD in the United States. Citizenship alone does not establish the person's Chinese FX customer category, tax residence, source of funds, or bank eligibility.  
**Evidence rule:** “Verified” below means the linked official text was opened and checked during this research. It does **not** mean a bank has approved the user's transaction or confirmed a current executable quote. Unconfirmed execution details are left blank and flagged rather than estimated.

## 1. Findings that control route selection

| ID | Verified rule or finding | Practical consequence | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CN-F01 | Under Article 37 of the Personal Foreign Exchange Measures, an **境外个人 / overseas individual** includes a foreign citizen holding a passport; a **境内个人 / domestic individual** is a Chinese citizen holding specified Chinese identity documents. The account bank uses the identity documents to classify the customer. | Determine the bank's recorded customer category first. Do not infer it solely from “U.S. citizen,” physical presence, or tax residence. A Chinese permanent-resident card has a specific purchase-quota exception. | [PBOC Personal Foreign Exchange Measures, Arts. 28 and 37 (SAFE)](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2022/0818/21330.html); [SAFE 2025 permanent-resident Q&A](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/1113/2926.html) |
| CN-F02 | The USD 50,000 annual figure is an **annual convenience quota** for individual FX settlement and for domestic-individual FX purchases. It is not a universal ceiling on genuine, lawful current-account transactions or transactions under opened capital-account channels. | Do not split a large balance among relatives. First establish whether the money is documented current income, unused original FX, or a permitted capital-account realization. | [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Arts. 54–60 (PDF)](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf); [SAFE Xiamen personal guide, pp. 2–4](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf) |
| CN-F03 | An overseas individual purchasing FX from lawful current-account RMB income must provide valid ID and amount-bearing source materials, **including tax evidence**. This documented purchase is outside the annual convenience quota. | For a foreign-passport holder with documented after-tax salary or other current income, the first route to price is conversion at a qualified bank plus a same-name U.S. wire—not borrowed quotas. | [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Art. 59](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf) |
| CN-F04 | An overseas individual can remit foreign currency already held in an FX account on valid ID; hand-held foreign cash has a separate USD 10,000 daily cumulative threshold and source-document rules. | Once USD is lawfully in the FX account, conversion review and wire review are distinct steps. The remittance rule does not retroactively validate the CNY source. | [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Art. 65](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf) |
| CN-F05 | Banks review authenticity and consistency, may choose document types and review points based on customer risk, and may refuse a transaction that violates rules or has doubtful authenticity; the bank must explain the refusal and appeal channel. | A published rule is not a transaction approval. Obtain a branch-specific document list before trading FX. | [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Arts. 68 and 73](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf) |
| CN-F06 | Current-account transactions require a genuine, lawful background. Fabricating a trade background and splitting transactions to evade quota or authenticity review are prohibited. | A false invoice, service contract, export, gift, or travel purpose is not an optimization route. | [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Arts. 47, 54 and 62](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf) |
| CN-F07 | For covered service-trade and similar outward payments, the single-payment tax-filing threshold is **more than USD 50,000 equivalent**. Multiple payments under one contract generally require filing only before the first covered payment; online and paper channels exist. A payment not exceeding the threshold does not require this filing under the cited announcement. | Tax filing is a procedural step, not a separate “conversion tax” and not proof that no tax is due. Exemptions and payment category must be checked against the full announcement. | [SAT/SAFE Announcement 2021 No. 19 explanation, §§2, 4 and 6](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810341/n810760/c5166114/content.html); [SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Art. 49(4)](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf) |
| CN-F08 | Chinese IIT applies by income category and residence. The statute lists comprehensive income rates of 3%–45%, business income rates of 5%–35%, and a 20% proportional rate for interest/dividends, rent, property transfers and incidental income; deductions, exemptions, residence and treaty positions alter the actual liability. | Do not apply a flat rate to an unexplained bank balance. Reconstruct the original income event and obtain tax records; the remittance does not create the original wage/dividend/property characterization. | [PRC Individual Income Tax Law, Arts. 1–6 and 12 (SAT, full text effective)](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193028/content.html) |
| CN-F09 | PBOC Notice 银发〔2020〕330号 supports bank handling of personal current-account cross-border RMB settlement, including lawful salary, under know-your-customer/business/diligence principles and anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and anti-tax-evasion duties. | Cross-border RMB followed by an offshore CNH/USD conversion is a legitimate candidate for eligible current income, not a bypass of source or tax review. Receiving-bank support and pricing remain unverified. | [PBOC et al. Notice 银发〔2020〕330号, §4(13) and final compliance paragraph](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4158143/index.html) |

## 2. Route matrix

**Cost-cell convention:** “Quote required” is deliberately not a number. No bank-specific executable spread, U.S. receiving fee, intermediary deduction, or total is assumed. “Underlying tax” means tax on the original income/asset event, not a tax created by pressing the convert button. U.S. consequences are separated in `research/us-tax-reporting.md`.

## 5. Irregularities and open questions handed to integration

1. **Unknown fund character:** “CNY in a bank” is not enough to establish lawful current income. Do not infer salary, savings, gift, inheritance, property or business proceeds.
2. **Identity/category ambiguity:** a U.S. passport generally supports the overseas-individual category under the Personal FX Measures, but the account bank's actual KYC record and any Chinese identity documents must be checked. Do not silently assume domestic or overseas treatment.
3. **No live quotes:** no amount, bank, province, customer category, destination bank, simultaneous mid-rate, or executable spread was provided. All personalized totals and lowest-cost rankings remain blank.
4. **Published BOC tariff age:** the page's stated effective date is 2014-11-01. Preserve it as an official published example with its local/agreement caveat; never display it as a confirmed current charge.
5. **Payment Connect:** official existence and supported currencies/scenarios are verified; same-name accumulated-savings eligibility and onward USD route are not.
6. **Capital-guide operational differences:** the 2004 emigration/inheritance measures and 2024 operational guide must be read together. Current SAFE checklists and the local branch should control document form, authentication and approval workflow.
7. **Tax filing ≠ tax clearance:** the USD 50,000 service-payment filing threshold, FX convenience quota, daily remittance threshold and cash/customs thresholds are separate. None is an annual “tax-free amount.”
8. **No general “conversion tax” identified in the reviewed FX rules:** the cited rules impose authenticity, tax-evidence and channel requirements. Bank spreads/fees and underlying income taxes must not be relabeled as a conversion tax. This is a scoped research finding, not an opinion that every possible transaction is tax-free.
9. **No named nonbank provider approved:** provider/corridor claims are excluded until official product and regulatory evidence confirms this exact use.
10. **Rejected methods remain visible:** quota borrowing, underground swaps and crypto are documented as unsuitable so the dashboard does not appear to omit common but unsafe suggestions.
