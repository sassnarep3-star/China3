# F52 decision checklist — what must be answered before any decision (2026-11-11)

Full table (17 questions, Q01–Q17): `f52-decisions.html` /
`data/f52_decisions.csv`. Six are settled by official sources; eleven are
blank because only your documents, your bank, or your advisers can settle
them. No manual input was requested during the research: the blanks below
*are* the deliverable — each names exactly how to discover the answer.

## Settled from official sources (no discovery needed)

1. **Conversion/wire tax?** None found. Current payments/transfers are not
   restricted (FX Regulation Art. 5); the bank needs a genuine lawful basis
   and documents. The tax attaches to the underlying income. (F-S08, F-S04)
2. **$50,000 on a U.S. passport?** No — Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders
   only (Art. 59). Passport route is documented income (F01). (F-S05)
3. **Is $50,000 a hard cap?** No — convenience amount; documented purchases
   above it proceed; genuine current-account items have no limit. (F-S06)
4. **Bank cost today (BOC)?** 0.2100% spread + 1‰ wire (CNY 50–260) + CNY 150
   cable → $53.42 at $10k, $901.20 at $400k. (F-S01, F-S02)
5. **Candidate Chinese rates?** Comprehensive 3%–45%; dividends/rent/property
   gain/incidental 20%; business 5%–35%; property tax on the gain only. (F-S07)

**Least-penalty lawful path, conditionally:** whichever of F03 (already FX),
F02 (change-back), or F01 (documented income) matches the true source —
each costs the same published bank price (F01 basis) with no conversion tax —
*provided* Q07 is clean and Q12 is cured first. If Q06 lands on home sale,
inheritance or emigration, use F04/F05/F06; if the money is a gift or
undocumented, no verified route exists today (F52-U07) and the next step is a
written bank answer, not a workaround (X01–X06 are prohibited).
