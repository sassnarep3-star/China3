# F52 methods & penalty breakdown (2026-11-11)

Tables: `f52-methods.html`, `data/f52_methods.csv`, `data/f52_penalty.csv`,
`data/f52_steps.csv`. All propositions cite the source register in
`research/f52-verification.md`.

## Lawful routes (bank price = Costs page; paperwork differs)

- **F01 Documented income** — Art. 59: ID + source materials incl. tax
  evidence. No ceiling. Best default for a passport holder.
- **F02 Change-back** — Art. 60: ID + exchange slip within 24 months;
  $500/day without a slip. Only for self-converted, unused RMB.
- **F03 Already-FX wire** — no conversion, wire fee only. Cheapest.
- **F04 Home sale** — capped at price minus sale taxes; contract +
  registration + tax certificate; IIT 20% on the gain.
- **F05 Inheritance / F06 Emigrant property** — SAFE approval routes;
  bank cost as F01 after approval; timing unpublished (blank).
- **F07 $50k/year convenience** — Chinese ID / PR-ID only; $50k/yr on ID
  alone; 8 years at $400k. Splitting via others is X01.
- **F08 Cross-border RMB** — lawful in principle; no official fee/rate
  fetched, so fully blank. Get both banks' written quotes first.
- **F09 Company dividend** — real tax: 20% statutory vs 10% treaty ceiling
  (beneficial-owner/residency procedure unverified, F52-U06). Never relabel
  personal savings.
- **F10 UnionPay ATM abroad** — CNY 10k/card/day, CNY 100k/person/year;
  BOC fee CNY 12+1% (max 112) priced; conversion rate blank. 27 yrs at $400k.
- **F11 Cash carry** — delivery only: ≤$5k/trip direct, $5–10k with bank
  permit, >$10k in principle barred. Still needs a lawful purchase first.

## Prohibited (X01–X06)

Splitting/“ants moving” (watch list: listing year + 2 years; organised flight
fined as 逃汇）, private swaps/underground banks (Art. 45), false documents/
purposes (Art. 40; documents alone ≤ CNY 50,000, Art. 48), unpermitted cash
(Art. 42 ≤20%), crypto conversion (banned; earlier-pass reading, flagged),
card borrowing (汇发〔2017〕29号）.

## Penalty decomposition — keep the layers separate

1. Spread 0.2100% (today) · 2. Published wire fee · 3. Intermediary/receiving
fees (**blank**) · 4. Pre-existing Chinese IIT on the income (0 if already
paid) · 5. U.S. §988 tax on the gain (**blank** — marginal rate; ordinary-vs-
capital conflict flagged) · 6. U.S. reporting penalties (FBAR $16,536 /
willful $165,353-or-50%; 8938 $10k→$60k; 3520 5%/mo→25%) · 7. Chinese
enforcement fines (≤30%, 30%–100% serious; cash ≤20%) · 8. Time (F07/F10/F11).
