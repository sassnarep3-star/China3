# f52c — the decision register: what must be settled before any money moves

Session `arena/f52c089a-china3`. Branch based on `main` @ `d9763dc` (PR #49 merged). Read date: **2026-11-11**.
Published page: [`questions.html`](../questions.html). Data: `data/f52c_*.csv`. Code: `scripts/f52c_engine.py`,
`scripts/f52c_data.py`, `scripts/f52c_build.py`, `scripts/f52c_verify.py`.

## Why this page exists

The repository already answers two questions well: what the bank charges, and which rules apply. It did not
answer the question the brief puts first — *what must be settled before any decision can reasonably be made,
and how do I find each missing fact?* `index.html` carried three open items and the Flagged page nineteen,
but neither turned them into a decision checklist that says, for each item, whether an official answer
exists, what artefact would settle it, and where to get that artefact. A search of all 60+ pages found no
such page. That is the deliverable here.

**Shape of the answer.** 45 questions in four groups:

- **A01–A14** facts only the account holder can supply (origin of the money, currency, amount, acquisition
  date, Chinese residence status, PR status, hukou, inheritance, property, tax evidence, U.S. filing
  history, branch, the tax that was quoted, exchange slips). These are `open` by design: no official page
  can answer them, and no route is recommended until A01 is answered.
- **B01–B16** Chinese-side rules — 16 questions, all `verified` against a page read today.
- **C01–C09** U.S.-side rules — 7 verified, 2 explicitly `unverified` (treaty dividend rate, foreign tax
  credit) because the governing text was not read today.
- **D01–D06** the decision itself.

Every row carries either official source ids or a flag id. The verifier refuses a page where an
`unverified` row has no flag, an `open` row has no way to settle it, or a cited id does not exist.

Two findings from that pass are worth naming here:

1. **银发〔2021〕237号 has been repealed.** The virtual-currency prohibition now rests on 银发〔2026〕42号
   (2026-02-06), which says so in terms. The canonical dashboard already cites the 2026 notice; nine older
   pages still cite the 2021 one. The substance of the prohibition is unchanged, so this is a citation
   defect, not a wrong answer (flag F52C-R02).
2. **The landing page's 30 % fine is attributed to pages that contain no fine.** `index.html` says quotas,
   private swaps and crypto are prohibited "with fines of up to 30 % of the amount" and cites S05, S06, S10
   and S21. S10 — read today — is the watchlist answer and mentions no fine; S05 and S06 are about document
   requirements. The 30 % is in Regulation arts 39, 40 and 45 (S17), which that card does not cite
   (flag F52C-R01). Left for review rather than edited, because `index.html` is generated by another
   session's builder.

## Arithmetic

The engine is written from the published inputs, not imported from the dashboard's engine, and it is
compared against it: at the dashboard's own 10 November inputs the two implementations agree on **440 of 440
cells**. At today's board it reproduces the figures PR #49 published independently ($53.42 / $901.20).

Convention, stated on the page: conversion at the bank's USD selling rate; "fair value" is the midpoint of
the board's own buying and selling rates (6.67705 today), *not* the board's "Middle Rate" column (6.7191),
which is not that midpoint; conversion cost = `usd × (ask − mid) / ask`; the wire fee is charged in RMB
(1 ‰, floor RMB 50, ceiling RMB 260, plus RMB 150 cable), then converted; rounding half-up to the cent at
the end, and percentages computed from the rounded cost that is printed.

| Amount | Convert + wire | Wire only | 50k-a-year (8 yrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $10,000 | $53.42 | $32.42 | $53.42 |
| $100,000 | $271.26 | $61.28 | $332.53 |
| $400,000 | $901.20 | $61.28 | $1,330.13 |

Three defects were found by the verifier against the engine and fixed before publication, which is the
reason the verifier does not import the engine:

- **ATM years were understated by one year at every amount.** `Decimal //` truncates toward zero, so the
  `-(-x // y)` ceiling idiom returned 26 where the true figure is 27 at $400,000. Replaced with
  `ROUND_CEILING`. The page text was corrected from "26 calendar years" to 27.
- **Percentage rounding disagreed** between the engine (from the rounded cost) and the verifier (from the
  exact fraction) in three places. The page convention — printed cost ÷ amount — was adopted in both.
- **The page/data money check had an incomplete allowlist**, so U.S. gain values and ladder amounts were
  reported as stray. Fixed in the verifier, not the page.

## Verification performed

`scripts/f52c_verify.py` (does not import the engine or the builder):

- recomputes all 40 × 11 = 440 cost cells and all 40 ladder rows from the raw board figures with exact
  fractions, and compares every column of every CSV row;
- checks the tariff behaviour directly (RMB 50 floor, RMB 260 cap, RMB 150 cable);
- enforces the sourcing contract: no question without a source, no `unverified` row without a flag, no
  dangling source or flag id, `read_on` = 2026-11-11 on every source, a quote long enough to be a quote;
- enforces page/data parity in both directions: every question, flag and source id appears on the page;
  every money value printed is in the CSV; every ladder value and percentage is printed;
- resolves every local link and anchor, and restricts external links to the official allowlist;
- requires the board timestamp and read date on the page, and rejects placeholder text and merge markers;
- requires `f52c_build.py --check` to pass (byte-identical rebuild).

All 21 pre-existing validators still pass on the combined tree, including `e347_verify` (659 table rows,
380 blanks flagged, 2 147 links).

Not checked: how the page looks in a browser — there is no browser in this sandbox. The CSS reuses
`dashboard/site.css` unchanged and adds six lines in `assets/f52c.css`; the stacked-table behaviour on
phones is the dashboard's existing `table.stack` rule.
