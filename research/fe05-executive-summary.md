# Executive summary — 11 November 2026

A U.S. citizen's CNY balance does not, by itself, establish the source of funds, their Chinese FX classification, tax basis, or eligibility for any route. Begin with the account bank and documentary evidence, then a U.S.–China tax adviser. For a foreign individual's lawful mainland current-account RMB income, SAFE requires identity and amount-bearing source materials including tax evidence; the annual convenience purchase quota applies to foreign permanent resident ID holders, not all foreign passport holders ([SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html)). Inheritance and emigrant transfers have distinct approval criteria; the 2014 reform removed mandatory staging ([SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0903/2594.html)). Do not split transactions to evade controls or use informal exchangers ([SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/guangdong/2020/0304/1668.html)).
