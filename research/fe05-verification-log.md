# Source verification notes — 2026-11-11

Directly read official issuer texts, not just search snippets. Source URLs and access dates appear in `data/fe05_sources.csv`; rows cite the relevant authority. Key observations:

* [SAFE current-account guide](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2020/art_0dd36157a03f494db7571002c7f785b8.html) articles 54–65: domestic convenience quota, foreign income purchases and reconversion, anti-splitting and separate domestic/foreign outward-remittance requirements. Article 59 grants the purchase convenience quota to holders of the foreign permanent resident identity card.
* [SAFE 2014 reform](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0903/2594.html) §6 cancels mandatory three-tranche/two-year remittance and the head-office reporting requirement; do not apply older 2004 terms without the amendment. [SAFE 2025 operational guide](https://www.safe.gov.cn/anhui/file/file/20250925/43f09589bb2046c2a01a58d3a80c2b5f.pdf) §8 discusses bank verification of property-sale proceeds.
* [BOC board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), 2026-11-11 15:30:04 Beijing, spot bid 666.06 and ask 668.86 CNY per 100 USD; midpoint is calculated, not an executable quotation. [BOC tariff](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html): outward wire 1‰, min ¥50, max ¥260, plus ¥150 cable to non-HK/Macau/Taiwan destinations. Confirm fees and exchange rates with bank.
* [PRC tax administration law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5195081/content.html) arts. 32, 63–64 establish late surcharge and specified penalties; amounts are not generic transaction costs. [IIT law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html) arts. 2–3 and 6 identify categories, tax bases and rates. [VAT announcement](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/zcfgk/zzs/202601/t478899.html) applies from 2026.
* [IRS comparison](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements): FBAR aggregate exceeds $10,000 any time; Form 8938 has different thresholds. [IRC §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title26/html/USCODE-2023-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm) includes foreign currency deposits and a conditional personal-transaction exception. [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821) displayed its penalty table as of 2026-11-09, with last adjustment dated January 2025; confirm future updates.
* [IRS treaty](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf) contains articles 9–11 and 22 and a saving clause; limits are conditional, not an automatic rate for a U.S. citizen. [IRS exchange guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/yearly-average-currency-exchange-rates) says generally use the spot rate when income is received, paid or accrued and notes that IRS has no official exchange rate.
