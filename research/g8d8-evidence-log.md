## G. Arithmetic convention

Cost of buying A dollars with RMB and wiring once at a bank with selling rate `ask`, buying rate `bid`, fee rate r, minimum m, cap M, cable c:
`mid = (ask + bid) / 2`; `spread = A × (ask − mid) / ask`; `fee = (min(max(A × ask × r, m), M) + c) / ask`; `total = round(fee, 2) + round(spread, 2)`.
This is the convention of the main dashboard (spread against the buy/sell midpoint, not the 折算价 column). Where a bank's own board was not read, only the fee is computed, on `A × 6.6911` and converted at 6.6911 (flag F06).
