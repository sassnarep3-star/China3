# R3 — Comparison methodology, executive evidence and risk flags

**Research date:** 2026-11-07  
**Inputs:** R1 `research/china-routes.md` and R2 `research/us-tax-reporting.md`.  
**Purpose:** Turn sourced research into a public decision resource without inventing eligibility, quotes, taxes or a universal “cheapest” route.

## 1. Executive answer approved for publication

> **Start with the source of the money, not a quota workaround.** For a foreign-passport customer with documented lawful current-account RMB income and tax evidence, the first option to price is a regulated Chinese bank's direct CNY→USD conversion followed by a wire to the person's U.S. account. SAFE Article 59 provides that documented purchase route outside the annual purchase convenience quota; Article 65 separately covers an overseas individual's foreign-currency account remittance. Bank review and branch approval still control execution. ([SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Arts. 59, 65 and 68](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf))
>
> **USD 50,000 is not a universal annual repatriation ceiling.** It is an annual convenience quota for individual settlement and domestic-individual purchases. Genuine documented current-account transactions and specifically opened capital channels use their own rules. A U.S. citizen whose bank treats them as a domestic individual cannot assume unlimited same-name “savings repatriation”; the payment purpose and legal channel must be established. ([SAFE Current Account Guide 2020, Arts. 54–64](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf); [SAFE Xiamen personal guide, pp. 2–4](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf))
>
> **No numeric cheapest route or total tax rate is supportable yet.** Amount, source, identity, historical tax, CNY basis, simultaneous dealing quotes, intermediary/receiving fees and U.S. filing history are missing. Compare transaction execution and historical compliance separately; do not call bank friction a “conversion tax.” ([BOC tariff caveat](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html); [IRS worldwide-income guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad); [26 U.S.C. §988](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-138/t26/s988/))
>
> **Before moving the money, reconcile U.S. reporting as well as Chinese documents.** FBAR uses aggregate foreign-account maximums above USD 10,000 at any time in the year, and Form 8938 is a separate test. A same-owner wire is not a cure for unreported historical income, and conversion can create §988 currency gain/loss. ([FinCEN FBAR](https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts); [IRS Form 8938/FBAR comparison](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements); [26 U.S.C. §988(c)(1)(C)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-138/t26/s988/))
>
> **Do not use borrowed quotas, false purposes, underground banks or crypto as an offshore off-ramp.** Those are rejected routes, not “alternatives.” ([SAFE anti-splitting and enforcement](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hunan/2017/1204/561.html); [PBOC virtual-currency notice 银发〔2021〕237号](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4348658/index.html))

## 4. Comparison formulas and calculator contract

### 4.1 Inputs

Use the same amount and timestamp for competing quotes.

- `Q`: eligible CNY amount approved for conversion, not automatically the entire bank balance.
- `r_mid`: benchmark USD per CNY mid-rate at the quote timestamp.
- `r_bank`: executable USD per CNY customer rate after bank spread.
- `F_cny`: all Chinese-side charges denominated in CNY before conversion.
- `F_out`: outgoing/telecom charges denominated in USD (convert CNY charges at `r_bank` if necessary).
- `F_corr`: correspondent/full-amount/OUR adjustments in USD.
- `F_recv`: U.S. receiving and known processing charges in USD.
- `T_now`: incremental U.S./Chinese tax triggered by the current conversion/transaction in USD, computed externally; **not** historical income tax.
- `r_cnh`: executable offshore USD per CNH rate for an eligible CNH alternative.
- `F_off`: offshore conversion and transfer charges in USD.

### 4.2 Outputs

```text
Direct-bank net USD
N_direct = (Q - F_cny) × r_bank - F_out - F_corr - F_recv - T_now

Eligible offshore-CNH net USD
N_cnh = (Q - F_cny) × r_cnh - F_off - F_recv - T_now

Benchmark-value comparison
N_benchmark = (Q - F_cny) × r_mid
```

To avoid double counting, the site calculator will use the following normalized implementation:

```text
gross_before_external_fees = (Q - F_cny) × r_bank
net_received = gross_before_external_fees - F_out - F_corr - F_recv - T_now
benchmark_value = (Q - F_cny) × r_mid
execution_friction = benchmark_value - net_received
fx_spread_cost = (Q - F_cny) × (r_mid - r_bank)
external_fees = F_out + F_corr + F_recv
identity_check = execution_friction = fx_spread_cost + external_fees + T_now
```

If a fee is already included in the executable net rate, it must be excluded from `F_out`; the quote template must mark this explicitly. No calculator result appears until all required numeric inputs are present and valid. Negative net proceeds, an inconsistent fee currency or an eligibility stop produces an error, not a ranking.

### 4.3 Two views, never one blended tax rate

1. **Execution view:** `net_received`, `execution_friction`, `fx_spread_cost`, itemized external fees and `T_now`.
2. **Full-compliance workpaper:** historical Chinese/U.S. tax shortfall, professional costs and potential reporting issues shown separately. Already paid historical tax is not deducted again; unpaid amounts are not treated as an FX cost.

The public calculator must default to blank fields and “Enter verified quotes,” preserve the formula visibly, and never ship a preset exchange rate or a made-up cheapest result.

## 7. Site behavior and acceptance criteria for integration

1. **Eligibility before cost:** routes marked “Start here,” “Alternative,” “Source-specific,” “Limited substitute,” “Flagged for review,” or “Not suitable”; no numeric rank across ineligible options.
2. **Every factual route/tax row has an official source:** research IDs remain attached to site rows for audit. Editorial formulas and clearly labeled recommendations cite their controlling evidence nearby.
3. **Unknowns stay visible:** no zero substitution, silent completion, guessed fees/taxes, fake testimonials, live-rate badges or “approved” claims.
4. **All route classes included:** ordinary bank/current account, source-specific capital routes, card/ATM/cash/draft, rejected methods, U.S. reporting and correction paths.
5. **Comparison is reproducible:** formula, units, timestamp, source and quote status are visible; all required fields are validated; blank initial state produces no answer.
6. **Executive summary matches evidence:** foreign-passport documented current income → direct bank first; domestic-individual/generic savings → purpose/channel review; no universal USD 50,000 cap; no numeric cheapest claim.
7. **Flagged for Review is real:** RF-01–RF-17 link to this methodology and the relevant official source; urgent safety issues are not hidden in footnotes.
8. **Professional boundary:** site says it is an evidence-based research resource, not individualized legal/tax/investment advice; legal review is recommended for entity/trust, past noncompliance or possible willfulness.
9. **Accessibility and mobile:** keyboard-operable navigation/calculator, visible focus, labeled inputs/errors, no color-only risk meaning, responsive tables/cards, readable type and no inaccessible modal-only content.
10. **No fake backend:** static site works without accounts, API keys, personal-data transmission or “AI advice.” Calculator runs locally; no user financial records are collected.
