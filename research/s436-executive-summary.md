## 1. Bottom-Line Answer (Most Important Info First)

1. **There is NO Chinese "conversion tax" on converting already-taxed personal CNY savings into USD, and NO U.S. transfer tax on wiring your own money to a U.S. bank account.**
   - Neither SAFE, PBOC, China's State Taxation Administration (STA), nor the U.S. IRS imposes a tax on the act of converting personal post-tax CNY principal to USD or wiring it between your own accounts ([SAFE 2022 Q&A](https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html); [PRC IIT Law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html); [IRS Pub. 54](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p54); [FinCEN §361(b) Report](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/shared/ReportToCongress361.PDF)).
2. **Why people experience a "considerable tax penalty" — six separate mechanisms get conflated:**
   - **Mechanism 1 (Paperwork Gate Mistaken for a Tax — `$0` actual tax):** Above **USD 50,000** per single payment in listed categories (including foreign individuals' China remuneration, dividends, or real-estate proceeds), China requires an online **Outbound Payment Tax Recordal (`《服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表》编号和验证码`)** via the Electronic Tax Bureau (`电子税务局`) under [STA/SAFE Announcement [2013] No. 40](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5194375/content.html) and [STA/SAFE Announcement [2021] No. 19](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/c102449/c5237521/content.html). That recordal costs **`$0`** when underlying taxes were already paid.
   - **Mechanism 2 (Unpaid PRC Tax on the *Origin* of the CNY — `0%` to `45%`):** If the CNY was **never taxed when earned**, obtaining the tax proof triggers the *original* PRC tax: **3%–45%** progressive IIT on untaxed wages/comprehensive income, **20%** flat IIT on property capital gains, rent, dividends, or incidental income ([PRC IIT Law Art. 3](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html)), or **10%** PRC dividend withholding if the CNY is still inside a Chinese company (`WFOE/FIE`) ([U.S.–China Tax Treaty Art. 9(2)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf)). Conversely, mainland RMB savings-account interest has been **exempt (`0%`) from PRC IIT since 2008-10-09** ([财税〔2008〕132号](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/tax/zcfw/zcfgk/grsds/200810/t288953.html)).
   - **Mechanism 3 (Bank FX Spread + Wire/Cable/Intermediary Tariffs — `0.78%` at `$10k` dropping to `0.23%` at `$400k` on BOC's published board):** Under Bank of China's official `2026-11-09 09:29:12` FX board (`6.6924` spot ask vs `6.67835` bid-ask midpoint = **`0.2100%`** one-way conversion cost, [BOC FX Board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/enindex_1619.html)) plus BOC's published personal e-banking outward wire fee (`0.1%`, min `¥50`, **capped at `¥260/wire`** + `¥150` cable = **`$61.26/wire`** max, [BOC E-Banking Tariff](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html)) plus BOC's published `OUR` full-amount-received fee (**`$25/wire`** to any U.S. bank, [BOC Notice](https://www.boc.cn/cbservice/bi2/201711/t20171109_10646775.html)) and `$0` incoming wire at [BOC U.S.A.](https://www.bocusa.com/personal-banking/remittance-exchange), **converting and wiring `$400,000 USD` in a single lawful transfer costs `$927.78` (`0.23%`) in published bank charges**.
   - **Mechanism 4 (U.S. Worldwide Income & IRC §988 Personal FX Gain):** U.S. citizens owe U.S. tax on previously unreported foreign income (including PRC-exempt bank interest), offset by the **Foreign Tax Credit** ([Form 1116 / Pub. 514](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p514)) or **Tax Year 2026 Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (`$132,900`)** ([IRS FEIE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/figuring-the-foreign-earned-income-exclusion)). Personal currency-appreciation gain upon converting CNY→USD is **exempt if `≤ $200` per transaction** and taxed as **capital gain** (not ordinary income) above `$200` ([IRS Pub. 525](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525)).
   - **Mechanism 5 (U.S. Information-Reporting Penalties — Up to `50%` of Balance or `$165,353+`):** Failing to file **FBAR (FinCEN Form 114)** when aggregate foreign accounts exceed **`$10,000`** carries up to **`$16,536`** non-willful or **`max($165,353, 50% of balance)`** willful penalties ([31 CFR 1010.821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821); 2026 adjustment cancelled per [OMB M-26-11](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/07/2026-13629/no-adjustment-to-civil-monetary-penalty-amounts)). Unreported **Form 8938** (`$10,000`–`$60,000` + `40%` understatement penalty) and **Form 3520** (`5%/month` up to **`25%`** on foreign gifts/inheritances `> $100,000`, [IRS Penalties](https://www.irs.gov/payments/international-information-reporting-penalties)) dwarf all bank fees. **Crucially, curing non-willful past omissions *before* IRS contact costs `0%` penalty under [Streamlined Foreign Offshore](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-taxpayers-residing-outside-the-united-states) or `5%` under [Streamlined Domestic Offshore](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-taxpayers-residing-in-the-united-states).**
   - **Mechanism 6 (PRC FX Circumvention Fines & Criminal Thresholds — `30%–100%` + Prison):** Quota-splitting ("smurfing"), underground banking (`对敲`), or USDT/crypto swaps trigger **up to `30%` (or `30%–100%` serious) PRC fines** ([FX Regs Arts. 39, 45](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html)), PBOC's 2026 crypto ban ([银发〔2026〕42号](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)), and **PRC Criminal Law Art. 225 (`非法经营罪`)** once illegal FX volume hits **`RMB 2,500,000` (`~$372,090 USD`, i.e., at the `$380k–$400k` steps with aggravating factors) or `RMB 5,000,000` (`~$744,180 USD` unconditionally)** ([法释〔2019〕1号](https://www.hncourt.gov.cn/public/detail.php?id=176932)).
