Bank checklist to copy into an email

  • What exact Chinese FX category and transaction purpose are you using to review this payment?
  • Does the requested CNY-to-USD purchase count toward the annual convenience amount? If more is requested, which exact transaction documents establish eligibility?
  • Can the bank process the full proposed amount in one genuine transaction, and is any local SAFE pre-approval needed?
  • For an outward payment, what document proves the purpose and what rule determines the amount threshold? Do purchase and payment purposes need to match?
  • Please quote the rate, timestamp, spread, every sending/correspondent/recipient fee, net USD, and identify any actual tax separately with the tax type, legal basis, taxable base, payer and tax receipt/assessment.
