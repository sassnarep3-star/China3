Transfer routes, compared

These are not equally available substitutes. First match the route to the funds’ legal owner, source, and purpose; a payment rail cannot make an otherwise ineligible transfer permissible.

Most likely first call: the China account-holding bank’s foreign-exchange / cross-border remittance desk. Ask for the written document list and classification before converting. The annual USD 50,000 facilitation amount is not an absolute limit for an authentic, documented current-account transaction; however it does not by itself create a right to remit any purpose or capital balance.

1. Mainland bank buys USD from CNY under individual convenience amount, then remits
Bank FX + wire		Individual requests FX purchase through a qualified domestic bank for a truthful, eligible purpose. Annual convenience amount: equivalent of USD 50,000 per person for individual purchase/sale of FX. Above it, proper transaction documents—not merely an ID—are required for applicable current-account cases.Bank customer FX rate/spread, bank transfer fee and any intermediary/recipient fee: not confirmed; request a written all-in quote. The USD 50,000 figure is a processing convenience threshold, not a tax-free allowance or universal right to remit arbitrary savings.Direct regulated-bank channel; clean audit trail; no informal counterparty; potentially lowest operational complexity when documents and purpose fit.FX purchase and cross-border remittance are separate checks. A generic wish to move accumulated savings may not establish a permissible current-account transaction. No splitting across relatives or accounts.SAFE Q&A (Chinese)Annual facilitation amount; above-amount bona fide transaction support; distinction from cap.
SAFE press conferenceUSD 50,000, supporting materials, no quota splitting.
2. Documented current-account purchase / remittance above USD 50,000
Evidence-based		For a real, lawful current-account purpose where bank accepts source/purpose documents. SAFE says authentic, legitimate current-account purchases/settlements above the facilitation amount can be handled with supporting materials; genuine amounts are not restricted by the convenience quota alone.Same bank FX spread and transfer charges, plus any underlying tax on the transaction/income if applicable; actual charge values not verified. Documentation is not itself a tax charge.May allow a larger legitimate payment without waiting multiple years; the bank can review the full true amount and its actual purpose.Cannot transform personal capital/savings into a current-account expense by relabeling it. Bank reviews transaction authenticity and consistency of purchase and outward-payment purpose; acceptance is fact-specific.SAFE Q&A (Chinese)Authentic/legal current-account transactions; annual amount is not a hard ceiling.
SAFE FAQ (Chinese)Outward remittance requirements and purchase/payout purpose consistency.
3. Remit USD / other FX already held in the China bank
Existing foreign-currency balance		Where the bank account already contains foreign currency and the outward transfer is permitted. SAFE’s cited current-account rule allows foreign-currency-account remittance up to USD 50,000 equivalent per day with valid ID; above that amount, ID plus transaction-value evidence is required.No CNY-to-USD conversion needed if the balance is already USD. Outgoing/incoming bank and correspondent fees, and any other currency conversion spread, are not confirmed.Avoids a new CNY/USD exchange step if funds are already in USD; straightforward bank record.This does not solve conversion of a CNY balance. The cited USD 50,000/day rule concerns documentation for a current-account outward remittance, not a general right to export capital or an annual conversion exemption.SAFE FAQ (Chinese)Daily threshold and evidence requirement for current-account remittance of FX funds.
4. Lawful RMB current-account income of a foreign individual
Income-backed		PBOC’s individual FX rules state a non-domestic individual may convert legitimate RMB current-account income obtained in China to foreign exchange and remit abroad through a bank, with valid identity and relevant documents.Actual conversion rate/spread and service charges not confirmed. Underlying income tax depends on the income and tax status; this rule does not state a universal conversion tax.Specific rule for certain foreign individuals’ legitimate current-account income; bank-based and documentable.“Non-domestic individual” is a defined regulatory category—not automatically the same as U.S. citizenship. Evidence and current bank practice need confirmation. Not a general path for every resident’s savings/capital.PBOC Decree [2006] No. 3, Art. 13Official English rendering of the individual FX administration rules.
5. Personal-property transfer after emigration / qualifying inheritance
Special SAFE approval		Separate, narrowly defined procedure: for a qualifying emigrant moving lawful China assets held before attaining foreign permanent-residence/citizen status, or a foreign citizen/HK/Macao/Taiwan resident transferring legally inherited assets. SAFE approval and specified ownership/source/tax materials apply.Tax is not a set percentage stated by the transfer rule. Property realization may have its own taxes; tax documents are required in the process. Bank FX rate and charges not confirmed.Can provide a purpose-built, one-time legal transfer process for eligible emigration or inheritance assets, including amounts above the routine convenience amount, after approval.Not a generic “get savings out” exception. Requires eligibility, clean legal title, application to the competent local SAFE branch and documentation. Emigration timing/asset ownership history matter. Inheritance procedure is for inherited property, not unrelated funds.PBOC publication of provisional rulesPersonal-asset transfer framework; legal ownership and approval.
SAFE personal-transfer Q&A (PDF, Chinese)Eligibility, approval, tax evidence, payment directly to applicant’s foreign account.
SAFE capital-account policy circularProcedural adjustment for approved transfers.
6. Foreign-invested company distributes dividends / profits
Company-owned funds only		Relevant only when the money belongs to a company and is a legally distributable profit paid to its overseas investor—not when it is already an individual’s bank savings. PBOC’s RMB FDI rules require bank review of profit-disposal resolution and tax-payment evidence for outbound RMB profits.Company and shareholder taxes depend on entity, source, residence, treaty and applicable tax law; this rule confirms a tax-payment document is part of the bank file but supplies no personal tax calculation. FX spread/fees not confirmed.Recognized company-to-investor process with corporate records and bank checks.Cannot route personal money through a company just to avoid individual rules. Requires real ownership, lawful distributable profits, resolutions, tax and relevant regulatory documentation.PBOC Administrative Rules on RMB-denominated FDI, Art. 12–13Profits and capital reduction/equity transfer/liquidation procedures.
7. Return of company investment / equity transfer / liquidation proceeds
Company capital account		Only for a genuine investor/company transaction such as capital reduction, equity transfer or liquidation. PBOC rule says outward RMB remittance follows bank examination of regulatory approval/filing documents and tax-payment certificate.Underlying tax depends on actual disposition and ownership. No tax rate or bank charge inferred. Obtain transaction-specific professional calculation before closing.Specific regulated channel when the funds are truly company investment proceeds; may return capital through the correct process rather than disguise it as an individual expense.Not available merely because personal savings are in China. Corporate structure, approval/filing, tax evidence and source documents are essential; transfer purpose cannot be misrepresented.PBOC Administrative Rules on RMB-denominated FDI, Art. 13Capital reduction, equity transfer and liquidation remittance evidence.
8. Cross-border RMB payment rail (e.g. bank-mediated CIPS)
Settlement rail, not a workaround		Ask whether both banks support a bank-mediated cross-border RMB payment for the actual permitted transaction. PBOC describes CIPS as clearing/settlement for cross-border RMB payments including trade, capital-account transfers and individual remittances.Any conversion at the U.S. receiving bank, fees, exchange rate and eligibility are not confirmed. Routing RMB does not remove Chinese FX-management or purpose checks.May avoid a CNY-to-USD conversion at the sending bank if the recipient bank can receive RMB and offers an acceptable conversion (must be confirmed by both banks).CIPS is infrastructure, not a consumer service or legal exception. Availability, receiving-bank acceptance and conversion costs are account-specific; do not assume it bypasses controls.PBOC CIPS provisional business rules (PDF)Scope includes cross-border RMB remittances by financial institutions/individuals.

Routes not recommended

Do not use another person’s annual amount, many small transfers, informal underground exchangers, false-purpose invoices or third-party accounts to evade the rules. SAFE’s circular expressly identifies splitting via multiple individuals—including relatives—as a prohibited circumvention pattern. Potential enforcement and fraud risk outweigh any assumed rate saving.

SAFE Circular, 2009 · SAFE press conference, 2015

Ask for an apples-to-apples comparison

Get the quote for the same CNY principal and same date: bank’s USD net proceeds after all send/intermediary/receiving fees, the customer rate and timestamp, and any tax/withholding itemized separately with its legal basis. Never treat an unexplained “tax penalty” as established tax without a tax authority receipt, assessment or bank explanation.

No numerical bank fee or FX spread is supplied in this guide: those values have not been confirmed from a current official tariff and an account-specific quote.

Table rows are general route summaries, not assurances that a specific bank will accept an application. Official rules are primary evidence; service implementation, forms, and required translations should be confirmed with the local bank and competent SAFE office.
