Route comparison

Legal pathways to USD

There is no universal route independent of the funds’ source. Start with the China account bank and ask it to classify the transaction before choosing an alternative.

How to read this table“USD 50,000” is an annual facilitation amount for individual purchases/sales of foreign exchange, not a tax and not necessarily the maximum lawful transaction. Banks review authenticity documents above the facilitation amount. Route eligibility depends on the actual facts. Sources below are official; variable fees/rates and personal eligibility are intentionally not estimated. [S1] [S2]

Do not use these as “routes”Splitting the amount among relatives/friends or using their USD 50,000 allowances, false invoices/contracts, underground banks, or informal value-transfer brokers to evade FX controls. SAFE expressly prohibits split purchases/sales; official cases document fines and other enforcement. A crypto/peer-to-peer swap is not verified here as a lawful China outbound route and introduces serious compliance/counterparty risk. [S3] [S4]

For transfer fees, request an itemized quote for: exchange rate, bank conversion fee, outbound remittance fee, cable/SWIFT fee, correspondent deductions, receiving-bank fee, and the final amount of USD credited. The sources do not set a universal rate or fee schedule, so the site intentionally provides no numerical price estimate.
