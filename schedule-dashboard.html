Bankable first, evidence first

Move documented China RMB legally — start with the origin, not a workaround.

For a U.S. citizen holding CNY in mainland China, the lowest verified cost is generally a licensed-bank conversion and wire of lawful, documented, already-taxed funds. Conversion itself is not a new PRC income tax. The largest risks are missing source/tax evidence, an improper FX route, and missed U.S. reporting — not the published BOC bank fee.

Best-cost order: (1) transfer existing USD first; (2) foreign-individual documented salary/current-account route; (3) other documentary routes matching the real origin; (4) do not use quota splitting, underground banks, or crypto.

Read before acting

1. Identify the money’s real origin

Salary, sale proceeds, company profits, inheritance and already-held USD have different evidence and tax paths. A bank balance alone does not establish eligibility.

S436-08 · S436-09

2. Do not confuse cost with tax

Published spread/wire/cable costs are calculable. Unpaid income tax, U.S. FX gain, U.S. cure penalties, property tax, intermediary fees and provider rates are fact-dependent and shown blank.

S436-19 · S436-23

3. Do not evade controls

Borrowing quotas, underground matched orders and crypto bypasses are not savings strategies. They can create administrative and criminal exposure.
