Execution playbooks

Step-by-step — without inventing documents or eligibility

Steps describe a compliance-first sequence. They do not override a bank’s current written instructions. Prohibited methods are shown only to make the prohibition unmistakable.

M01 · Lawful when documented

Foreign individual: tax-paid salary / lawful current-account income → BOC USD wire

  1. Confirm account identity classification and obtain a written/current bank document checklist. (S436-08)
  2. Gather the lawful RMB-origin evidence; do not invent contracts, invoices, tax proof, or a purpose. (S436-08)
  3. Obtain a live bank FX quote and itemized wire/OUR/SHA charges; compare it against the timestamped benchmark. (S436-01)
  4. Complete required tax recordal or SAFE/bank review before payment where the rules apply. (S436-09)
  5. Report U.S. worldwide income and required foreign-account forms; retain records. (S436-20)

M10 · Lawful but impractical

China-issued UnionPay card overseas ATM cash withdrawal

  1. Confirm the issuing card’s overseas ATM availability and limits directly with the issuer. (S436-04)
  2. Withdraw only for a lawful travel purpose and retain ATM receipts. (S436-04)
  3. Do not treat ATM cash as a scalable U.S. bank-transfer technique. (S436-04)
  4. Account for cash travel/reporting rules and foreign-account reporting. (S436-20)

M12 · Lawful only within cash-control rules

Buy USD and carry it physically with required Chinese permit / U.S. CMIR

  1. First obtain the Chinese bank/SAFE documentation required for the amount; do not assume a declaration replaces a permit. (S436-36)
  2. Use the bank’s live USD selling quote and preserve exchange receipts. (S436-36)
  3. Carry only within the applicable Chinese cash/permit conditions and file FinCEN Form 105 if more than USD 10,000 enters/leaves the U.S. (S436-28)
  4. Deposit with a U.S. bank only after confirming its cash-source and AML documentation requirements. (S436-36)
  5. Report foreign-account/income items where applicable. (S436-20)

M14 · Prohibited

  1. Do not use this method. (S436-15)
  2. Use a licensed bank and a lawful documented route instead. (S436-15)
  3. Seek qualified PRC and U.S. legal/tax advice if prior conduct may create exposure. (S436-15)

M16 · Prohibited

Convert CNY to USDT/other virtual currency then sell overseas

  1. Do not use virtual currency to evade or bypass China FX controls. (S436-17)
  2. Do not send money to an informal OTC counterparty. (S436-17)
  3. Use a lawful, documented bank route instead. (S436-17)
