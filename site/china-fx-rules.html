China-side foreign-exchange rules

What the State Administration of Foreign Exchange and the State Council actually provide, in their own words. The two most important rows are CN-FX-01 (the USD 50,000 quota) and CN-FX-02 (the official statement that the quota is not an amount limit).

CN-FX-01Annual facilitation quota (年度便利化额度)USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year, separately for 结汇 (FX->CNY) and for 境内个人购汇 (CNY->FX)SAFE's Current Account FX Business Guidelines (2020 edition) Art. 54: 个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度便利化额度管理，便利化额度分别为每人每年等值5万美元. Note the asymmetry: the quota is stated for '个人结汇' (all individuals settling FX) and for '境内个人购汇' (domestic individuals buying FX) only.Buying USD with CNY inside China (购汇) by a 境内个人 is ID-only within USD 50k/yr. This is the single most important constraint on a simple CNY->USD conversion.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-02Quota is not a ceiling on lawful conversionblankSAFE's own policy Q&A states: 目前对于真实、合法的经常项目和已开放的资本项目下购汇和结汇都没有金额限制. 年度便利化额度不是个人每年购汇或者结汇的限额 — the USD 50k figure is a documentary-convenience threshold, not a legal maximum. Above it, the same transaction remains lawful if documentary evidence of the transaction is supplied.This is the key corrector to the widespread belief that 'only USD 50,000 can ever leave China per year'. Above the quota the constraint becomes evidence, not amount.safe.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-03Over-quota current-account purchase (documentary route)No statutory amount limit; requires 本人有效身份证件 + 有交易额的购汇资金用途材料Guidelines 2020 Art. 56: 境内个人凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的购汇资金用途材料，在银行办理不占用年度便利化额度的经常项目购汇.The lawful large-transfer route for an individual who is a Chinese national or a foreigner resident in China. It works only if the underlying purpose is a genuine current-account item (study, travel, medical, family support, wages, services).gov.cnverified

CN-FX-04Remitting existing foreign-currency savings out of ChinaUp to USD 50,000 equivalent per day by ID alone; above that, documentary evidenceGuidelines 2020 Art. 64(1): 外汇账户内外汇汇出境外当日累计等值5万美元以下（含）的，凭本人有效身份证件办理；超过上述金额的，凭本人有效身份证件、有交易额的相关材料办理。Note this is a DAILY figure, not annual.Once CNY has been lawfully converted to USD and sits in an FX savings account, outward remittance is comparatively unconstrained. The binding constraint is therefore the 购汇 step, not the wire.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-05Purpose restrictions written into the FX purchase applicationSix prohibitions, including: 不得用于境外买房、证券投资、购买人寿保险和投资性返还分红类保险等尚未开放的资本项目The 个人购汇申请书 (Individual FX Purchase Application) is reproduced as an annex to a SAFE branch publication. It lists six prohibitions and states that violators are placed on the 关注名单 with no facilitation quota for the year of listing plus the two following years.Overseas property, overseas securities, and life/return-of-premium insurance are closed capital-account purposes. Framing a transfer as one of these is a false declaration. Formulating a lawful plan therefore means identifying a genuine current-account or open-capital-account purpose.m.safe.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-06Splitting / structuring prohibition and the 'watch list'Watch-list period = year of listing + 2 following years, during which the facilitation quota is unavailableGuidelines 2020 Art. 62: 个人不得以分拆等方式规避便利化额度管理及真实性管理。外汇局对规避管理的个人实行'关注名单'管理。 (一) lending your own quota to help another triggers a written risk warning; a repeat triggers watch-listing. (二) borrowing another's quota places you on the watch list directly. (三) 关注期限为列入'关注名单'的当年及之后连续2年.Using relatives' or friends' USD 50k quotas to move a larger sum ('smurfing' / 蚂蚁搬家) is expressly addressed and is a defined violation, not a grey area.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-07Penalty for evasion of foreign exchange (逃汇)Fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount; if serious, 30% up to 100% of the amount; repatriation orderedForeign Exchange Administration Regulations (State Council Order No. 532, 2008) Art. 39: 有违反规定将境内外汇转移境外，或者以欺骗手段将境内资本转移境外等逃汇行为的，由外汇管理机关责令限期调回外汇，处逃汇金额30%以下的罚款；情节严重的，处逃汇金额30%以上等值以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。The downside of an unlawful route is quantified in the statute: a fine measured as a percentage of the sum moved, plus forced repatriation, plus criminal exposure. This is the number to compare against the ~0.3-0.6% cost of the lawful route.beijing.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-11Carrying foreign cash out of ChinaUp to USD 5,000: no permit. USD 5,000-10,000: bank-issued 携带证. Above USD 10,000: SAFE-issued 携带证; in principle not permittedSAFE/Customs Order 汇发〔2003〕102号, Art. 5 and 7. Art. 5(1) 不超过等值5000美元的，不需申领《携带证》; (2) 5000-10000美元 应当向银行申领《携带证》; (3) 超过10000美元 应当向…外汇局申领《携带证》. Art. 7: 原则上不得携带超过等值10000美元外币现钞出境. Bank-issued permits are capped at USD 10,000 each (Art. 9).Cash is the lowest-capacity route by a wide margin. Moving a meaningful sum in cash would require dozens of trips and would itself be structuring.m.mofcom.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-13Overseas ATM cash withdrawal on a mainland-issued bank cardCNY 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year; CNY 10,000 equivalent per card per daySAFE 汇发〔2017〕29号, Art. 1: 个人持境内银行卡在境外提取现金，本人名下银行卡（含附属卡）合计每个自然年度不得超过等值10万元人民币。超过年度额度的，本年及次年将被暂停持境内银行卡在境外提取现金。Art. 4: 外币卡由每卡每日不得超过等值1000美元调整为等值1万元人民币；人民币卡管理维持每卡每日不得超过等值1万元人民币。Roughly USD 14,000/yr at the BOC rate — a useful but small channel, and capped per person, which rules out scaling by opening more accounts.m.safe.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-14Overseas card spending (consumption, not conversion)Not capped by the USD 50k facilitation quota; overseas consumption is explicitly outside itSAFE's own Q&A on 汇发〔2017〕29号: 个人出境旅游、商务、留学所涉食、宿、行、购等经常项下交易，均可以使用银行卡支付，且不占用个人便利化年度5万美元购汇额度。Spending while abroad is a lawful way to consume the money, but it is not a way to obtain USD you keep. CN-FX-15Emigrant property transfer (移民转移) — structureMust be applied for once for the entire amount; paid out in stages: up to 50% first, up to half of the remainder after 1 year, the rest after 2 years. Amounts up to CNY 200,000 equivalent may be remitted in one go with approval.PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 16, Art. 6. Approval tier: Art. 10 — total up to CNY 500,000 equivalent is approved by the local SAFE branch; above that, local branch screens and SAFE headquarters approves. Art. 11 — after conversion the bank must remit directly to the applicant's own account in the destination country and may not hand out cash.This is the only route that scales to large sums without a purpose-matching current-account transaction, but it is available only to a person who has emigrated from mainland China and it takes two years to complete.gov.cnverified

CN-FX-25Definition of 境内个人 vs 境外个人 — unresolved tension between two official textsblankForeign Exchange Administration Regulations Art. 52(2) defines 境内个人 as 中国公民 and 外国人 in China who have resided continuously for one full year. The Implementing Rules Art. 37(1) instead define 境内个人 as Chinese citizens holding a resident ID card, military ID or armed-police ID. For a US citizen living in China these two definitions can point in different directions, and the size of the applicable facility varies with it: Guidelines Art. 59 grants the USD 50k purchase quota to a 境外个人 only if they hold a 外国人永久居留身份证 (foreigner permanent-residence ID).FLAGGED. Whether a given US citizen in China may use the USD 50,000 purchase quota by ID alone, or must document every purchase, depends on which definition the bank applies. This project did not find an official text reconciling them, so no conclusion is asserted.beijing.gov.cn gov.cnpartial
