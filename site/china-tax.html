China-side tax

China imposes no exit tax and no FX conversion tax on an individual moving their own lawful funds abroad. The Chinese tax that exists attaches to the income (interest, dividends, property gain), and a bank deposit's interest is currently exempt. The large penalties that people experience are US inbound-reporting penalties and PRC exchange-control fines, not PRC income tax.

CN-TAX-02Is there a PRC exit tax on an individual leaving China?No. There is a pre-departure tax settlement obligation, not an exit tax on unrealised gains.IIT Law Art. 10(5) requires a filing where 因移居境外注销中国户籍 (cancelling PRC household registration on emigrating). Art. 13 requires 纳税人因移居境外注销中国户籍的，应当在注销中国户籍前办理税款清算 (settle tax before cancelling household registration). Neither provision imposes a tax on unrealised appreciation; the settlement covers tax already due.This is the single most important China-side finding for the user's question about 'tax penalties'. There is no PRC departure tax to trigger by moving the money out, and no PRC tax on the act of converting CNY to USD. The cost of moving money out lawfully is a bank spread, not a tax.npc.gov.cnverified

Two rows worth reading twice

CN-TAX-02 — no exit tax. China requires a tax settlement before you cancel household registration to emigrate, but that is a settlement of tax already due. It is not a tax on leaving and not a tax on unrealised gains. Nothing is triggered by moving the money.

CN-TAX-05 — the dividend exemption ended. From 1 September 2026, a foreign national receiving dividends from a Chinese foreign-invested enterprise pays 20%, withheld by the company. The old exemption under 财税字〔1994〕20号 is repealed. Older commentary will get this wrong.
