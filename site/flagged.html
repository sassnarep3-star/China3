I-5 Two official texts define 境内个人 differently — the FX Regulations (State Council Order 532) Art. 52(2) and the SAFE Implementing Rules Art. 37(1). They can point in opposite directions for a US citizen living in China, and no reconciling text was found. Flagged as F-1 / CN-FX-25. Recorded as a genuine conflict between official sources, not resolved by assumption.
I-6 The PRC estate-tax finding rests on a 2017 Ministry of Finance reply as reported by People's Daily. The reply letter itself was not read on mof.gov.cn. Flagged. The quoted sentence is verbatim from the report, and the date and provenance are stated on the row.

3. How blanks were handled

Every table in this site shows — with the word blank where a value could not be confirmed from an official publisher. No cell was filled by interpolation, by analogy to another country, or from memory. The build script fails if any row renders without at least one official source link, so a row with no evidence cannot appear silently.

Evidence rule applied throughout: every figure carries a link to the official publisher. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source it is left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. No value was inferred or estimated.
