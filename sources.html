Official references

Government sources for the statements in this dashboard. Each reference identifies the issuing agency and what it supports; the link is available for manual review.

Translation and currency-noteSAFE’s English-language pages state that English is for reference and the original Chinese prevails in case of a different interpretation. The English source is cited for accessibility; a high-stakes filing should be checked with the bank/SAFE and Chinese-language text. SAFE translation disclaimer
S1

SAFE — Detailed Rules on Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange: official press Q&A

2007-01-05. Annual USD 50,000 facilitation quota for individual purchases/sales; more rigorous authenticity review above quota; documentation examples for donations, family maintenance, inheritances; distinction between savings accounts and trade-settlement accounts; banks may set different banknote/exchange rates and charges; individual capital-account controls.

S2

SAFE — 2015 Q4 press conference transcript

2015-12-25. Says there is no ceiling on amounts above USD 50,000 when authentic supporting materials are presented; confirms annual individual quota and bank verification. Historical official explanation; confirm current bank implementation.

Open official source ↗
S3

SAFE — Circular concerning further improving administration of individual FX settlement and sale

2009-12-23. Explicit prohibition on evading the annual aggregate amount by splitting funds; banks must reject identified split transactions and verify authenticity. Historical rule text; confirm its current interaction with later rules.

Open official source ↗
S4

SAFE — Published foreign-exchange violation cases

2019-05-20. Enforcement examples include use of 34 other individuals’ annual quotas and an RMB 830,000 fine; examples are not a penalty estimate for this case.

Open official source ↗
S5

SAFE — Further Improving and Adjusting Foreign Exchange Administration Policies under the Capital Account

2014-08-01, Huifa No. 2 [2014]. Personal-property transfer section addresses immigration and inheritance transfers; requires SAFE approval; bank may remit subject to approval documentation. Does not authorize general transfers of personal savings under this special channel.

Open official source ↗
S6

State Taxation Administration — Individual Income Tax Law

Law last amended 2018-08-31, English official text. Defines PRC resident individual using domicile or 183 days; lists taxable income categories and statutory rates; residents’ foreign income and foreign tax credit provisions are relevant to tax residency/credit analysis.

Open official source ↗
S7

IRS — Reporting foreign income and filing a tax return when living abroad

IRS guidance. States U.S. citizens’ worldwide income and filing obligations; discusses Schedule B, Form 8938, and links to FBAR requirements. Its tax-year-specific example is old; consult current forms/instructions for current filing dates.

Open official source ↗
S8

IRS — Publication 54, Tax Guide for U.S. Citizens and Resident Aliens Abroad

IRS publication. Worldwide income, foreign tax credit or deduction options, Form 1116 and limitations. Use the current tax-year edition and individual facts.

Open official source ↗
S9

SAFE / State Taxation Administration — Filing for certain external payments for trade in services and other accounts

2013-07-24. Defines specified categories where domestic payers file taxes before single external payments above USD 50,000 equivalent. It concerns qualifying payment categories, not every own-account transfer; filing is not itself the tax amount.

Open official source ↗
S10

SAFE — Overseas cash withdrawals with bank cards: official Q&A

2017-12-30. Describes annual overseas cash withdrawal limit RMB 100,000 per person and daily RMB 10,000 per card; bank-card cash limits are distinct from annual individual FX purchase quota.

Open official source ↗
S11

PBOC and agencies — Notice on further optimizing cross-border RMB policies to support foreign trade and investment

2020 Notice No. 330. Includes a section supporting cross-border RMB settlement for personal current-account lawful and compliant income such as salary. This does not establish that an arbitrary personal savings transfer is eligible.

Open official source ↗
S12

PBOC — Cross-border RMB business report (2024/2025 publication)

Official PBOC report on cross-border RMB settlement, including personal current-account and payment-institution services. Aggregate system-wide facts do not prove a particular bank will process this user’s transfer.

Open official source ↗
S13

SAFE Tianjin — Inheritance-property-transfer FAQ

2017-03-16. Explains that inheritance transfer is for eligible foreign citizens and Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan residents, applications are filed with the SAFE branch where the decedent had household registration, and lawful property/source must be established.

Open official source ↗
S14

SAFE — Foreign-exchange administration of domestic individuals’ participation in overseas listed-company equity incentive plans

2012-05-15. A specific registration/agency channel for qualifying employee equity incentives, sale proceeds and dividends. Not a generic personal transfer mechanism.

Open official source ↗
S15

FinCEN — Report Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts

FBAR filing criteria: U.S. person with financial interest or signature authority over foreign accounts; aggregate value greater than USD 10,000 at any time during calendar year.

Open official source ↗
S16

IRS — Do I need to file Form 8938?

IRS reporting thresholds vary by filing status and U.S./foreign residence; Form 8938 and FBAR are separate filings. Review current instructions.

Open official source ↗
S17

IRS — Gifts from a foreign person

Form 3520 information reporting for certain gifts/bequests; generally the amount received from a nonresident alien or foreign estate must exceed USD 100,000 (including related-party aggregation rules). Separate rules for foreign entities.

Open official source ↗
S18

SAFE — Measures for the Administration of Foreign Exchange Business of Payment Institutions

Official translated regulatory text. Requires payment institution contracts/disclosures to address exchange-rate quotation, service charges and other terms for covered payment-institution foreign exchange services. It does not set this customer’s specific fee.

Open official source ↗
S19

SAFE — FAQs on Foreign Exchange Management Policies

2010-07-02. Describes commercial banks buying/selling foreign exchange and bank quotations in general. Does not provide a universal customer conversion rate or current personal fee schedule.
