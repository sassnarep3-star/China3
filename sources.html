Official sources & verification method

Every substantive route, tax, or reporting row elsewhere links to at least one primary official source. A source link supports only the specific proposition described—not an assumption about a particular account.

Source hierarchy: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), People’s Bank of China (PBOC), IRS / U.S. Treasury and FinCEN primary material. Chinese-only sources are labeled. Some regulatory translations are official government pages that may be described as unofficial translations; always check the Chinese text and current local implementation for a real application.

How findings were written

  • Primary government pages only for material legal, tax and reporting assertions.
  • Dollar amounts are included only where a linked official source supports a threshold.
  • No bank fee, FX rate, tax amount or individual eligibility was inferred; absent values are explicitly marked unverified.
  • Each comparison-table row contains its own source links, not only a bibliography.
  • Route eligibility is qualified rather than promised because bank review depends on genuine purpose and evidence.

Repository review & irregularities

  • The initial repository contained only a minimal README and no application, source data, tests, or deployment configuration.
  • “Considerable taxes” was an unsupported user-reported description; no supporting notice, tax receipt or bank quotation was present.
  • SAFE materials are dispersed across national and provincial sites; localized Q&As may become stale and require current branch confirmation.
  • Some primary Chinese pages and an official PBOC English rendering are used; translation and current implementation should be checked against the Chinese text.
