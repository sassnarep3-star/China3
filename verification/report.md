## Claims that need action

- **VC-01 (contradicted)** USD 50,000: SAFE's own Q&A says the annual amount is not a limit on yearly FX purchases or sales; it only decides whether ID alone is enough. Art. 54 applies the purchase side to domestic individuals (境内个人). For a non-domestic individual, Art. 59 is a documents-based route for lawful China-earned RMB income, with no annual amount stated unless the holder has a Foreigner's Permanent Residence ID Card. The 2007 press conference predates the 2020 Guidelines. Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.safe.gov.cn/ningxia/2022/0929/1997.html
- **VC-03 (partly)** Classification: The PBOC Measures define a non-domestic individual as a foreign citizen holding a non-Chinese passport (Art. 37(2)); the Regulations (Art. 52(2)) count foreigners resident for at least one continuous year as domestic individuals. Guidelines Art. 59 treats foreign nationals as 境外个人, with a permanent-residence carve-out. The conflict is not resolved in the texts read; the bank's registration decides. Session 7e08715a flags the same conflict (I03). Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130733/2025080815071447034/index.html, https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html
- **VC-04 (partly)** Salary route: Confirmed in substance by primary text: Art. 59, quoted again in SAFE Tianjin's 2026 FAQ, lets a non-domestic individual convert lawful China-earned current-account RMB income on valid ID plus source-of-funds materials showing the amount, including tax certificates. No annual amount is stated; holders of a Permanent Residence ID Card are subject to the convenience amount. 'Employment contract', 'work permit', 'entire salary' and '100%' are not words of Art. 59 (the document list is bank practice). Banks may refuse but must give reasons and an appeal channel (Art. 68). Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-04/02/content_5597577.htm, https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html
- **VC-05 (partly)** Savings route: That is the domestic-individual regime (Arts. 54-56). For a non-domestic individual, Art. 59 covers only lawful China-earned current-account RMB income. No primary text read gives a standard route for a non-domestic individual's savings that cannot be traced to such income (blank - VB-07). If the bank treats the holder as a domestic individual the convenience-amount route applies. Art. 187 lets SAFE branches handle genuine needs outside the rules by collective deliberation. Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm
- **VC-06 (contradicted)** Carrying cash out: Art. 89: the USD 5,000-10,000 tier is a bank-issued Carry-out Permit (where there is no entry record, or the amount exceeds it) - the middle tier is right, though the cited 2007 page does not say it. Above USD 10,000 Art. 89 provides a SAFE-branch permit only for travel to war-torn or strictly controlled countries; it gives no 'customs declaration' route for other destinations. The written customs declaration is the inbound rule above USD 5,000. 'No declaration up to USD 5,000' is implied, not stated. Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html
- **VC-07 (superseded)** Foreign cash rules: Superseded by Arts. 86-89 (2020): deposits and withdrawals up to USD 10,000 a day need ID only (the 2007 USD 5,000 deposit trigger no longer applies); above that, a customs-stamped declaration or the original withdrawal slip (deposit), or a prior SAFE filing for specified destinations (withdrawal). The 2020 Notice states that earlier inconsistent provisions yield to it. Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html
- **VC-09 (superseded)** Anti-splitting circular: The circular's English title matches item 7 of the repeal list in the 2020 Notice (汇发〔2009〕56号) - matched by title and date; the English page does not print the document number. Its 5-or-more-individual patterns are therefore no longer published rules. The prohibition itself continues in Guidelines Arts. 47 and 62. Evidence: https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm, https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2009/1223/698.html
